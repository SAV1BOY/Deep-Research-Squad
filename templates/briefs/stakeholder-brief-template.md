# Stakeholder Brief

> **Purpose:** Communicate research findings to non-technical stakeholders in plain language.
> Strips away jargon and focuses on business impact, decisions, and next steps.
> Use `{{placeholder}}` markers to fill in project-specific details.

---

## When to Use

- Presenting findings to business leaders, partners, or clients without technical backgrounds
- Summarizing research for cross-functional meetings
- Providing updates to stakeholders who need implications, not methodology
