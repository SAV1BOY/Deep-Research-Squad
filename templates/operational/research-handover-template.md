# Research Handover Document

> **Purpose:** Enable seamless handover of an ongoing research project to a new researcher
> or team. Captures institutional knowledge, current state, and operational details
> needed to continue without interruption. Use `{{placeholder}}` markers to fill in details.

---

## When to Use

- A researcher is leaving the project and a replacement is taking over
- Long-running research is transitioning between phases with new personnel
- A project is being paused and must be resumable later with full context
