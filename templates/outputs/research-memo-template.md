# Research Memo

> **Purpose:** Quick-turnaround memo format for capturing research findings that do not
> warrant a full report. Ideal for time-sensitive insights or narrow questions.
> Use `{{placeholder}}` markers to fill in project-specific details.

---

## When to Use

- Answering a focused research question in under a day
- Providing a rapid-response finding to a stakeholder
- Documenting a preliminary finding before a full analysis is complete
