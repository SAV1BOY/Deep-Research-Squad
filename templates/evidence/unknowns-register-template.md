## Category Definitions

### Known Unknown
Something we know we do not know. We can name the gap and potentially investigate it.
- Example: "We do not know the competitor's actual R&D budget."

### Assumption
Something we are treating as true without direct evidence. Must be validated or flagged.
- Example: "We assume the regulatory environment will remain stable."

### Blind Spot
An area we may not be examining due to framing, bias, or scope limitations.
- Example: "We have not considered the impact on adjacent markets."

> Instruction: Actively seek blind spots. Ask: "What are we not looking at?"

## Impact Assessment Matrix

| Impact Level | Description | Action Required |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **High** | Unknown could change core findings or recommendations | Must attempt resolution or prominently flag |
| **Medium** | Unknown affects supporting details or secondary findings | Document and note in report caveats |
| **Low** | Unknown has minimal effect on analysis quality | Document for completeness |
