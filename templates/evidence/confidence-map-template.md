## Confidence Level Definitions

| Level | Criteria | Typical Evidence Profile |
|-------|----------|------------------------|
| **High** | Multiple strong, independent sources agree. No significant contradictions. Methodology is sound. | 3+ strong sources, 0-1 contradictions resolved |
| **Medium** | Some corroborating evidence exists but with caveats. Minor contradictions or gaps. | 1-2 strong sources OR 3+ moderate sources, some gaps |
| **Low** | Limited evidence. Significant contradictions unresolved. Based partly on inference or extrapolation. | 0-1 moderate sources, major gaps or contradictions |

> Instruction: Apply these definitions consistently. When in doubt, assign the lower
> confidence level. It is better to undersell certainty than oversell it.
