## Column Definitions

### Severity
- **High:** Contradiction affects a core finding or recommendation
- **Medium:** Contradiction affects supporting detail but not core conclusions
- **Low:** Minor discrepancy with limited impact on overall analysis

### Resolution Methods
- **Source Quality:** One source is clearly more authoritative or methodologically sound
- **Recency:** More recent data supersedes older data
- **Scope Difference:** Sources address different contexts or populations
- **Methodology Difference:** Different methods yield different results; both may be valid
- **Unresolved:** Cannot determine which position is correct; flagged as uncertainty
- **Synthesis:** Both positions are partially correct; a combined view is more accurate

> Instruction: Document the reasoning behind each resolution. If you cannot resolve
> a contradiction, mark it as "Unresolved" and note it in the final report.
