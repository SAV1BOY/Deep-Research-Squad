# Think Tank Sources

## Purpose

This registry catalogs major think tanks that produce policy-relevant
research. Think tanks bridge academic rigor and policy relevance, providing
analysis that is more accessible than journals but more rigorous than media.

## Tier 1: Global Policy Research

### Brookings Institution
- **Focus**: US domestic policy, global economics, governance, technology
- **Access**: brookings.edu (free)
- **Strengths**: Non-partisan reputation, deep policy expertise, data-driven
- **Key outputs**: Policy briefs, research reports, congressional testimony
- **Bias note**: Center-left lean on some domestic issues

### RAND Corporation
- **Focus**: National security, defense, health, education, technology
- **Access**: rand.org (most free, some gated)
- **Strengths**: Rigorous methodology, invented scenario planning, long tradition
- **Key outputs**: Research reports, tools, databases
- **Bias note**: Defense establishment perspective, US-centric

### Chatham House (Royal Institute of International Affairs)
- **Focus**: International affairs, security, economics, environment
- **Access**: chathamhouse.org (free + membership)
- **Strengths**: Global perspective, strong UK/Europe focus, network access
- **Key outputs**: Research papers, policy briefs, expert commentary

### Council on Foreign Relations (CFR)
- **Focus**: US foreign policy, international relations
- **Access**: cfr.org (free + membership)
- **Strengths**: Authoritative on US foreign policy, excellent backgrounders

## Tier 2: Specialized Think Tanks

### Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS)
- **Focus**: Defense, security, technology policy, geopolitics
- **Strengths**: Strong technology and cybersecurity analysis

### Peterson Institute for International Economics (PIIE)
- **Focus**: International economics, trade, finance
- **Strengths**: Quantitative economic analysis, trade policy expertise

### World Economic Forum (WEF)
- **Focus**: Global risks, technology governance, stakeholder capitalism
- **Strengths**: Global Risk Report, Future of Jobs surveys
- **Limitations**: Corporate membership may influence agenda

### Carnegie Endowment for International Peace
- **Focus**: International affairs, technology and democracy
- **Strengths**: Strong on AI governance, democracy and technology

## Tier 3: Regional and Specialized

- **Bruegel** (EU economics): bruegel.org
- **Lowy Institute** (Asia-Pacific): lowyinstitute.org
- **Observer Research Foundation** (India/South Asia): orfonline.org
- **Carnegie-Tsinghua** (China policy): carnegietsinghua.org

## Usage Guidelines

1. Always note the think tank's funding sources and potential biases
2. Think tank research is not peer-reviewed — treat as informed opinion
3. Cross-reference across think tanks with different perspectives
4. Policy briefs are entry points; read full reports for methodology
5. Track individual researchers, not just institutions
