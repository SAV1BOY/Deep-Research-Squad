# Regulatory Database Sources

## Purpose

This registry catalogs regulatory databases that track rules, enforcement
actions, and compliance requirements. Regulatory data is high-reliability
because it is official, dated, and legally binding.

## Major Regulatory Databases

### United States

**Federal Register / Code of Federal Regulations**
- **URL**: federalregister.gov, ecfr.gov
- **Coverage**: All US federal regulations
- **Use**: Track proposed and final rules, comment periods

**FDA (Food and Drug Administration)**
- **URL**: fda.gov, accessdata.fda.gov
- **Coverage**: Drug approvals, medical devices, food safety
- **Key databases**: Drugs@FDA, MAUDE (device adverse events), 510(k)

**FTC (Federal Trade Commission)**
- **URL**: ftc.gov
- **Coverage**: Consumer protection, antitrust enforcement
- **Key data**: Enforcement actions, consent decrees, merger reviews

**FCC (Federal Communications Commission)**
- **URL**: fcc.gov
- **Coverage**: Telecommunications, spectrum, broadband
- **Key data**: Licensing, enforcement, broadband deployment data

**USPTO (Patent and Trademark Office)**
- **URL**: patents.google.com, uspto.gov
- **Coverage**: Patent and trademark filings, grants, litigation

### European Union

**EUR-Lex**
- **URL**: eur-lex.europa.eu
- **Coverage**: All EU legislation, case law, treaties
- **Key**: GDPR, AI Act, Digital Services Act, Digital Markets Act

**EMA (European Medicines Agency)**
- **URL**: ema.europa.eu
- **Coverage**: Drug and medical device regulation in EU

### International

**WTO (World Trade Organization)**
- **URL**: wto.org
- **Coverage**: Trade agreements, disputes, tariff data

**WIPO (World Intellectual Property Organization)**
- **URL**: wipo.int
- **Coverage**: International patent and trademark data

**Basel Committee / BIS**
- **URL**: bis.org
- **Coverage**: Banking regulation, financial stability

## How to Use Regulatory Data for Research

### For Market Analysis
1. New regulations create markets (compliance tools, consulting)
2. Enforcement trends signal regulatory priorities
3. Proposed rules indicate future market conditions

### For Competitive Intelligence
1. Patent filings reveal R&D direction 18-24 months ahead
2. FDA submissions show pharma pipeline status
3. Antitrust filings reveal merger strategies

### For Risk Assessment
1. Enforcement actions show what regulators are targeting
2. Consent decrees set precedents for industry behavior
3. Comment periods reveal stakeholder positions

## Data Quality Notes

1. Regulatory data is authoritative but requires legal interpretation
2. There is often a lag between regulatory action and database updates
3. Cross-reference regulatory data with industry analysis for context
4. Proposed rules may change significantly before final publication
5. Different jurisdictions may have conflicting regulatory frameworks
