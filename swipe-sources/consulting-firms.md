# Consulting Firm Sources

## Purpose

This registry catalogs high-quality research outputs from top consulting
firms. These sources provide market sizing, industry analysis, and
strategic frameworks backed by proprietary data and client engagements.

## Tier 1: Strategy Consulting

### McKinsey & Company
- **McKinsey Global Institute (MGI)**: Long-form research reports on economics, labor, technology
- **McKinsey Quarterly**: Strategic insights articles, peer-reviewed internally
- **Industry practices**: Sector-specific reports (healthcare, financial services, tech)
- **Access**: mgi.com, mckinsey.com/quarterly (most free, some gated)
- **Strengths**: Proprietary survey data, large sample sizes, global scope
- **Limitations**: May reflect client interests, sometimes overly optimistic on tech adoption

### Boston Consulting Group (BCG)
- **BCG Henderson Institute**: Thought leadership and research
- **BCG Global**: Industry and functional reports
- **Access**: bcg.com (most free)
- **Strengths**: Strong quantitative frameworks, good visualization
- **Limitations**: Shorter time horizons than McKinsey, fewer longitudinal studies

### Bain & Company
- **Bain Insights**: Research briefs and reports
- **Bain Private Equity Report**: Annual PE market analysis
- **Access**: bain.com (free)
- **Strengths**: Strong in private equity, retail, and M&A analysis
- **Limitations**: Narrower industry coverage than McKinsey/BCG

## Tier 2: Professional Services

### Deloitte
- **Deloitte Insights**: Research across industries and functions
- **Tech Trends**: Annual technology trend report
- **Access**: deloitte.com/insights (free)
- **Strengths**: Broad coverage, good regulatory and compliance analysis
- **Limitations**: Can be promotional, less rigorous methodology disclosure

### Accenture
- **Accenture Research**: Technology and business strategy
- **Technology Vision**: Annual tech prediction report
- **Strengths**: Deep technology implementation perspective
- **Limitations**: Strong technology bias, less independent

### PwC / EY / KPMG
- **PwC Strategy&**: Strategy-focused research
- **EY Megatrends**: Long-term trend analysis
- **KPMG Insights**: Industry-specific research
- **Strengths**: Strong regulatory and financial analysis
- **Limitations**: Audit/advisory conflicts can influence positioning

## Usage Guidelines for Research Squad

1. Cross-reference consulting reports with academic and independent sources
2. Note potential conflicts of interest (firm may sell services in the area)
3. Consulting data is often survey-based — note sample sizes and methodology
4. Use consulting frameworks (e.g., BCG matrix) as tools, not truths
5. Prefer MGI and BCG Henderson for research depth; use others for breadth
