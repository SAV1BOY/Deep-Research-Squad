# Public Filing Sources

## Purpose

This registry catalogs public filing databases where companies are required
to disclose financial, operational, and legal information. These are among
the most reliable sources because disclosure is legally mandated.

## Primary Filing Databases

### SEC EDGAR (United States)
- **URL**: sec.gov/edgar
- **Coverage**: All US public companies, many foreign filers
- **Key filings**:
  - **10-K**: Annual report with full financial statements and MD&A
  - **10-Q**: Quarterly financial report
  - **8-K**: Material event disclosure (acquisitions, leadership changes)
  - **S-1**: IPO registration statement (most detailed public disclosure ever)
  - **DEF 14A**: Proxy statement (executive comp, governance)
  - **13F**: Institutional investor holdings (quarterly)
  - **SC 13D/G**: Significant ownership stakes (5%+)
- **Strengths**: Full-text search, free access, machine-readable (XBRL)

### Companies House (United Kingdom)
- **URL**: companieshouse.gov.uk
- **Coverage**: All UK registered companies
- **Key filings**: Annual accounts, confirmation statements, director filings
- **Strengths**: Free access, extensive coverage including private companies

### CVM (Brazil - Comissao de Valores Mobiliarios)
- **URL**: cvm.gov.br
- **Coverage**: Brazilian public companies
- **Key filings**: DFP (annual), ITR (quarterly), formulario de referencia

### Other Major Registries
- **SEDAR+** (Canada): sedarplus.ca
- **CNMV** (Spain): cnmv.es
- **BaFin** (Germany): bafin.de
- **ASIC** (Australia): asic.gov.au
- **HKEX** (Hong Kong): hkexnews.hk

## How to Use Public Filings for Research

### For Company Analysis
1. Start with most recent 10-K for comprehensive overview
2. Read MD&A section for management's strategic perspective
3. Check risk factors for honest disclosure of vulnerabilities
4. Compare financial statements across 3-5 years for trends

### For Competitive Intelligence
1. Search competitor 10-Ks for market sizing data
2. Check patent filings for R&D direction
3. Monitor 8-K filings for real-time material events
4. Review proxy statements for executive incentive alignment

### For Market Research
1. Aggregate revenue data from multiple company filings
2. Use S-1 filings for the most detailed market data (companies reveal more at IPO)
3. Track 13F filings to see where institutional money is flowing

## Filing Quality Notes

1. Filings are audited (10-K) or reviewed (10-Q) by independent accountants
2. Management can still spin narrative in MD&A — cross-reference with data
3. Risk factors tend to be comprehensive (legal protection) but boilerplate
4. S-1 filings are uniquely valuable: most disclosure with least track record
5. International filing standards vary — US GAAP vs IFRS differences matter
