# Academic Journal Sources

## Purpose

This registry catalogs top-tier academic journals across disciplines relevant
to deep research. Peer-reviewed publications represent the highest standard
of evidence but require critical evaluation of methodology and relevance.

## Tier 1: Multidisciplinary

### Nature
- **Publisher**: Springer Nature
- **Impact**: Highest prestige in natural sciences
- **Access**: nature.com (abstract free, full text paywalled)
- **Strengths**: Rigorous peer review, high impact, broad scope
- **Sub-journals**: Nature Machine Intelligence, Nature Medicine, Nature Energy

### Science
- **Publisher**: AAAS
- **Impact**: Peer to Nature in prestige
- **Access**: science.org (similar access model)
- **Strengths**: Strong on breakthrough research, excellent review articles

### The Lancet
- **Publisher**: Elsevier
- **Impact**: Top medical journal globally
- **Access**: thelancet.com (some open access)
- **Strengths**: Clinical research, global health, health policy

### PNAS (Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences)
- **Access**: pnas.org (open access after 6 months)
- **Strengths**: Broad scope, faster publication than Nature/Science

## Tier 2: Domain-Specific Leaders

### Technology and Computing
- **ACM Computing Surveys**: Comprehensive review articles
- **IEEE Transactions**: Engineering and computing
- **JMLR (Journal of Machine Learning Research)**: Open access ML research
- **NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR proceedings**: Top ML conference papers

### Business and Economics
- **Harvard Business Review**: Practitioner-focused business research
- **Journal of Finance**: Top finance academic journal
- **Quarterly Journal of Economics**: Top economics journal
- **Management Science**: Operations and management research

### Social Science
- **American Political Science Review**: Political science
- **American Sociological Review**: Sociology
- **Psychological Science**: Psychology

## Pre-print Servers (Not Peer-Reviewed)

- **arXiv**: Physics, math, CS, quantitative biology (arxiv.org)
- **SSRN**: Social sciences, economics, law (ssrn.com)
- **medRxiv/bioRxiv**: Medical and biological sciences
- **Note**: Pre-prints are NOT peer-reviewed. Use with explicit caveat.

## Usage Guidelines for Research Squad

1. Peer-reviewed > pre-print > working paper > blog post
2. Check retraction databases (Retraction Watch) for any cited paper
3. Note sample sizes, methodology, and funding sources
4. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses outrank individual studies
5. Conference papers (NeurIPS, ICML) are peer-reviewed but less rigorously
6. Impact factor matters less than methodology quality
7. Check if findings have been replicated before citing as established
