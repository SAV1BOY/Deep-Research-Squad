# Investment Due Diligence — Swipe Examples

## Purpose

Investment due diligence packs provide comprehensive, structured analysis
of a potential investment target. They combine financial, strategic, and
operational analysis to support investment decisions.

## Gold Standard Structure

### 1. Investment Thesis (1 page)
- Why this company/asset at this price at this time
- Bull case in 3 bullets
- Bear case in 3 bullets
- Key assumptions that must hold

### 2. Company Overview
- Business model description (plain language)
- Revenue model and unit economics
- Customer concentration and retention metrics
- Competitive positioning

### 3. Market Assessment
- TAM/SAM/SOM with methodology
- Growth drivers and headwinds
- Competitive landscape summary
- Regulatory environment

### 4. Financial Analysis
- Revenue trajectory (3-5 year history, 3-5 year projection)
- Margin analysis and trend
- Cash flow and burn rate
- Key financial ratios benchmarked against peers

### 5. Risk Matrix

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|------|------------|--------|------------|-------|
| Customer concentration | High | High | Diversification plan | CEO |
| Key person dependency | Medium | High | Succession planning | Board |
| Regulatory change | Low | High | Compliance roadmap | Legal |
| Technology obsolescence | Medium | Medium | R&D investment | CTO |

### 6. Red Flags Checklist
- [ ] Unusual related-party transactions
- [ ] Revenue recognition changes
- [ ] Key employee departures
- [ ] Legal proceedings or regulatory actions
- [ ] Customer complaint patterns
- [ ] Glassdoor/employee sentiment trends

## Example Investment Thesis

> **Thesis**: Acme Health ($45M ARR, 120% NRR) is the leading patient
> engagement platform in the mid-market hospital segment. At 8x ARR,
> the valuation reflects growth but not the optionality of their data asset.
>
> **Bull case**: Expand to enterprise ($200M+ ARR by 2028), data
> monetization adds new revenue stream, 85% gross margins defensible.
>
> **Bear case**: Epic builds competing features, hospital IT budgets
> contract, regulatory changes to patient data sharing limit growth.

## Quality Standards

1. Every claim in the thesis must appear in the evidence base
2. Financial projections must state assumptions explicitly
3. Red flags must be investigated, not just listed
4. Competitor analysis must be independent, not from the target
5. At least 3 customer reference calls documented
