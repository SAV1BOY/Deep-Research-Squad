# Vendor Due Diligence — Swipe Examples

## Purpose

Vendor due diligence evaluates potential vendors, partners, or service
providers before commitment. It protects against vendor risk and ensures
the selection is evidence-based rather than relationship-driven.

## Gold Standard Structure

### 1. Vendor Profile
- Company overview: size, age, funding, ownership
- Relevant product/service description
- Customer base and reference quality
- Financial stability indicators

### 2. Capability Assessment

| Capability | Required | Vendor Rating | Evidence | Gap |
|-----------|----------|--------------|----------|-----|
| Core function A | Must-have | Strong | Demo + 3 refs | None |
| Integration B | Must-have | Moderate | Demo only | Testing needed |
| Scale capacity | Must-have | Unknown | Claimed only | Verify |
| Feature C | Nice-to-have | Weak | Roadmap item | 6-12 months |

### 3. Risk Assessment
- **Financial risk**: Could they go out of business? Funding runway?
- **Concentration risk**: How dependent would we be? Switching cost?
- **Security risk**: Data handling, compliance certifications
- **Operational risk**: SLA history, incident response track record
- **Strategic risk**: Are they likely to be acquired? Pivot? Sunset product?

### 4. Reference Check Summary
- Minimum 3 reference calls, at least 1 unsolicited
- Structured questions: what works, what doesn't, would you choose again?
- Red flag detection: patterns across references

### 5. Commercial Analysis
- Pricing model and total cost of ownership (3-year view)
- Contract terms assessment (auto-renewal, termination, IP ownership)
- Negotiation leverage points

## Example Vendor DD Summary

> **Vendor**: CloudSec Inc (Series B, $50M raised, 200 employees)
> **Product**: Cloud security posture management (CSPM)
>
> **Recommendation**: PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS
> - Strong core CSPM capability (verified via demo + 4 references)
> - Financial stability adequate for 2-year engagement
> - GAP: Multi-cloud support limited to AWS+Azure; GCP on roadmap Q3
> - CONDITION: Include contractual SLA for GCP support delivery
> - CONDITION: Escrow agreement for source code
> - RISK: 60% revenue from top 5 customers (concentration risk)

## Vendor DD Red Flags

1. Reluctance to provide references outside their curated list
2. Financial information inconsistencies or refusal to share
3. High employee turnover in engineering (check LinkedIn)
4. Discrepancy between marketing claims and reference feedback
5. Aggressive contract terms (long lock-in, limited termination)
6. Recent leadership changes without clear explanation

## Decision Framework

- **Proceed**: All must-haves met, risks manageable, references strong
- **Proceed with conditions**: Gaps identified but addressable contractually
- **Defer**: Key information missing, need more diligence
- **Reject**: Must-have gaps, unacceptable risks, or red flags confirmed
