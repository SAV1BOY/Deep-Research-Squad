# Industry Convergence Timeline — Swipe Example

## Why This Is Gold Standard

Industry convergence timelines map how previously separate industries merge through
technology, regulation, or business model shifts. Unlike linear timelines, convergence
timelines must show multiple streams colliding. The best examples identify the
convergence triggers, map the collision points, and project the resulting new
industry structure.

## Structure Template

- **Convergence**: Automotive + Energy + Telecommunications
- **Thesis**: Vehicle electrification, V2G, and connected infrastructure are merging
  three industries into an integrated mobility-energy-data ecosystem
- **Timeframe**: 2020-2032
- **Confidence in trajectory**: 0.72 (directionally confident, timing uncertain)

### 4. Convergence Enablers and Blockers

**Enablers (accelerating convergence):**
- Bidirectional charging standards (ISO 15118-20) finalized
- Regulatory incentives for grid flexibility (FERC Order 2222)
- Software-defined vehicle architecture enables OTA integration
- 5G network slicing provides dedicated vehicle connectivity

**Blockers (slowing convergence):**
- Utility regulatory models resist distributed energy participation
- Automotive and telecom operate on incompatible investment cycles
- Consumer willingness to share vehicle battery for grid unknown at scale
- Data privacy frameworks for vehicle-generated data unresolved

### 5. New Industry Structure (Projected)

**Winners in converged landscape:**
- Companies that control the vehicle-grid-network interface
- Platform players that aggregate demand across all three domains
- Battery manufacturers who become energy infrastructure providers

**Losers or displaced:**
- Pure-play gas station networks (stranded assets)
- Traditional auto dealers (disintermediated by direct + digital)
- Utility companies that fail to adopt distributed models

**New roles that don't exist today:**
- Mobility energy aggregator
- Vehicle data exchange operator
- Cross-industry regulatory navigator

### 7. What Could Invalidate This Timeline
- Battery technology breakthrough that eliminates V2G value proposition
- Regulatory reversal on distributed energy participation
- Autonomous vehicles arriving faster than expected (changes convergence dynamics)
- Geopolitical disruption to battery mineral supply chains
