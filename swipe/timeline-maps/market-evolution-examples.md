# Market Evolution Timeline — Swipe Examples

## Purpose

Market evolution timelines track how markets form, grow, consolidate, and
transform over time. They reveal patterns useful for predicting future
market dynamics and identifying strategic windows.

## Gold Standard Structure

### 1. Market Definition
- What market is being tracked
- How boundaries have shifted over time
- Current market size and growth rate

### 2. Era-Based Timeline

**Era 1: Formation**
- First entrants and their value propositions
- Initial customer segments served
- Funding and revenue milestones

**Era 2: Growth and Fragmentation**
- Explosion of competitors
- Segment specialization begins
- Key product innovations

**Era 3: Consolidation**
- M&A activity accelerates
- Winners and losers emerge
- Platform dynamics take hold

**Era 4: Maturity or Transformation**
- Market becomes commoditized OR gets disrupted
- New adjacent markets form
- Next cycle begins

### 3. Key Events Log

| Date | Event | Impact | Significance |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| 2015-Q1 | Company X IPO | Market validated | Category creation moment |
| 2017-Q3 | Regulation Y passed | New compliance market | Expanded TAM 40% |
| 2020-Q1 | COVID-19 pandemic | Digital acceleration | Compressed 5yr adoption to 1yr |

## Example: Cloud Computing Market Evolution

> **Formation (2006-2010)**: AWS launches EC2 and S3. Market doesn't exist yet.
> Skeptics call it "someone else's computer." Early adopters are startups
> avoiding CapEx. Market size: <$5B.
>
> **Growth (2011-2016)**: Azure and GCP enter. Enterprise adoption begins.
> "Cloud-first" policies emerge. Market reaches $100B. Hundreds of
> cloud-native startups form the ecosystem.
>
> **Consolidation (2017-2022)**: Top 3 capture 65% share. Multi-cloud
> becomes enterprise standard. Serverless and containers shift the stack.
>
> **Transformation (2023-present)**: AI workloads reshape cloud economics.
> GPU scarcity creates new power dynamics. Sovereign cloud requirements
> fragment the global market. Edge computing blurs boundaries.

## Evolution Pattern Recognition

- **S-curve timing**: How long is each phase for this market type?
- **Consolidation triggers**: What causes market consolidation?
- **Disruption windows**: When are incumbents most vulnerable?
- **Adjacent market formation**: What new markets spin off?

## Quality Standards

1. Mark exact dates, not vague ranges, for key events
2. Include failed competitors and dead-end paths
3. Note external shocks and their market impact
4. Track market size at each phase transition
5. Identify the pattern archetype (winner-take-all, oligopoly, fragmented)
