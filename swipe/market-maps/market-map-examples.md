# Market Map — Swipe Examples

## Purpose

Market maps visually and structurally organize all players in a market by
category, positioning, and relationships. They are reference tools that
enable strategic conversations about positioning, gaps, and opportunities.

## Gold Standard Structure

### 1. Map Header
- Market defined with clear boundaries
- Time snapshot (as of date)
- Total market size and growth rate

### 2. Category Grid

**Layer 1: Infrastructure**
| Company | Funding/Revenue | Position | Trend |
|---------|----------------|----------|-------|
| Co A    | $500M rev      | Leader   | →     |
| Co B    | Series C $80M  | Rising   | ↑     |

**Layer 2: Platform**
| Company | Funding/Revenue | Position | Trend |
|---------|----------------|----------|-------|
| Co C    | $1.2B rev      | Leader   | →     |
| Co D    | Series B $40M  | Niche    | ↑     |

**Layer 3: Application**
(Same structure per layer)

### 3. Relationship Annotations
- Partnership lines between companies
- Acquisition targets flagged
- Competitive overlaps highlighted

### 4. White Space Analysis
- Gaps in the map where no strong player exists
- Adjacent markets that could expand into gaps
- Timing estimate for gap closure

## Example: AI Development Tools Market Map (2025)

> **Market Size**: $12.4B (2024) → $28.1B (2027E), 31% CAGR
>
> **Infrastructure Layer**: NVIDIA (dominant GPU), AMD (gaining share),
> Intel (restructuring), custom silicon (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium)
>
> **Model Layer**: OpenAI (leader), Anthropic (challenger), Google DeepMind,
> Meta AI, Mistral (European challenger), open-source ecosystem
>
> **Application Layer**: Fragmented. 200+ startups. Key segments:
> code generation, content creation, data analysis, customer service
>
> **White Space**: Enterprise model management, AI security/governance,
> domain-specific fine-tuning platforms

## What Makes a Market Map Excellent

1. **Clear boundaries**: Defines what's in and what's out
2. **Consistent categorization**: MECE layers that don't overlap
3. **Dynamic indicators**: Shows movement, not just current state
4. **White space identification**: The gaps matter more than the players
5. **Data-backed positioning**: Revenue/funding, not subjective opinion

## Maintenance Notes

Market maps decay fast. Include refresh cadence recommendation.
Quarterly minimum for fast-moving markets, semi-annual for stable ones.
