# Emerging Technology Market Map — Swipe Example

## Why This Is Gold Standard

Emerging tech market maps must balance comprehensiveness with clarity. Unlike mature
market maps, they account for high uncertainty, rapid entrant flux, and blurred category
boundaries. The best examples distinguish between proven traction and speculative
positioning, and flag where the map will likely be wrong in 12 months.

### 4. Market Dynamics

**Funding trends:**
- Total sector funding: $4.2B in 2025 (up 28% from 2024)
- Median Series A: $18M (up from $12M in 2023)
- Late-stage concentration: Top 10 companies hold 62% of total funding

**Entry/exit patterns:**
- 34 new entrants in trailing 12 months
- 8 acquisitions (5 by incumbents, 3 by larger startups)
- 6 companies quietly shut down or pivoted

**Category boundary shifts:**
- Design tools and data platforms converging — expect merger within 12 months
- Bio-foundries expanding into application layer (vertical integration trend)
- "Infrastructure" increasingly commoditized — margin compression visible

### 5. Gaps and White Spaces
1. **Regulatory navigation tools** — No dedicated player; opportunity for specialized compliance platform
2. **Scale-up bridging** — Gap between lab-scale proof and industrial production remains underserved
3. **B2B marketplace** — No dominant platform for bio-based material sourcing

### 6. Map Reliability Assessment

| Layer | Confidence | Reason |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Infrastructure | 0.82 | Established players, clear category boundaries |
| Platform | 0.65 | Some convergence happening, categories may shift |

**Where this map will be wrong:**
- 20-30% of seed-stage companies will pivot or die within 12 months
- At least one new category will emerge that does not exist on this map today
- 2-3 companies currently in one layer will vertically integrate across layers
