# Competitive Intelligence — Swipe Examples

## Purpose

Competitive intelligence (CI) tracks multiple competitors simultaneously,
identifies patterns across the competitive landscape, and provides ongoing
strategic awareness rather than point-in-time analysis.

## Gold Standard Structure

### 1. Competitive Dashboard

| Competitor | Recent Move        | Signal Strength | Impact | Response Needed |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|--------|-----------------|
| Alpha Co  | Acquired DataCorp  | Strong         | High   | Yes - 30 days   |
| Beta Inc  | Launched API v3    | Medium         | Medium | Monitor         |
| Gamma Ltd | CFO departure      | Weak           | Low    | Note            |

### 2. Signal Categories
- **Product signals**: Launches, updates, deprecations, patents filed
- **People signals**: Key hires, departures, reorgs, job postings
- **Financial signals**: Funding, revenue reports, pricing changes
- **Strategic signals**: Partnerships, acquisitions, market entries/exits
- **Customer signals**: Win/loss data, review trends, churn patterns

### 3. Pattern Analysis
- What are competitors converging on? (consensus plays)
- What is no one doing? (potential opportunities)
- Who is diverging from the pack? (contrarian bets)
- What sequence of moves suggests a larger strategy?

### 4. Early Warning System
- Defined triggers that require immediate briefing
- Monitoring cadence per competitor (daily/weekly/monthly)
- Source freshness requirements

## Example CI Pattern Analysis

> **Convergence signal**: Three of five competitors launched AI-powered
> features in Q4 2025. All targeted the same use case (automated reporting).
> This suggests a market expectation forming. Risk of commoditization within
> 12 months.
>
> **Divergence signal**: CompetitorX is hiring heavily in healthcare vertical
> while others remain horizontal. If they succeed, they create a defensible
> niche. Monitor their healthcare customer logos in next 2 quarters.
>
> **Absence signal**: No competitor has addressed the SMB segment. Market
> research suggests 40% of demand is unserved. Opportunity or trap?

## Source Hierarchy for CI

1. **Tier 1**: Public filings, earnings calls, patent databases
2. **Tier 2**: Job postings, press releases, product changelogs
3. **Tier 3**: Industry analysts, trade publications
4. **Tier 4**: Social media, employee posts, conference talks
5. **Tier 5**: Rumors, anonymous reports (flag clearly)

## CI Ethics Boundary

- All sources must be legally obtainable
- No misrepresentation to obtain information
- No use of stolen or leaked proprietary data
- Employee social media is fair game; private communications are not
