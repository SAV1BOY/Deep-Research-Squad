# Regulatory Compliance Evidence Table — Swipe Example

## Why This Is Gold Standard

Regulatory evidence tables demand extreme precision. Unlike market research, regulatory
claims carry legal weight — ambiguity is dangerous. The best examples cite specific
statute sections, distinguish between enacted law and proposed rules, track enforcement
patterns, and flag jurisdictional variations. Every cell must be verifiable.
