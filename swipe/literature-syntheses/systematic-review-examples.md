# Systematic Review — Swipe Examples

## Purpose

Systematic reviews comprehensively identify, evaluate, and synthesize all
relevant research on a specific question. They are the highest level of
evidence when done rigorously, following transparent and reproducible methods.

## Gold Standard Structure (adapted from Cochrane/PRISMA)

### 1. Research Question
- Structured using PICO: Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome
- Specific, answerable, and scoped appropriately

### 2. Search Strategy
- Databases searched (minimum 3 for rigor)
- Search terms and Boolean logic documented
- Date range and language restrictions stated
- Grey literature search strategy

### 3. Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria

| Criterion | Include | Exclude |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Population | Enterprise SaaS companies | Consumer, <$1M ARR |
| Intervention | AI-powered pricing | Manual pricing only |
| Outcome | Revenue impact measured | No quantitative outcome |
| Study type | Empirical, n>30 | Opinion pieces, case n=1 |
| Date | 2020-2025 | Pre-2020 |

### 4. PRISMA Flow

> Records identified: 847
> Duplicates removed: 203
> Screened (title/abstract): 644
> Full-text assessed: 89
> Studies included: 23
> Reasons for exclusion documented for all 66 rejected

### 5. Quality Assessment
Each included study rated on methodology quality.

### 6. Synthesis
- Narrative synthesis of findings organized by theme
- Quantitative synthesis (meta-analysis) where data permits
- Confidence assessment in overall body of evidence

### 7. Limitations
- What the review could not capture
- Publication bias assessment
- Quality limitations across included studies

## Example Systematic Review Summary

> **Question**: Does AI-assisted code review reduce defect rates in production?
>
> **Included**: 23 studies (2020-2025), total n=14,200 developers
> **Finding**: AI-assisted review reduced production defects by 12-28%
> (median 19%). Effect was strongest for security defects (31% reduction)
> and weakest for logic errors (8% reduction). Effect moderated by
> developer experience — junior developers benefited most.
>
> **Confidence**: MODERATE — consistent direction across studies but
> heterogeneous effect sizes and potential publication bias.

## Adaptation for Research Squad

- Apply systematic review principles to business research questions
- Document search strategy transparently in all research briefs
- Use PRISMA-style flow for source inclusion decisions
- Maintain quality assessment for all evidence tables
- Acknowledge this is rapid review, not full systematic review timeline
