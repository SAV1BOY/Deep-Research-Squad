# State of the Art Review — Swipe Examples

## Purpose

State-of-the-art reviews map the current frontier of knowledge in a domain.
Unlike systematic reviews (which answer specific questions), SOTA reviews
answer: "What do we know, what don't we know, and where is the field heading?"

## Gold Standard Structure

### 1. Domain Definition
- Clear boundary of what's covered
- Adjacent domains noted but excluded
- Time horizon (typically focuses on recent 3-5 years)

### 2. Knowledge Map

**Established Knowledge** (High confidence, broad consensus)
- Finding 1: [Claim] — supported by [N] studies, replicated
- Finding 2: [Claim] — meta-analysis confirms, effect size [X]

**Emerging Knowledge** (Moderate confidence, growing evidence)
- Finding 3: [Claim] — 5+ studies, consistent direction, methodology debates
- Finding 4: [Claim] — promising results but limited replication

**Frontier Knowledge** (Low confidence, early signals)
- Finding 5: [Claim] — 1-2 studies, novel methodology, watch this space
- Finding 6: [Claim] — theoretical support but empirical gap

**Active Controversies**
- Debate 1: [Position A] vs [Position B] — evidence split
- Debate 2: [Position A] vs [Position B] — methodological disagreement

### 3. Gap Analysis
- Questions the field hasn't addressed
- Methodological gaps limiting progress
- Data availability gaps

### 4. Trajectory Assessment
- Where the field is heading based on current research trends
- Funding patterns indicating future direction
- Key researchers and labs to watch

## Example SOTA Summary

> **Domain**: AI-assisted drug discovery (2021-2025)
>
> **Established**: AI can predict protein structures with high accuracy
> (AlphaFold2+). Virtual screening outperforms random screening by 10-50x.
>
> **Emerging**: Generative models can propose novel molecular structures
> with desired properties. Early clinical candidates from AI-first pipelines
> entering Phase I trials (Insilico Medicine, Recursion).
>
> **Frontier**: Foundation models for biology showing promise but not yet
> validated in prospective drug discovery. Multimodal models combining
> genomic, proteomic, and clinical data in early development.
>
> **Gap**: Limited prospective validation. Most AI drug discovery claims
> are retrospective. True test is Phase II/III success rates vs baseline.

## Quality Standards

1. Cover the full spectrum from established to frontier
2. Explicitly note confidence levels for each finding
3. Identify active controversies without taking sides prematurely
4. Include gap analysis — what we don't know is as important as what we do
5. Cite key researchers and groups driving the field forward

## SOTA Review vs Other Review Types

| Feature | SOTA Review | Systematic Review | Literature Review |
|---------|-----------|------------------|------------------|
| Scope | Broad domain | Specific question | Variable |
| Method | Semi-structured | Highly structured | Unstructured |
| Goal | Map the frontier | Answer a question | Summarize knowledge |
| Strength | Breadth + direction | Rigor + completeness | Accessibility |
