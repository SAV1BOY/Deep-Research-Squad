# Legal Search Operators

## Google Scholar Case Law
- **URL**: scholar.google.com (select "Case law" radio button)
- `"exact phrase"` - Exact legal terminology search
- Select specific courts: Federal, State, or individual courts
- Date range filters for temporal analysis
- "Cited by" for subsequent case history
- "How cited" for treatment analysis (positive/negative citations)

## Free Legal Databases

### CourtListener (Free Law Project)
- **URL**: courtlistener.com
- Full-text search of millions of federal and state court opinions
- RECAP archive: PACER documents made free
- Oral argument audio from appellate courts
- API available for programmatic access
- **Use**: Federal case law research, PACER document access

### Google Scholar Case Law
- Free access to federal and state court opinions
- "How cited" feature shows treatment of cases
- **Limitation**: Not comprehensive; missing many lower court decisions

### Congress.gov
- **URL**: congress.gov
- Full text of bills, resolutions, amendments
- Legislative history and status tracking
- Committee reports and hearing transcripts
- **Use**: US legislative research, bill tracking

### Federal Register
- **URL**: federalregister.gov
- Daily journal of the US federal government
- Proposed rules, final rules, notices, executive orders
- **Use**: Regulatory landscape analysis, compliance research

## Commercial Legal Databases

### Westlaw (Thomson Reuters)
- Comprehensive US and international case law, statutes, regulations
- Key Number System for topical classification
- KeyCite for citation verification and case treatment
- **Operators**: `"term" /s "term"` (same sentence), `/p` (same paragraph), `/n` (within n words)

### LexisNexis
- Comprehensive legal database comparable to Westlaw
- Shepard's Citations for case treatment analysis
- **Operators**: `w/n` (within n words), `w/s` (same sentence), `w/p` (same paragraph)

### PACER (Public Access to Court Electronic Records)
- **URL**: pacer.uscourts.gov
- Federal court filings (complaints, motions, orders, judgments)
- $0.10 per page, quarterly fee waiver under $30
- **Use**: Litigation research, corporate dispute analysis

## International Legal Sources

### EUR-Lex
- **URL**: eur-lex.europa.eu
- EU legislation, case law, preparatory acts
- **Use**: European regulatory research (GDPR, AI Act, etc.)

### WIPO Lex
- **URL**: wipo.int/wipolex
- IP legislation from all WIPO member states
- **Use**: International IP law research

### Brazil Legal
- **Planalto**: planalto.gov.br - Brazilian federal legislation
- **STF**: portal.stf.jus.br - Supreme Court jurisprudence
- **JusBrasil**: jusbrasil.com.br - Legal search aggregator

## Legal Research Best Practices
1. Verify case law is still "good law" (not overturned or distinguished)
2. Use citators (KeyCite, Shepard's, or Scholar "How cited") for treatment
3. Check statutory amendments - laws change frequently
4. Distinguish between binding and persuasive authority
5. Note jurisdiction - laws vary by state/country
6. For regulatory research, check both the rule AND enforcement actions
7. Read dissents for alternative legal reasoning and potential future shifts
