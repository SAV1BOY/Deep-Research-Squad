# Kumar - Research Methodology: A Step-by-Step Guide

## Research Methodology Fundamentals

### The Research Process (8 Steps)
1. Formulate a research problem
2. Conceptualize a research design
3. Construct an instrument for data collection
4. Select a sample
5. Write a research proposal
6. Collect data
7. Process and display data
8. Write a research report

### Types of Research by Purpose
- **Descriptive**: What is happening? (surveys, observational)
- **Correlational**: What is the relationship? (statistical association)
- **Explanatory**: Why is it happening? (causal mechanisms)
- **Exploratory**: What is this phenomenon? (open-ended inquiry)

## Study Design Fundamentals

### Variables
- **Independent**: The cause or predictor
- **Dependent**: The effect or outcome
- **Extraneous/Confounding**: Variables that could distort the relationship
- **Intervening**: Mechanisms through which IV affects DV

### Validity Types
- **Internal validity**: Did X actually cause Y? (control for confounds)
- **External validity**: Can results generalize? (sample representativeness)
- **Construct validity**: Are we measuring what we think? (operationalization)
- **Ecological validity**: Do results apply in real settings?

### Sampling Methods
**Probability (quantitative)**:
- Simple random, systematic, stratified, cluster

**Non-probability (qualitative)**:
- Purposive, convenience, snowball, quota

## Data Collection Methods
| Method | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Questionnaire | Large samples, standardized | Low response rate, superficial |
| Interview | Depth, flexibility, clarification | Time-intensive, interviewer bias |
| Observation | Natural behavior, nonverbal data | Observer effect, limited scope |
| Document analysis | Unobtrusive, historical reach | Incomplete records, access issues |

## Data Analysis Basics

### Quantitative
- Descriptive statistics: Mean, median, mode, SD
- Inferential statistics: t-test, ANOVA, chi-square, regression
- Effect size: Practical significance beyond p-values

### Qualitative
- Content analysis: Categorize and count themes
- Thematic analysis: Identify patterns across data
- Narrative analysis: Analyze stories and accounts

## Ethical Considerations
- Informed consent from participants
- Confidentiality and anonymity
- No harm to participants
- Transparency about research purpose
- Right to withdraw at any time

## Application to Deep Research
- Match your methodology to your research question type
- Always consider validity threats to your conclusions
- Use triangulation (multiple methods/sources) for robustness
- Document your methodology so others can assess your work
- Consider sampling bias in any data you collect or analyze
