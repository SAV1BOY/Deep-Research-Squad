# Prescott & Miller - Proven Strategies in Competitive Intelligence

## CI Program Design

### Building a CI Capability
1. **Needs assessment**: What decisions require intelligence?
2. **Stakeholder mapping**: Who needs what intelligence, when?
3. **Source development**: Build a network of information sources
4. **Process design**: Systematic collection, analysis, dissemination
5. **Technology infrastructure**: Tools for storage, analysis, sharing
6. **Performance measurement**: Track CI impact on decisions

### CI Maturity Levels
1. **Ad hoc**: Reactive, project-based, no formal process
2. **Developing**: Some processes, dedicated resources emerging
3. **Established**: Formal CI unit, defined processes, regular output
4. **Advanced**: Integrated into strategic planning, early warning systems
5. **World-class**: CI culture, continuous improvement, proven ROI

## Proven CI Strategies

### War Gaming
- Simulate competitor responses to your strategic moves
- Assign teams to play each competitor
- Each team develops strategy from competitor's perspective
- Reveals blind spots and assumptions
- Best conducted before major strategic decisions

### Scenario Development for CI
1. Identify key uncertainties (2-3 most impactful)
2. Develop 3-4 plausible scenarios
3. Assess competitor behavior under each scenario
4. Develop robust strategies that work across scenarios
5. Identify early indicators for each scenario

### Competitive Benchmarking
- Identify best practices, not just best performers
- Focus on processes, not just outcomes
- Include non-traditional competitors and adjacent industries
- Quantify gaps and set realistic targets

### Technology Intelligence
- Monitor patent filings for competitor R&D direction
- Track hiring patterns for technology capabilities
- Analyze open source contributions and technical blogs
- Attend technical conferences and track presentations

## Analysis Techniques

### Blind Spot Analysis
- What assumptions does the competitor hold?
- What information are they likely ignoring?
- Where is their analysis likely weakest?
- What would surprise them most?

### Win/Loss Analysis
- Systematic analysis of won and lost deals
- Interview customers about decision factors
- Identify patterns across wins and losses
- Feed insights back into strategy and CI priorities

### Competitor Financial Decomposition
- Break down revenue by segment, geography, product
- Analyze cost structure and margin drivers
- Identify investment priorities from capital allocation
- Track cash flow patterns for strategic flexibility

## Ethical Standards in CI
- Society of Competitive Intelligence Professionals (SCIP) code
- Full disclosure of identity when gathering information
- Respect all legal requirements
- Provide honest recommendations
- Promote ethical CI within the organization

## Application to Deep Research
- Structure CI around specific decision needs, not general curiosity
- Use war gaming to stress-test competitive assumptions
- Combine multiple analysis techniques for comprehensive view
- Always include a blind spot analysis for each competitor
- Win/loss analysis provides ground truth for competitive dynamics
- Measure CI effectiveness by its impact on decision quality
