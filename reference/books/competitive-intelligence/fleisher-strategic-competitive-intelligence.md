# Fleisher & Bensoussan - Strategic and Competitive Analysis

## Strategic Analysis Methods

### STEEP/PESTEL Analysis
- **Social**: Demographics, cultural trends, lifestyle changes
- **Technological**: Innovation pace, R&D, digital transformation
- **Economic**: GDP, inflation, interest rates, exchange rates
- **Environmental**: Regulations, sustainability, climate impact
- **Political**: Government stability, trade policy, regulation
- **Legal**: Antitrust, IP law, labor law, compliance

### Porter's Five Forces (Applied)
Assess each force as High/Medium/Low with specific evidence:
1. Threat of new entrants (barriers to entry)
2. Bargaining power of suppliers
3. Bargaining power of buyers
4. Threat of substitutes
5. Rivalry among existing competitors

### SWOT Analysis (Done Right)
- Must be evidence-based, not wishful thinking
- Strengths/Weaknesses: Internal, relative to competitors
- Opportunities/Threats: External, market-driven
- Cross-reference: How do strengths address opportunities? How do weaknesses amplify threats?

### Value Chain Analysis
- Identify each activity in the value chain
- Assess cost and differentiation at each stage
- Compare against competitors at each stage
- Find where value is created and captured

## Competitive Positioning Tools

### Strategic Group Mapping
1. Identify two key dimensions of competition
2. Plot competitors on both dimensions
3. Identify clusters (strategic groups)
4. Analyze mobility barriers between groups
5. Identify white space opportunities

### Competitor Response Profiling
- What drives the competitor? (Goals, assumptions)
- What is the competitor doing? (Current strategy)
- What can the competitor do? (Capabilities)
- What will the competitor do? (Predicted response)

### Benchmarking Process
1. Identify what to benchmark
2. Identify best-in-class performers
3. Collect data on practices and metrics
4. Analyze performance gaps
5. Set improvement targets
6. Implement and monitor

## Financial Analysis for CI
- Revenue growth trends and composition
- Margin analysis (gross, operating, net)
- R&D spending as percentage of revenue
- Customer acquisition cost trends
- Cash flow and investment patterns
- Debt structure and financial health

## Application to Deep Research
- Use multiple frameworks; no single tool captures full picture
- Always ground analysis in specific evidence, not generalizations
- Compare competitors on specific measurable dimensions
- Update analyses quarterly as conditions change
- Present findings as actionable intelligence, not academic exercises
- Combine quantitative (financial) and qualitative (strategic) analysis
