# Fuld - The New Competitor Intelligence

## CI Methodology Framework

### The Intelligence Cycle
1. **Planning & Direction**: Define intelligence needs, key questions
2. **Collection**: Gather raw data from multiple sources
3. **Processing**: Organize, filter, validate data
4. **Analysis**: Transform data into actionable intelligence
5. **Dissemination**: Deliver insights to decision makers

### Key Intelligence Topics (KITs)
- Competitor strategies and capabilities
- Market trends and disruptions
- Regulatory and political changes
- Technology developments
- Customer behavior shifts

## Collection Techniques

### Primary Sources (Human Intelligence)
- Trade show conversations and observations
- Industry conference presentations
- Job posting analysis (reveals strategic direction)
- Customer and supplier interviews
- Former employee insights (within legal/ethical bounds)

### Secondary Sources (Published Intelligence)
- SEC/CVM filings and financial reports
- Patent filings and applications
- Press releases and news articles
- Industry analyst reports
- Academic research and white papers
- Social media and online forums

### Creative Sources
- Help wanted ads reveal new initiatives
- Building permits signal expansion plans
- Shipping records indicate supply chain changes
- Domain name registrations hint at new products
- Regulatory filings reveal compliance strategies

## Analysis Frameworks

### Competitor Profiling
1. Background and history
2. Financial performance and resources
3. Current strategy and objectives
4. Capabilities and core competencies
5. Assumptions and blind spots
6. Likely future moves

### Early Warning Indicators
- Changes in hiring patterns
- Patent filing activity shifts
- New partnerships or acquisitions
- Pricing changes
- Marketing message shifts
- Leadership changes

## Ethical Guidelines
- Never misrepresent your identity
- Never steal proprietary information
- Never bribe or blackmail for information
- Respect NDAs and trade secrets
- Use only legally obtainable information
- Document all sources for audit trail

## Application to Deep Research
- Structure competitive analysis around the intelligence cycle
- Combine primary and secondary sources for triangulation
- Build competitor profiles as living documents
- Set up early warning indicators for each competitor
- Always maintain ethical standards in information gathering
- Validate intelligence from multiple independent sources
