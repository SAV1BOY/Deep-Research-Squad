# Academic Databases

## Primary Academic Search Platforms

### PubMed / MEDLINE
- **URL**: pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
- **Coverage**: Biomedical and life sciences, 35M+ citations
- **Operator**: National Library of Medicine (NLM)
- **Strengths**: MeSH controlled vocabulary, free full-text links, comprehensive biomedical coverage
- **Search Tips**: Use MeSH terms for precision; Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT)
- **API**: E-utilities API for programmatic access
- **Use**: Health, biotech, pharmaceutical, neuroscience research

### arXiv
- **URL**: arxiv.org
- **Coverage**: Physics, mathematics, computer science, quantitative biology, statistics, economics, electrical engineering
- **Operator**: Cornell University
- **Strengths**: Preprints available immediately, free access, full text
- **Limitations**: Not peer-reviewed; quality varies significantly
- **Search Tips**: Use category filters (cs.AI, cs.CL, stat.ML, etc.)
- **Use**: Cutting-edge AI/ML research, physics, mathematics

### SSRN (Social Science Research Network)
- **URL**: ssrn.com
- **Coverage**: Social sciences, economics, law, business, humanities
- **Operator**: Elsevier (since 2016)
- **Strengths**: Early-stage working papers, author rankings, download metrics
- **Limitations**: Quality varies (working papers); Elsevier paywall concerns
- **Search Tips**: Filter by network (FEN, LSN, etc.) for domain focus
- **Use**: Finance, economics, law, management research

### Google Scholar
- **URL**: scholar.google.com
- **Coverage**: Broad cross-disciplinary, includes books, theses, patents
- **Strengths**: Largest index, citation tracking, "Cited by" feature, free
- **Limitations**: No controlled vocabulary, includes non-peer-reviewed sources, opaque ranking
- **Search Tips**: Use "allintitle:" for precise title search, author:"Name" for author search
- **Use**: Discovery and citation tracking across all disciplines

### Semantic Scholar
- **URL**: semanticscholar.org
- **Coverage**: 200M+ papers across all fields
- **Operator**: Allen Institute for AI
- **Strengths**: AI-powered relevance ranking, TLDR summaries, citation context
- **API**: Free API for programmatic access with good rate limits
- **Use**: AI-assisted literature discovery, citation graph analysis

### Scopus
- **URL**: scopus.com
- **Coverage**: 84M+ records, 27K+ journals
- **Operator**: Elsevier
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive citation analysis, author profiles, institutional data
- **Limitations**: Subscription required
- **Use**: Bibliometric analysis, author evaluation, journal comparison

### Web of Science
- **URL**: webofscience.com
- **Coverage**: 21K+ journals, 90M+ records
- **Operator**: Clarivate Analytics
- **Strengths**: Journal Impact Factor source, rigorous journal selection, strong citation tools
- **Limitations**: Subscription required; narrower coverage than Scopus
- **Use**: Citation analysis, journal quality assessment

## Search Strategy Best Practices
1. Start broad with Google Scholar for discovery
2. Use specialized databases (PubMed, arXiv, SSRN) for domain depth
3. Track citations bidirectionally (who cited this? what did this cite?)
4. Use Semantic Scholar API for automated literature monitoring
5. Cross-reference across databases to avoid selection bias
