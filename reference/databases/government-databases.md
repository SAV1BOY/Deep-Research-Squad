# Government and International Organization Databases

## International Organizations

### World Bank Open Data
- **URL**: data.worldbank.org
- **Coverage**: 1,400+ indicators, 217 economies, decades of time series
- **Key Datasets**: GDP, poverty, education, health, trade, climate
- **API**: Free REST API with JSON/XML output
- **Strengths**: Standardized cross-country comparisons, long time series, free
- **Use**: Macroeconomic analysis, development indicators, country comparisons

### OECD Data
- **URL**: data.oecd.org
- **Coverage**: 38 member countries + partners, economic and social indicators
- **Key Datasets**: GDP, trade, employment, education, health, innovation (OECD.Stat)
- **API**: SDMX REST API for programmatic access
- **Strengths**: High-quality standardized data, detailed methodology documentation
- **Use**: Policy analysis, developed economy comparisons, innovation metrics

### UN Data
- **URL**: data.un.org
- **Coverage**: Global, all UN agency statistical databases
- **Key Datasets**: Population, trade (UN Comtrade), development (UNDP HDI)
- **Strengths**: Broadest country coverage, SDG indicators
- **Use**: Global development metrics, population data, trade flows

### IMF Data
- **URL**: data.imf.org
- **Coverage**: Macroeconomic and financial data for 190 countries
- **Key Datasets**: World Economic Outlook, Balance of Payments, Financial Soundness
- **Strengths**: Authoritative macroeconomic forecasts, financial stability data
- **Use**: Economic forecasting, fiscal policy analysis, financial system assessment

## US Government Databases

### US Census Bureau
- **URL**: data.census.gov
- **Coverage**: Population, housing, business, economic data for the US
- **Key Datasets**: ACS, Economic Census, County Business Patterns, ASE
- **API**: Census API with free key
- **Use**: US demographic analysis, market sizing, business demographics

### Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS)
- **URL**: bls.gov/data
- **Coverage**: Employment, wages, prices, productivity, occupational data
- **Key Datasets**: CPI, PPI, Current Employment Statistics, JOLTS
- **Use**: Labor market analysis, inflation tracking, wage benchmarking

### Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA)
- **URL**: bea.gov
- **Coverage**: GDP, personal income, trade, industry accounts
- **Use**: US economic output analysis, regional economics

### Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)
- **URL**: fred.stlouisfed.org
- **Coverage**: 800K+ time series from 100+ sources
- **Strengths**: Excellent visualization, easy API, combines many sources
- **API**: Free API, widely used in economics research
- **Use**: Single-stop for US economic and financial data

## Brazil-Specific Databases
- **IBGE**: ibge.gov.br - Brazilian census, GDP, demographic data
- **Banco Central do Brasil**: bcb.gov.br - Monetary policy, financial data
- **IPEA Data**: ipeadata.gov.br - Social and economic research data
- **RAIS/CAGED**: Ministry of Labor employment data

## Best Practices
1. Always check methodology documentation for cross-country comparisons
2. Use FRED as a convenient aggregator, but cite the original source
3. Be aware of revision cycles - preliminary data gets revised
4. World Bank and OECD data are complementary; OECD is deeper for member countries
5. For Brazil/LATAM context, combine IBGE with World Bank for benchmarking
