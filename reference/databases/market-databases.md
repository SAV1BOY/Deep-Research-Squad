# Market and Startup Databases

## Venture Capital and Startup Data

### Crunchbase
- **URL**: crunchbase.com
- **Coverage**: 2M+ companies, funding rounds, acquisitions, people, investors
- **Free tier**: Basic company profiles, limited search
- **Paid tier**: Advanced search, exports, API access, contact data
- **Key Features**: Funding round history, investor profiles, company graph, trend analysis
- **Search Tips**: Use "Discover" for filtered searches; track specific sectors/stages
- **API**: Available on Pro/Enterprise plans
- **Use**: Startup discovery, funding landscape, competitive mapping

### PitchBook
- **URL**: pitchbook.com
- **Coverage**: 3.4M+ companies, PE/VC deals, M&A, public markets
- **Access**: Subscription only (enterprise pricing)
- **Key Features**: Detailed financial data, valuation estimates, fund performance, comparables
- **Strengths**: Deepest PE/VC data, cap table estimates, custom screening
- **Use**: Investment analysis, market sizing, deal sourcing, due diligence

### CB Insights
- **URL**: cbinsights.com
- **Coverage**: Private companies, emerging technology, VC trends
- **Key Features**: Mosaic score, market maps, patent analytics, earnings transcript analysis
- **Access**: Enterprise subscription; blog/newsletter free
- **Use**: Trend identification, competitive intelligence, industry mapping

### Dealroom
- **URL**: dealroom.co
- **Coverage**: European and global startup ecosystem
- **Key Features**: Strong European coverage, ecosystem rankings, government partnerships
- **Access**: Freemium model with paid tiers
- **Use**: European startup research, ecosystem analysis

### Tracxn
- **URL**: tracxn.com
- **Coverage**: 10M+ companies globally, 900+ sectors
- **Key Features**: Granular sector taxonomy, company scoring, feed-based monitoring
- **Access**: Subscription required; limited free access
- **Use**: Sector-specific deal sourcing and competitive analysis

## Market Intelligence Platforms

### Gartner
- **URL**: gartner.com
- **Use**: Technology market research, Magic Quadrants, Hype Cycles

### IDC (International Data Corporation)
- **URL**: idc.com
- **Use**: IT market sizing, forecasts, vendor market share

### Forrester
- **URL**: forrester.com
- **Use**: Technology and business strategy research, Wave reports

## Best Practices
1. Cross-reference startup data across Crunchbase and PitchBook for completeness
2. Use CB Insights for trend analysis, PitchBook for financial details
3. Be aware of data lag - funding rounds may take weeks to appear
4. Self-reported data can be inaccurate - verify key figures independently
5. Free tiers provide directional data; paid tiers needed for comprehensive analysis
