# Gartner Hype Cycles

## Overview
Gartner's Hype Cycle is a graphical representation of technology maturity and adoption. Published annually across 90+ topic areas. Gartner is a leading IT research and advisory firm.

## The Five Phases
1. **Innovation Trigger** - Breakthrough, early proof-of-concept, media interest
2. **Peak of Inflated Expectations** - Over-enthusiasm, unrealistic projections
3. **Trough of Disillusionment** - Interest wanes as experiments fail, shakeout occurs
4. **Slope of Enlightenment** - Understanding crystallizes, practical use cases emerge
5. **Plateau of Productivity** - Mainstream adoption, market applicability clear

## Key Hype Cycles for Research
- **Hype Cycle for Emerging Technologies** - Flagship annual publication
- **Hype Cycle for Artificial Intelligence** - AI-specific maturity assessment
- **Hype Cycle for Digital Commerce** - E-commerce and fintech trends
- **Hype Cycle for Cloud Computing** - Infrastructure and platform evolution
- **Hype Cycle for Blockchain and Web3** - Decentralized technology maturity

## Time-to-Plateau Estimates
- Less than 2 years / 2-5 years / 5-10 years / More than 10 years / Obsolete before plateau
- These estimates have mixed historical accuracy but useful for relative comparisons

## Using Hype Cycles in Research
- **Strength**: Quick visual snapshot of technology maturity landscape
- **Strength**: Useful framework for setting expectations with stakeholders
- **Limitation**: Paywalled - full reports require Gartner subscription
- **Limitation**: Subjective placement; methodology is opinion-based, not empirical
- **Limitation**: Binary thinking (hype vs reality) oversimplifies complex adoption patterns

## Critical Assessment
- Hype Cycle positions are determined by analyst judgment, not quantitative models
- Technologies can skip phases or regress
- The model assumes a single adoption curve; reality has multiple overlapping curves
- Useful as a conversation framework, not a predictive tool
- Cross-reference with actual market data (funding, revenue, adoption metrics)

## Access and Citation
- **Access**: Requires Gartner subscription; summaries available in press releases
- **Workaround**: Search for "[Technology] Gartner Hype Cycle [Year]" press coverage
- **Citation**: Gartner, "Hype Cycle for [Topic]," [Year]
- **Note**: Gartner prohibits reproducing the graphic without permission

## Historical Accuracy Observations
- Correctly identified AI/ML trajectory through hype and disillusionment to mainstream
- Overestimated speed for blockchain, 3D printing consumer adoption
- Underestimated speed for cloud computing, mobile payments
- Best used as one input among many, not as standalone forecast
