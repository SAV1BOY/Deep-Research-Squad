# CB Insights Reports

## Overview
CB Insights is a tech market intelligence platform that uses data science, ML, and predictive analytics to analyze private company data, emerging tech trends, and venture capital activity.

## Key Report Series

### State of Venture (Quarterly)
- Global VC funding trends, deal counts, and mega-round activity
- Geographic and sector breakdowns
- Unicorn tracker and birth/death rates
- **Use**: Understanding investment flow patterns and startup ecosystem health

### AI 100 (Annual)
- 100 most promising private AI companies across categories
- Selection methodology: patent activity, investor quality, market potential, team strength
- **Use**: Identifying leading AI startups and emerging application areas

### Fintech 250 / Healthtech 150 / Retail Tech 100
- Curated lists of top private companies by sector
- Company profiles with funding, investors, and technology details
- **Use**: Competitive landscape mapping for specific industries

### Industry Analyst Reports
- Deep dives into emerging technology sectors
- Market maps showing competitive landscapes
- Disruption timelines for traditional industries
- **Use**: Understanding market structure and competitive dynamics

## Data Products
- **Mosaic Score**: Quantitative scoring of private company health (momentum, market, money)
- **Business Social Graph**: Relationship mapping between companies, investors, and people
- **Patent Analytics**: Technology trend analysis through patent filings
- **Earnings Call Transcripts**: NLP analysis of public company strategy signals

## How to Use CB Insights in Research
- **Strength**: Real-time private company data; strong visualization and taxonomy
- **Strength**: Data-driven approach reduces subjective bias in company evaluation
- **Limitation**: Paywalled - most detailed reports require subscription
- **Workaround**: Blog posts and newsletters share key findings publicly
- **Limitation**: US/tech-centric; less coverage of non-US and non-tech sectors

## Access and Citation
- **Free content**: cbinsights.com/research - newsletters and blog posts
- **Paid content**: Full platform requires enterprise subscription
- **Citation**: CB Insights, "Report Title," Month Year

## Evaluation Notes
- Generally reliable for VC/startup data due to direct data collection
- Predictions and forecasts should be cross-referenced
- Market size estimates can be aggressive (common in tech research)
- Strong for identifying patterns but weaker on causal analysis
- Useful complement to PitchBook and Crunchbase data
