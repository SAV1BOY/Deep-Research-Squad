# Statista Market Reports

## Overview
Statista aggregates statistical data from over 22,500 sources into a single platform. Provides market forecasts, industry reports, infographics, and company statistics across 170+ industries and 150+ countries.

## Key Data Products

### Market Insights
- Market size and growth forecasts by industry and region
- Revenue projections, user metrics, and penetration rates
- Competitive landscape data with market share breakdowns
- **Coverage**: Digital markets, consumer goods, industrial, services

### Industry Reports (Statista Industry Reports)
- Structured overviews of 170+ industries worldwide
- Key metrics: revenue, employment, companies, trade data
- SWOT analysis and trend identification per industry
- **Use**: Quick industry overview and sizing for research scoping

### Consumer Insights
- Survey data on consumer behavior, preferences, and attitudes
- Brand awareness and perception tracking
- Cross-country comparisons on consumption patterns
- **Use**: Understanding demand-side dynamics and consumer trends

### Country Reports
- Macroeconomic indicators per country
- Industry breakdown by country
- Demographic and socioeconomic data
- **Use**: Market entry analysis and country-level benchmarking

### Company Profiles (ecommerceDB, appDB)
- E-commerce company profiles with revenue estimates
- App store performance data
- **Use**: Competitive analysis for digital businesses

## How to Use Statista in Research
- **Strength**: Wide coverage, standardized format, easy visualization
- **Strength**: Good for quick data points and supporting statistics
- **Limitation**: Aggregator - always check the original source for methodology
- **Limitation**: Forecasts use proprietary models with limited transparency
- **Limitation**: Requires subscription for full access

## Access and Citation
- **Free**: Limited charts and basic statistics
- **Paid**: Full access requires Statista subscription (Basic, Professional, Enterprise)
- **Citation**: Statista, "[Chart/Report Title]," [Year]. Source: [Original data provider]
- **Important**: Always cite the original data source, not just Statista as aggregator

## Evaluation Notes
- Useful as a starting point but always trace back to primary data sources
- Market size estimates can vary significantly from other providers
- Forecast methodology is not always transparent - cross-reference with IDC, Gartner, etc.
- Best for: quick stats, presentation-ready charts, broad market overviews
- Weaker for: deep analysis, methodology transparency, niche markets
