# Key Research Methodology Papers

## Foundational Methodology

### "The Design of Experiments" - Fisher (1935)
- Established randomization, replication, and blocking
- Foundation of experimental design
- **Key takeaway**: Proper experimental design is prerequisite for valid inference

### "Qualitative Data Analysis" - Miles & Huberman (1994)
- Systematic approaches to qualitative analysis
- Data displays, coding, and pattern identification
- **Key takeaway**: Qualitative analysis can be rigorous and systematic

### "Naturalistic Inquiry" - Lincoln & Guba (1985)
- Trustworthiness criteria for qualitative research
- Credibility, transferability, dependability, confirmability
- **Key takeaway**: Quality criteria for non-positivist research

## Mixed Methods

### "Toward a Definition of Mixed Methods Research" - Johnson et al. (2007)
- Synthesized 19 definitions of mixed methods
- Established mixed methods as a third methodological movement
- **Key takeaway**: Mixed methods combines philosophical and practical considerations

### "The Convergent Parallel Design" - Creswell & Plano Clark (2011)
- Practical guide to mixed methods design
- Six major design types with decision criteria
- **Key takeaway**: Choose design based on timing, priority, and mixing strategy

## Sampling and Validity

### "Sample Size in Qualitative Research" - Morse (2000)
- Guidelines for determining sample size in qualitative studies
- Saturation as the primary criterion
- **Key takeaway**: Sample size depends on scope, data quality, and research design

### "Construct Validity in Psychological Tests" - Cronbach & Meehl (1955)
- Defined construct validity and the nomological network
- Foundation for all measurement validation
- **Key takeaway**: Validity is not a property of a test but of test score interpretations

## Systematic Review Methods

### "PRISMA Statement" - Moher et al. (2009)
- Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses
- 27-item checklist and flow diagram
- **Key takeaway**: Transparent reporting is essential for review credibility

### "Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews" - Higgins et al.
- Gold standard methodology for systematic reviews
- Risk of bias assessment, data extraction, synthesis
- **Key takeaway**: Systematic reviews require rigorous, pre-specified protocols

## Research Ethics

### "The Belmont Report" (1979)
- Respect for persons, beneficence, justice
- Foundation of research ethics in the US
- **Key takeaway**: Ethical considerations must precede methodological ones

## Application to Deep Research
- Use PRISMA guidelines when conducting systematic evidence reviews
- Apply trustworthiness criteria to qualitative findings
- Consider construct validity when using any measurement or metric
- Follow mixed methods design principles when combining data types
- Document sampling rationale explicitly
