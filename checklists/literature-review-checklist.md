# Literature Review Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure that literature reviews are systematic, comprehensive, and methodologically sound. This checklist validates that the review captures the relevant body of knowledge, identifies gaps, and synthesizes findings in a structured manner.

## When to Use
- Before finalizing any literature review deliverable
- When validating the completeness of a systematic or scoping review
- During peer review of literature review drafts
- When assessing whether a review adequately supports subsequent research phases

## Prerequisites
- Search strategy has been defined and documented (databases, keywords, date ranges)
- Inclusion and exclusion criteria have been established before the search begins
- Research question or review objective has been formally approved
- Access to required databases and repositories has been confirmed
- Citation management system is set up and functional

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Search Strategy and Coverage
1. [ ] Search strategy is documented with specific databases, keywords, and Boolean operators — PASS: fully reproducible; FAIL: incomplete documentation
2. [ ] Minimum of 3 databases or source categories have been searched — PASS: 3+ sources; FAIL: fewer than 3
3. [ ] Date range for the search is explicitly stated and justified — PASS: range stated with rationale; FAIL: arbitrary or unstated range
4. [ ] Grey literature and non-academic sources have been considered — PASS: grey literature addressed; FAIL: exclusively academic sources without justification
5. [ ] Snowball sampling (checking reference lists) has been performed — PASS: backward and forward citation checks done; FAIL: no citation tracing

### Section 2: Selection and Screening
6. [ ] Inclusion criteria are clearly defined and applied consistently — PASS: criteria documented and applied; FAIL: ad hoc selection
7. [ ] Exclusion criteria are clearly defined with rationale — PASS: exclusions justified; FAIL: arbitrary exclusions
8. [ ] Number of sources identified, screened, and included is reported — PASS: flow data provided; FAIL: no screening metrics
9. [ ] Screening was performed against predefined criteria — PASS: systematic screening; FAIL: subjective filtering
10. [ ] Final corpus size is appropriate for the topic scope — PASS: 20+ sources for focused topics, 50+ for broad; FAIL: insufficient coverage

### Section 3: Analysis and Synthesis
11. [ ] Sources are organized thematically rather than merely listed — PASS: thematic structure; FAIL: source-by-source listing only
12. [ ] Key themes and patterns across the literature are identified — PASS: synthesis present; FAIL: no cross-source analysis
13. [ ] Areas of consensus in the literature are highlighted — PASS: agreements noted; FAIL: consensus not addressed
14. [ ] Areas of disagreement or debate are documented — PASS: controversies mapped; FAIL: disagreements ignored
15. [ ] Gaps in the existing literature are explicitly identified — PASS: gaps section present; FAIL: no gap analysis
16. [ ] Methodological quality of reviewed sources is assessed — PASS: quality commentary included; FAIL: all sources treated equally regardless of rigor

### Section 4: Documentation and Transparency
17. [ ] All sources are properly cited in a consistent format — PASS: uniform citation style; FAIL: inconsistent or incomplete citations
18. [ ] A summary table of key sources is provided — PASS: table with author, year, method, findings; FAIL: no summary table
19. [ ] Review methodology section is present and detailed — PASS: methodology described; FAIL: no methodology section
20. [ ] Limitations of the review itself are acknowledged — PASS: limitations stated; FAIL: no self-assessment
21. [ ] Date of the review and last search date are recorded — PASS: dates documented; FAIL: undated review

### Section 5: Conclusions and Implications
22. [ ] Conclusions are grounded in the reviewed evidence — PASS: conclusions trace to sources; FAIL: conclusions exceed evidence
23. [ ] Implications for the current research project are stated — PASS: practical relevance articulated; FAIL: abstract conclusions only
24. [ ] Recommendations for further research are provided — PASS: next steps suggested; FAIL: no forward-looking recommendations
25. [ ] Review answers the original research question or objective — PASS: question addressed; FAIL: scope drift from original question

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 25) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 92-100%: Excellent — comprehensive and rigorous review
  - 80-91%: Good — solid review with minor gaps
  - 68-79%: Acceptable — notable gaps that should be addressed
  - Below 68%: Insufficient — significant rework required

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 80% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 6, 11, or 15
- **Revise**: Score of 68-79%, or any FAIL on items 1, 6, 11, or 15
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 68%, or systematic bias detected in source selection, or review scope fundamentally misaligned with research needs
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Review reveals that the original research question needs to be reformulated based on the state of the literature
