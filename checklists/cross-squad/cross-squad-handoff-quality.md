# Cross-Squad Handoff Quality Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that research outputs handed off from one squad to another
meet quality and completeness standards sufficient for the receiving squad
to continue work without backtracking. Poor handoffs cause rework, introduce
errors at squad boundaries, and break the chain of research integrity.
This gate protects the seam between squads where quality most commonly
degrades.

## Gate Question

**Does this handoff meet quality standards for the receiving squad?**

A handoff must contain all information the receiving squad needs to continue
work effectively, in a format they can consume, with clear provenance and
explicit documentation of what is complete, what is in progress, and what
remains undone.

## Pass Criteria

1. The handoff document identifies the sending and receiving squads explicitly.
2. The research context and original problem framing are included for continuity.
3. All findings are accompanied by their evidence sources and trust assessments.
4. The current status of each research thread is clearly marked (complete, in progress, blocked).
5. Open questions and unresolved issues are explicitly listed with context.
6. Assumptions made by the sending squad are documented for the receiving squad to validate.
7. Data files, references, and supporting materials are organized and accessible.
8. The handoff format matches the receiving squad's expected input format.
9. Key contacts and subject matter experts consulted are listed for follow-up.
10. Known risks, limitations, and quality caveats are prominently disclosed.
11. A brief summary is provided that allows the receiving squad to orient quickly.
12. Dependencies on external data, approvals, or events are flagged with timelines.
13. The handoff has been reviewed by the sending squad lead before transfer.

## Fail Actions

- If context or problem framing is missing, add it before handing off.
- If evidence sources lack trust assessments, complete source evaluation first.
- If status of research threads is unclear, categorize each as complete, in progress, or blocked.
- If open questions are unlisted, enumerate them with available context.
- If assumptions are undocumented, make them explicit before transfer.
- If supporting materials are disorganized, structure them for the receiving squad.
- If the format does not match expectations, convert to the required format.
- If no squad lead review has occurred, obtain sign-off before transfer.
- Do not transfer the handoff until all quality criteria are met.
