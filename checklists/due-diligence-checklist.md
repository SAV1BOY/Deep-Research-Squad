# Due Diligence Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure that due diligence research meets the heightened standards of accuracy, completeness, and objectivity required for investment, partnership, acquisition, or strategic decisions with significant financial or operational consequences. Due diligence demands the highest evidentiary bar of any research type.

## When to Use
- Before delivering any due diligence report or findings
- When evaluating companies, assets, or partnerships for investment or acquisition
- During vendor, supplier, or partner evaluation processes
- When due diligence findings will inform binding agreements or material commitments

## Prerequisites
- Due diligence scope and mandate have been formally defined
- Legal and compliance framework for the investigation is established
- Confidentiality agreements and data room access are in place where applicable
- Checklist of required information has been agreed upon with deal team or stakeholders
- Timeline and decision milestones are documented

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Scope and Mandate Compliance
1. [ ] Due diligence scope document is referenced and followed — PASS: scope fully covered; FAIL: scope gaps or unauthorized scope creep
2. [ ] All mandatory investigation areas have been addressed — PASS: complete coverage; FAIL: any required area skipped
3. [ ] Materiality thresholds for findings are defined — PASS: thresholds set; FAIL: no materiality framework
4. [ ] Timeline milestones have been met or deviations documented — PASS: on schedule or variance explained; FAIL: unexplained delays

### Section 2: Financial and Operational Review
5. [ ] Financial statements have been reviewed for the specified period — PASS: full period reviewed; FAIL: incomplete financial review
6. [ ] Revenue quality and sustainability have been assessed — PASS: revenue analysis complete; FAIL: top-line figures accepted without scrutiny
7. [ ] Key financial ratios and metrics are calculated and benchmarked — PASS: ratios compared to industry norms; FAIL: no benchmarking
8. [ ] Operating model and cost structure are analyzed — PASS: cost analysis done; FAIL: cost structure not examined
9. [ ] Working capital and cash flow dynamics are evaluated — PASS: cash flow analysis present; FAIL: liquidity not assessed
10. [ ] Contingent liabilities and off-balance-sheet items are investigated — PASS: contingencies examined; FAIL: off-balance-sheet blind spots

### Section 3: Legal and Compliance
11. [ ] Corporate structure and ownership are verified — PASS: ownership confirmed; FAIL: unverified ownership claims
12. [ ] Material contracts and agreements are reviewed — PASS: key contracts examined; FAIL: contract review incomplete
13. [ ] Litigation history and pending legal matters are documented — PASS: legal landscape mapped; FAIL: litigation not investigated
14. [ ] Regulatory compliance status is assessed — PASS: compliance review done; FAIL: regulatory status unknown
15. [ ] Intellectual property portfolio is cataloged and assessed — PASS: IP reviewed; FAIL: IP not examined

### Section 4: Risk Identification
16. [ ] Red flags and material concerns are clearly highlighted — PASS: red flags section present with severity ratings; FAIL: concerns buried in text
17. [ ] Each risk is rated by likelihood and potential impact — PASS: risk scoring applied; FAIL: unrated risks
18. [ ] Deal-breaker issues are explicitly called out — PASS: showstoppers identified if any; FAIL: no deal-breaker assessment
19. [ ] Information gaps and areas of uncertainty are cataloged — PASS: gaps documented; FAIL: false completeness implied
20. [ ] Mitigation strategies or contractual protections are suggested for key risks — PASS: mitigations proposed; FAIL: risks without remedies

### Section 5: Evidence Standards
21. [ ] All findings are supported by documentary evidence — PASS: evidence-based findings; FAIL: opinion-based conclusions
22. [ ] Source documents are cataloged and accessible — PASS: document index maintained; FAIL: sources not traceable
23. [ ] Conflicting information has been investigated and resolved or flagged — PASS: conflicts addressed; FAIL: contradictions ignored
24. [ ] Management representations have been independently verified where possible — PASS: claims verified; FAIL: unverified management assertions accepted
25. [ ] Findings distinguish between verified facts, inferences, and assumptions — PASS: clear categorization; FAIL: facts and assumptions mixed

### Section 6: Reporting and Deliverables
26. [ ] Executive summary presents key findings and overall assessment — PASS: summary with clear verdict; FAIL: no executive overview
27. [ ] Detailed findings are organized by investigation area — PASS: structured report; FAIL: unstructured narrative
28. [ ] Appendices include supporting documentation and data — PASS: appendices complete; FAIL: supporting materials missing

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 28) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 95-100%: Excellent — meets the highest due diligence standards
  - 85-94%: Good — minor items to address but fundamentally sound
  - 75-84%: Acceptable — gaps must be closed before decision reliance
  - Below 75%: Insufficient — material gaps that prevent informed decision-making

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 85% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 16, 18, 21, or 24
- **Revise**: Score of 75-84%, or any FAIL on items 1, 16, 18, 21, or 24
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 75%, or discovery of potential fraud indicators, or inability to access critical information
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Deal-breaker issues identified, or due diligence timeline cannot accommodate required investigation depth, or findings materially contradict deal thesis
