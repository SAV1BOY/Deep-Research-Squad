# Market Research Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Validate that market research deliverables provide accurate, timely, and actionable intelligence about market dynamics, customer segments, competitive landscape, and growth opportunities. This checklist ensures research is grounded in data and suitable for strategic decision-making.

## When to Use
- Before delivering market research reports or briefs
- When reviewing market sizing, segmentation, or opportunity analyses
- During quality assurance of customer or industry research
- When market research is a component of a larger strategic engagement

## Prerequisites
- Market research scope and objectives have been defined and agreed upon
- Target market geography, industry, and segment boundaries are established
- Data sources (industry reports, surveys, databases) have been identified
- Stakeholder requirements for granularity and format are understood
- Relevant time horizon for the analysis has been confirmed

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Market Definition and Scope
1. [ ] Target market is clearly defined with geographic and segment boundaries — PASS: unambiguous definition; FAIL: vague or overly broad scope
2. [ ] Total addressable market (TAM), serviceable addressable market (SAM), and serviceable obtainable market (SOM) are distinguished where relevant — PASS: market layers defined; FAIL: single undifferentiated market figure
3. [ ] Time horizon for projections is stated — PASS: explicit time frame; FAIL: undated projections
4. [ ] Market definition aligns with the stakeholder's strategic context — PASS: alignment confirmed; FAIL: mismatch with stakeholder needs

### Section 2: Data Quality and Sources
5. [ ] Market size figures cite specific sources — PASS: sourced figures; FAIL: unsourced estimates
6. [ ] At least 3 independent data sources are used for market sizing — PASS: triangulated data; FAIL: single-source reliance
7. [ ] Data recency is within 18 months unless historical trend requires older data — PASS: current data; FAIL: stale figures without justification
8. [ ] Primary research (surveys, interviews) supplements secondary data where feasible — PASS: primary data included or justified exclusion; FAIL: secondary-only without rationale
9. [ ] Currency, units, and exchange rate assumptions are stated — PASS: all units clear; FAIL: ambiguous figures

### Section 3: Analysis Depth
10. [ ] Market segmentation is performed along at least 2 dimensions — PASS: multi-dimensional segmentation; FAIL: single or no segmentation
11. [ ] Growth drivers and inhibitors are identified and ranked — PASS: drivers and barriers listed with priority; FAIL: missing or unranked
12. [ ] Customer needs or pain points are documented with supporting evidence — PASS: evidence-based needs analysis; FAIL: assumed needs without validation
13. [ ] Competitive landscape overview is included — PASS: key players identified; FAIL: no competitive context
14. [ ] Regulatory or legal factors affecting the market are addressed — PASS: regulatory landscape covered; FAIL: regulatory blind spot
15. [ ] Technology trends impacting the market are discussed — PASS: tech trends noted; FAIL: technology dimension absent

### Section 4: Projections and Assumptions
16. [ ] Growth projections include base, optimistic, and pessimistic scenarios — PASS: 3 scenarios; FAIL: single-point forecast only
17. [ ] Key assumptions underlying projections are documented — PASS: assumptions explicit; FAIL: hidden assumptions
18. [ ] Methodology for projections is described (top-down, bottom-up, or both) — PASS: method stated; FAIL: no methodology disclosure
19. [ ] Confidence intervals or ranges are provided for key estimates — PASS: uncertainty quantified; FAIL: false precision in estimates
20. [ ] Historical data is used to validate projection methodology where available — PASS: back-testing performed; FAIL: no historical validation

### Section 5: Actionability and Presentation
21. [ ] Strategic implications are clearly stated — PASS: so-what articulated; FAIL: data without interpretation
22. [ ] Opportunities are prioritized by attractiveness and feasibility — PASS: prioritization framework applied; FAIL: flat list of opportunities
23. [ ] Visualizations effectively communicate key data points — PASS: clear charts and graphs; FAIL: dense tables where visuals would help
24. [ ] Report includes an executive summary suitable for senior stakeholders — PASS: summary present; FAIL: no summary
25. [ ] Appendix includes detailed data tables for reference — PASS: supporting data available; FAIL: no backup detail

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 25) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 92-100%: Excellent — decision-ready market intelligence
  - 80-91%: Good — minor refinements needed
  - 68-79%: Acceptable — gaps should be addressed before delivery
  - Below 68%: Insufficient — significant rework or additional research needed

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 80% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 5, 6, or 16
- **Revise**: Score of 68-79%, or any FAIL on items 1, 5, 6, or 16
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 68%, or data integrity concerns identified, or market definition fundamentally questioned
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Market research reveals that the strategic premise needs revisiting, or timeline prevents adequate data collection
