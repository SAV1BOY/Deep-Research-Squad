# Executive Brief Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Validate that executive briefs are concise, decision-ready, and appropriately distill complex research into actionable intelligence for senior leadership. Executive briefs must prioritize clarity and impact over exhaustive detail.

## When to Use
- Before delivering any executive brief to C-suite or senior decision-makers
- When condensing a deep dive report into an executive format
- During review of briefs prepared by other squad members
- When time-sensitive decisions require rapid research summarization

## Prerequisites
- Underlying research or deep dive report has been completed
- Target audience and their decision context are clearly understood
- Key findings have been validated through the evidence table process
- Stakeholder preferences for format and length have been confirmed

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Content and Framing
1. [ ] Brief opens with a clear statement of the decision or question at hand — PASS: decision context stated in first paragraph; FAIL: buried or missing
2. [ ] Bottom-line-up-front (BLUF) is present within the first 200 words — PASS: BLUF clearly stated; FAIL: conclusion deferred to end only
3. [ ] No more than 5 key findings are presented — PASS: 3-5 focused findings; FAIL: more than 5 or fewer than 2
4. [ ] Each finding includes a confidence level (high/medium/low) — PASS: all findings rated; FAIL: any finding without confidence rating
5. [ ] Recommendations are specific and actionable — PASS: concrete next steps provided; FAIL: vague or generic suggestions
6. [ ] Risks and uncertainties are explicitly called out — PASS: risk section present; FAIL: risks omitted or minimized

### Section 2: Brevity and Clarity
7. [ ] Total length does not exceed 2 pages or 1,000 words — PASS: within limit; FAIL: exceeds limit
8. [ ] Sentences average fewer than 25 words — PASS: concise prose; FAIL: overly complex sentence structures
9. [ ] No acronyms are used without definition — PASS: all acronyms defined; FAIL: undefined acronyms present
10. [ ] Technical depth is appropriate for the audience — PASS: language matches audience level; FAIL: too technical or too simplistic
11. [ ] Bullet points and numbered lists are used for key information — PASS: scannable format; FAIL: dense paragraph blocks for lists

### Section 3: Evidence and Support
12. [ ] Each key finding traces back to verified evidence — PASS: evidence trail documented; FAIL: unsupported assertions
13. [ ] Source summary is provided (number and type of sources consulted) — PASS: source overview present; FAIL: no sourcing information
14. [ ] Data points are current and relevant — PASS: data within acceptable recency; FAIL: outdated figures used
15. [ ] Conflicting viewpoints are noted where they exist — PASS: dissent acknowledged; FAIL: false consensus presented

### Section 4: Visual and Format Standards
16. [ ] Header includes date, author, classification, and version — PASS: complete header; FAIL: any field missing
17. [ ] Visual elements (if any) are self-explanatory with labels — PASS: visuals clear; FAIL: unlabeled or confusing graphics
18. [ ] Consistent formatting throughout — PASS: uniform style; FAIL: mixed formatting
19. [ ] Footer includes reference to full report if applicable — PASS: cross-reference present; FAIL: no link to underlying research

### Section 5: Decision Readiness
20. [ ] Brief clearly distinguishes between facts, analysis, and opinion — PASS: distinctions explicit; FAIL: categories blended without notice
21. [ ] Timeline or urgency for decision is stated — PASS: temporal context provided; FAIL: no urgency framing
22. [ ] Alternative options or scenarios are briefly outlined — PASS: at least 2 options presented; FAIL: single recommendation without alternatives
23. [ ] Contact information for follow-up questions is included — PASS: contact details present; FAIL: no follow-up path provided

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 23) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 95-100%: Excellent — ready for executive delivery
  - 85-94%: Good — minor polish needed
  - 75-84%: Acceptable — revisions needed before delivery
  - Below 75%: Insufficient — rewrite required

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 85% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 2, 5, or 12
- **Revise**: Score of 75-84%, or any FAIL on items 1, 2, 5, or 12
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 75%, or brief has been through 2+ revision cycles, or factual accuracy concerns raised
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Decision timeline is at risk, or brief scope does not match the decision being supported
