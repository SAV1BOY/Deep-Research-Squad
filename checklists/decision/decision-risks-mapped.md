# Decision Risks Mapped Check

## Purpose

Ensure that the risks associated with each decision option are identified,
assessed, and documented. Unmapped risks lead to uninformed decision-making
and preventable negative outcomes.

## Gate Question

**Are the risks for each option identified, assessed, and documented?**

## Prerequisites

- Decision options and trade-offs documented.
- Risk assessment framework selected (likelihood x impact matrix, etc.).
- Domain-specific risk categories reviewed.
- Historical precedents (if any) analyzed.

## Pass Criteria

1. At least 3 risks are identified per option.
2. Each risk has a likelihood assessment (high, medium, low or quantified).
3. Each risk has an impact assessment (severe, moderate, minor or
   quantified).
4. Mitigation strategies are proposed for high-likelihood or high-impact
   risks.
5. Risks that are common across all options are identified separately.
6. Risks unique to each option are highlighted as differentiators.
7. A risk matrix or register is included in the output.

## Fail Actions

- If fewer than 3 risks per option: brainstorm additional risks including
  edge cases and tail events.
- If likelihood or impact is unassessed: apply the assessment framework.
- If mitigations are missing for top risks: propose at least one per
  high-priority risk.
- If common vs. unique risks are not distinguished: categorize them.
- If risk register is absent: create one.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate if risk assessment reveals a showstopper risk that was not
  previously known.
- Escalate if the risk landscape is so uncertain that meaningful
  assessment is not possible.
- Escalate if risk mitigation requires commitments beyond the requester's
  current authority or resources.
