# Trend Analysis Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure that trend analysis deliverables are grounded in robust data, distinguish between signals and noise, and provide actionable foresight for strategic planning. This checklist validates that trend identification, trajectory assessment, and implication analysis meet the standards required for reliable forward-looking intelligence.

## When to Use
- Before delivering trend reports, forecasts, or foresight analyses
- When reviewing macro-trend, industry-trend, or technology-trend research
- During assessment of trend inputs for scenario planning or strategy
- When trend analysis will inform resource allocation or investment decisions

## Prerequisites
- Time series data or longitudinal observations are available
- Scope of trend analysis (industry, geography, technology, time horizon) is defined
- Baseline or historical context has been established
- Analytical tools and frameworks for trend identification are accessible
- Stakeholder expectations for trend granularity and horizon are understood

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Data Foundation
1. [ ] Time series data spans a sufficient period to identify meaningful patterns — PASS: adequate historical depth; FAIL: insufficient data window
2. [ ] Data sources are diverse (not reliant on a single data stream) — PASS: 3+ data sources; FAIL: single-source reliance
3. [ ] Data quality has been assessed and issues documented — PASS: quality review completed; FAIL: raw data used without assessment
4. [ ] Data gaps or discontinuities are identified and addressed — PASS: gaps noted with treatment approach; FAIL: gaps ignored
5. [ ] Units, definitions, and measurement methods are consistent across the data set — PASS: consistency verified; FAIL: methodological inconsistencies

### Section 2: Trend Identification
6. [ ] Trends are distinguished from cyclical patterns and one-time events — PASS: clear differentiation; FAIL: cycles or anomalies labeled as trends
7. [ ] Trend direction (accelerating, decelerating, stable, emerging, declining) is stated — PASS: trajectory characterized; FAIL: direction not assessed
8. [ ] Trend maturity is assessed (nascent, growing, mature, declining) — PASS: lifecycle stage identified; FAIL: no maturity assessment
9. [ ] Leading indicators for each trend are identified — PASS: indicators specified; FAIL: trend observed without predictive signals
10. [ ] Weak signals and emerging trends are captured alongside established trends — PASS: early signals included; FAIL: only well-known trends analyzed

### Section 3: Analytical Rigor
11. [ ] Quantitative analysis supports trend identification where possible — PASS: statistical or quantitative basis; FAIL: purely anecdotal identification
12. [ ] Growth rates or rates of change are calculated — PASS: rates quantified; FAIL: qualitative direction only
13. [ ] Correlation vs. causation is properly distinguished — PASS: causal claims justified; FAIL: correlation presented as causation
14. [ ] Confidence levels are assigned to trend projections — PASS: uncertainty quantified; FAIL: projections without confidence framing
15. [ ] Counter-trends or forces that could reverse the trend are identified — PASS: counter-forces noted; FAIL: unidirectional analysis

### Section 4: Implications and Actionability
16. [ ] Each significant trend includes an impact assessment — PASS: impact analysis per trend; FAIL: trends without so-what analysis
17. [ ] Implications are specific to the stakeholder's context — PASS: contextualized implications; FAIL: generic implications
18. [ ] Time horizon for trend impact is estimated — PASS: temporal framing provided; FAIL: timeless assertions
19. [ ] Strategic opportunities arising from trends are identified — PASS: opportunities noted; FAIL: only threats analyzed or no recommendations
20. [ ] Risks or threats from adverse trends are flagged — PASS: threats identified; FAIL: Pollyanna analysis

### Section 5: Presentation and Communication
21. [ ] Visual representations of trends are included (charts, graphs, timelines) — PASS: trend visuals present; FAIL: text-only trend description
22. [ ] Trend prioritization framework is applied (impact vs. likelihood matrix or similar) — PASS: trends prioritized; FAIL: flat list of trends
23. [ ] Executive summary highlights top 3-5 trends — PASS: prioritized summary; FAIL: no summary or too many trends highlighted
24. [ ] Methodology for trend analysis is documented — PASS: methodology section present; FAIL: no methodology disclosure
25. [ ] Recommendations for ongoing trend monitoring are provided — PASS: monitoring plan included; FAIL: snapshot analysis with no update path

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 25) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 92-100%: Excellent — rigorous and actionable trend intelligence
  - 80-91%: Good — solid analysis with minor improvements needed
  - 68-79%: Acceptable — usable but should be strengthened before strategic reliance
  - Below 68%: Insufficient — trend analysis lacks the rigor for decision support

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 80% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 6, 13, or 16
- **Revise**: Score of 68-79%, or any FAIL on items 1, 6, 13, or 16
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 68%, or data integrity concerns undermine trend validity, or key trends may have been missed
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Trend analysis reveals disruptive shifts that require immediate strategic attention, or available data is insufficient to reliably project trends for the required time horizon
