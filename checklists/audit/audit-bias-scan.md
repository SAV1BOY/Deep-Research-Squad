# Audit Bias Scan

## Purpose

Systematically scan the research output for cognitive biases, analytical
biases, and source biases that may have influenced the findings. Even
rigorous research is susceptible to bias.

## Gate Question

**Has the research been scanned for cognitive and analytical biases?**

## Prerequisites

- Final synthesis document available.
- Bias checklist with common research biases prepared.
- Contrarian Agent outputs reviewed.
- Evidence collection and analysis logs available.

## Pass Criteria

1. Confirmation bias check: evidence was sought on both sides of claims.
2. Anchoring bias check: initial findings did not disproportionately
   influence later analysis.
3. Availability bias check: easily found evidence is not over-weighted.
4. Authority bias check: claims are evaluated on evidence, not just the
   authority of the source.
5. Selection bias check: the evidence sample is representative, not
   cherry-picked.
6. Framing bias check: the way the question was framed did not
   predetermine the answer.
7. Each identified bias risk is assessed for its likely impact on the
   conclusions.

## Fail Actions

- If confirmation bias detected: search for disconfirming evidence.
- If anchoring detected: re-evaluate conclusions starting from different
  anchor points.
- If availability bias detected: search for harder-to-find evidence.
- If selection bias detected: broaden the evidence sample.
- If framing bias detected: reframe the question and test if conclusions
  change.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate if multiple biases are detected pointing in the same direction,
  suggesting the conclusions may be systematically skewed.
- Escalate if bias correction would significantly change the conclusions.
- Escalate if the agents themselves may be exhibiting systematic bias
  patterns.
