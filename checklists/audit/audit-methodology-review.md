# Audit Methodology Review

## Purpose

Review the overall research methodology used in the investigation to ensure
it was appropriate, consistently applied, and sufficient for the question
asked. This is a meta-check on the research process itself.

## Gate Question

**Was the research methodology appropriate and consistently applied?**

## Prerequisites

- Research design document from Architect Agent.
- Methodology gate results available.
- All agent outputs collected.
- Research log documenting process decisions available.

## Pass Criteria

1. The methodology matches the question type and complexity.
2. The methodology was followed as designed (no undocumented deviations).
3. Deviations that occurred are documented with justification.
4. The methodology addresses the known biases in the source landscape.
5. Evidence collection was systematic, not ad hoc.
6. Analysis followed a documented procedure, not improvisation.
7. The methodology would be considered appropriate by a domain expert.

## Fail Actions

- If methodology was inappropriate: document the mismatch and assess
  impact on conclusions.
- If undocumented deviations occurred: document them retroactively and
  evaluate their effect.
- If evidence collection was ad hoc: note the limitation and its
  potential impact.
- If analysis was improvised: assess whether conclusions are still valid.
- If bias mitigation was absent: flag the specific bias risks.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate if the methodology review reveals fundamental flaws that
  could invalidate the conclusions.
- Escalate if the methodology was so far from best practice that the
  research may need to be redone.
- Escalate if methodology deviations were made without authorization
  and affected the results.
