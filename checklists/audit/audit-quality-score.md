# Audit Quality Score

## Purpose

Produce a final composite quality score for the research output based on
all audit dimensions. The quality score provides a single summary metric
that signals whether the output meets delivery standards.

## Gate Question

**What is the overall quality score, and does it meet the delivery
threshold?**

## Prerequisites

- All other audit checks completed (methodology, bias, completeness,
  reproducibility).
- Evidence and verification gate results available.
- Synthesis quality assessments completed.
- Quality scoring rubric defined with weights per dimension.

## Pass Criteria

1. Each quality dimension is scored on a 1-10 scale:
   - Methodology appropriateness
   - Evidence strength and diversity
   - Source quality and independence
   - Verification thoroughness
   - Bias mitigation
   - Completeness of coverage
   - Narrative coherence
   - Reproducibility
   - Actionability of output
   - Transparency of limitations
2. Dimension weights are applied based on the research type and stakes.
3. The composite score is calculated and documented.
4. The composite score meets the minimum threshold (>= 7/10 for standard,
   >= 8/10 for high-stakes).
5. No individual dimension scores below 4/10 (hard floor).
6. Scoring rationale is documented for each dimension.
7. Improvement recommendations are provided for any dimension below 7/10.

## Fail Actions

- If composite score is below threshold: identify the weakest dimensions
  and remediate.
- If any dimension is below 4/10: treat as a blocking issue requiring
  immediate fix.
- If scoring rationale is missing: add justifications.
- If improvement recommendations are absent for low-scoring dimensions:
  add them.
- If weights are not applied: assign and apply appropriate weights.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate if the composite score remains below threshold after one
  remediation pass.
- Escalate if the quality score reveals systemic issues across multiple
  dimensions.
- Escalate if the time required for remediation would miss the delivery
  deadline.
- Escalate if the quality score is borderline and a judgment call is
  needed on whether to deliver.
