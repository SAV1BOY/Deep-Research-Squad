# Final Delivery Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Serve as the last quality gate before any research deliverable is transmitted to the stakeholder. This checklist covers packaging, presentation, completeness, and professionalism standards that apply regardless of the specific deliverable type. It is used in conjunction with the type-specific checklist, not as a replacement.

## When to Use
- Immediately before sending any research deliverable to a stakeholder
- After all type-specific quality checks have been completed and passed
- When assembling a multi-component delivery package
- During final review before a presentation or briefing

## Prerequisites
- Type-specific quality checklist has been completed with a passing score
- All revisions from peer review have been incorporated
- Stakeholder preferences for format and delivery method are confirmed
- All team members who contributed have reviewed the final version
- Delivery timeline has been confirmed with the stakeholder

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Completeness Verification
1. [ ] All deliverables specified in the research plan are included — PASS: complete delivery package; FAIL: any promised deliverable missing
2. [ ] Executive summary or brief is included — PASS: summary present; FAIL: no summary for reports over 5 pages
3. [ ] Appendices and supporting materials referenced in the report are attached — PASS: all references satisfied; FAIL: referenced materials missing
4. [ ] Table of contents is accurate and page numbers are correct — PASS: navigation aids correct; FAIL: inaccurate ToC
5. [ ] All figures, tables, and exhibits are present and correctly numbered — PASS: all visuals accounted for; FAIL: missing or misnumbered visuals

### Section 2: Quality Standards
6. [ ] Type-specific quality checklist score meets the acceptance threshold — PASS: threshold met; FAIL: below acceptance level
7. [ ] Peer review has been completed and is documented — PASS: review on file; FAIL: no peer review performed
8. [ ] All factual claims have been verified through the evidence table — PASS: claims verified; FAIL: unverified claims present
9. [ ] Spell check and grammar review have been performed — PASS: clean copy; FAIL: errors present
10. [ ] Formatting is consistent throughout all documents in the package — PASS: uniform formatting; FAIL: formatting inconsistencies

### Section 3: Metadata and Provenance
11. [ ] Document title is clear and descriptive — PASS: informative title; FAIL: vague or missing title
12. [ ] Author names and roles are listed — PASS: attribution complete; FAIL: authors not identified
13. [ ] Date of completion and version number are displayed — PASS: dated and versioned; FAIL: undated or unversioned
14. [ ] Confidentiality classification is applied — PASS: classification marked; FAIL: unmarked document
15. [ ] Distribution list or intended audience is noted — PASS: audience specified; FAIL: no audience indication

### Section 4: Sourcing and Citations
16. [ ] All citations are complete and consistently formatted — PASS: uniform citation format; FAIL: incomplete or inconsistent citations
17. [ ] Bibliography or references section is present and comprehensive — PASS: full reference list; FAIL: missing or partial bibliography
18. [ ] All data sources are attributed — PASS: no unattributed data; FAIL: orphaned data points
19. [ ] Links and URLs have been verified as accessible — PASS: links work; FAIL: broken links present
20. [ ] Source quality ratings are available for key sources — PASS: quality context provided; FAIL: sources used without quality context

### Section 5: Delivery Readiness
21. [ ] File format matches stakeholder preference — PASS: correct format; FAIL: wrong format
22. [ ] File size is reasonable for the delivery method — PASS: within limits; FAIL: oversized files
23. [ ] File naming follows the agreed convention — PASS: proper naming; FAIL: non-standard file names
24. [ ] Delivery method and channel are confirmed — PASS: delivery method ready; FAIL: delivery logistics unclear
25. [ ] Follow-up meeting or Q&A session is scheduled if appropriate — PASS: follow-up arranged; FAIL: no follow-up plan when one is warranted
26. [ ] Cover note or delivery email is drafted — PASS: communication prepared; FAIL: no delivery communication planned
27. [ ] Stakeholder expectations for next steps are addressed — PASS: next steps outlined; FAIL: delivery with no forward context

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 27) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 95-100%: Excellent — ready for immediate delivery
  - 85-94%: Good — minor items to address but can proceed
  - 75-84%: Acceptable — must address gaps before sending
  - Below 75%: Not ready — hold delivery until gaps are closed

## Escalation
- **Accept and deliver**: Score of 85% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 6, 8, or 14
- **Hold and revise**: Score of 75-84%, or any FAIL on items 1, 6, 8, or 14
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 75%, or quality concerns that cannot be resolved within the delivery timeline
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Delivery timeline must be extended to meet quality standards, or scope of deliverable has changed from what was originally agreed
