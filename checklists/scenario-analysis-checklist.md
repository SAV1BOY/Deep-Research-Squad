# Scenario Analysis Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Validate that scenario analyses are methodologically sound, explore a sufficient range of futures, and provide decision-makers with a robust framework for strategic planning under uncertainty. This checklist ensures scenarios are plausible, internally consistent, and actionable.

## When to Use
- Before delivering scenario planning or scenario analysis deliverables
- When reviewing strategic foresight or futures research
- During quality review of contingency or risk scenario development
- When scenario analysis supports strategy formulation or stress testing

## Prerequisites
- Scope of the scenario analysis is defined (topic, time horizon, geographic scope)
- Key uncertainties and driving forces have been identified through preliminary research
- Stakeholder priorities and decision context are understood
- Historical data and trend analysis are available as inputs
- Relevant domain expertise has been consulted for scenario construction

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Scenario Design
1. [ ] At least 3 distinct scenarios are developed — PASS: 3+ scenarios; FAIL: fewer than 3
2. [ ] Scenarios span a meaningful range of outcomes (optimistic, base, pessimistic at minimum) — PASS: range coverage; FAIL: clustered around single outcome
3. [ ] Each scenario is internally consistent — PASS: no logical contradictions within scenarios; FAIL: internal inconsistencies present
4. [ ] Scenarios are plausible and grounded in observable trends — PASS: all scenarios defensible; FAIL: implausible scenarios included
5. [ ] Scenarios are mutually exclusive or their overlaps are explicitly noted — PASS: distinctions clear; FAIL: scenarios blur into each other
6. [ ] Time horizon is consistent across all scenarios — PASS: uniform horizon; FAIL: mixed time frames

### Section 2: Driving Forces and Assumptions
7. [ ] Key driving forces are identified and ranked by impact and uncertainty — PASS: forces mapped and prioritized; FAIL: driving forces not analyzed
8. [ ] At least 2 critical uncertainties form the basis for scenario differentiation — PASS: uncertainties identified; FAIL: scenarios not built on uncertainties
9. [ ] Assumptions for each scenario are explicitly documented — PASS: assumptions listed per scenario; FAIL: hidden assumptions
10. [ ] Driving forces include factors from multiple domains (economic, technological, political, social) — PASS: multi-domain analysis; FAIL: single-domain focus
11. [ ] Wild card events or black swan possibilities are considered — PASS: low-probability high-impact events noted; FAIL: only conventional outcomes explored

### Section 3: Analysis and Implications
12. [ ] Each scenario includes a narrative description of how the future unfolds — PASS: coherent narrative per scenario; FAIL: bullet points without storyline
13. [ ] Key indicators or signposts for each scenario are identified — PASS: early warning indicators defined; FAIL: no monitoring framework
14. [ ] Impact on the organization or decision is assessed for each scenario — PASS: impact analysis per scenario; FAIL: scenarios without organizational implications
15. [ ] Strategic options or responses are mapped to each scenario — PASS: response strategies outlined; FAIL: scenarios without action plans
16. [ ] Robust strategies (effective across multiple scenarios) are identified — PASS: cross-scenario strategies noted; FAIL: only scenario-specific responses

### Section 4: Quantification and Rigor
17. [ ] Probability or likelihood estimates are provided for each scenario where appropriate — PASS: likelihoods assigned; FAIL: no probability assessment
18. [ ] Key metrics are quantified for each scenario where data supports it — PASS: quantitative projections per scenario; FAIL: purely qualitative scenarios when data exists
19. [ ] Sensitivity analysis is performed on critical variables — PASS: sensitivity tested; FAIL: no sensitivity analysis
20. [ ] Historical analogies or precedents are referenced where relevant — PASS: precedents cited; FAIL: no historical grounding

### Section 5: Presentation and Usability
21. [ ] Scenario matrix or visual framework is provided — PASS: visual representation present; FAIL: text-only presentation
22. [ ] Executive summary compares scenarios and highlights key decision points — PASS: comparative summary; FAIL: no cross-scenario comparison
23. [ ] Scenarios are named memorably for easy reference — PASS: distinctive names; FAIL: generic labels (Scenario 1, 2, 3)
24. [ ] Methodology for scenario construction is documented — PASS: method described; FAIL: no methodology section
25. [ ] Limitations and blind spots of the analysis are acknowledged — PASS: limitations stated; FAIL: overconfidence in scenario coverage

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 25) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 92-100%: Excellent — robust scenario framework ready for strategic use
  - 80-91%: Good — sound analysis with minor improvements needed
  - 68-79%: Acceptable — gaps that should be addressed before strategic reliance
  - Below 68%: Insufficient — scenarios lack rigor or range

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 80% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 3, 7, or 14
- **Revise**: Score of 68-79%, or any FAIL on items 1, 3, 7, or 14
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 68%, or scenario plausibility challenged by domain experts, or critical driving forces may have been missed
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Scenario analysis reveals that the strategic question itself needs reframing, or emerging developments invalidate key assumptions before delivery
