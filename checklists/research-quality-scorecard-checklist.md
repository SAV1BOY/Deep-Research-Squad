# Research Quality Scorecard Checklist

## Purpose
Serve as the meta-checklist that evaluates the overall quality of a research engagement across all dimensions. This scorecard aggregates quality signals from individual deliverable checklists, process adherence, and stakeholder satisfaction to provide a holistic view of research quality. It is the definitive quality instrument for the Deep Research Squad.

## When to Use
- At the conclusion of every research engagement for retrospective quality assessment
- During quarterly quality reviews of squad output
- When evaluating squad member performance on research quality
- When establishing baseline quality metrics for continuous improvement
- As input to process improvement and training initiatives

## Prerequisites
- Research engagement is complete or at a major milestone
- All type-specific checklists for deliverables in the engagement have been scored
- Stakeholder feedback has been collected or solicited
- Process logs and timeline records are available
- Team retrospective has been conducted or scheduled

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Research Process Quality
1. [ ] Research plan was created and approved before execution began — PASS: plan preceded execution; FAIL: execution without approved plan
2. [ ] Research plan checklist was completed with a passing score — PASS: plan quality verified; FAIL: plan not quality-checked
3. [ ] Methodology was followed as planned or deviations were documented — PASS: adherence or documented deviation; FAIL: undocumented methodology drift
4. [ ] Quality checkpoints were conducted at planned milestones — PASS: checkpoints completed; FAIL: quality gates skipped
5. [ ] Timeline was met or deviations were communicated proactively — PASS: on time or early communication; FAIL: missed deadlines without notice

### Section 2: Evidence and Sourcing Quality
6. [ ] Evidence table was created and maintained throughout the engagement — PASS: evidence table used; FAIL: no systematic evidence tracking
7. [ ] Evidence table checklist was completed with a passing score — PASS: evidence quality verified; FAIL: evidence table not checked
8. [ ] Source quality checklist was applied to key sources — PASS: source quality assessed; FAIL: sources used without quality evaluation
9. [ ] Source diversity is adequate (mix of primary, secondary, academic, industry) — PASS: diverse source base; FAIL: homogeneous sources
10. [ ] Contradiction map was created and maintained — PASS: contradictions tracked; FAIL: no systematic contradiction analysis

### Section 3: Analytical Quality
11. [ ] Analysis demonstrates original synthesis beyond source summarization — PASS: synthesis evident; FAIL: aggregation without synthesis
12. [ ] Multiple analytical frameworks or perspectives are applied — PASS: multi-framework approach; FAIL: single-lens analysis
13. [ ] Assumptions are explicitly documented — PASS: assumptions listed; FAIL: hidden assumptions
14. [ ] Limitations and caveats are transparently disclosed — PASS: limitations stated; FAIL: overconfident presentation
15. [ ] Conclusions are proportionate to the evidence — PASS: calibrated conclusions; FAIL: conclusions exceed evidence base

### Section 4: Deliverable Quality
16. [ ] All deliverables passed their type-specific quality checklists — PASS: all checklists passed; FAIL: any deliverable below threshold
17. [ ] Average quality score across all deliverables is 80% or above — PASS: average meets standard; FAIL: average below standard
18. [ ] Peer review was conducted on all major deliverables — PASS: reviews completed; FAIL: any major deliverable unreviewed
19. [ ] Final delivery checklist was completed and passed — PASS: final gate cleared; FAIL: final checklist not completed
20. [ ] Deliverables are consistent with each other (no internal contradictions across documents) — PASS: cross-deliverable consistency; FAIL: inconsistencies between deliverables

### Section 5: Stakeholder Alignment
21. [ ] Research objectives as understood match stakeholder's original intent — PASS: alignment confirmed; FAIL: scope misalignment discovered
22. [ ] Stakeholder received deliverables in the agreed format and timeline — PASS: expectations met; FAIL: format or timeline misses
23. [ ] Stakeholder questions and feedback were addressed — PASS: responsive engagement; FAIL: unanswered questions
24. [ ] Stakeholder rates the research as useful for their purpose — PASS: positive utility feedback; FAIL: research not perceived as useful
25. [ ] Follow-up actions or monitoring recommendations were provided — PASS: forward-looking guidance; FAIL: engagement ended without next steps

### Section 6: Continuous Improvement
26. [ ] Lessons learned are documented from this engagement — PASS: retrospective completed; FAIL: no lessons captured
27. [ ] Reusable assets (templates, frameworks, source lists) are cataloged — PASS: assets preserved; FAIL: one-time-use artifacts
28. [ ] Quality improvement actions from previous engagements were applied — PASS: prior lessons incorporated; FAIL: known issues repeated
29. [ ] Training or skill gaps identified during the engagement are noted — PASS: development needs recorded; FAIL: capability gaps unacknowledged
30. [ ] Overall engagement quality score is calculated and recorded in the quality log — PASS: score recorded; FAIL: no quality record maintained

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 30) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 93-100%: Excellent — exemplary research quality, candidate for best practice case study
  - 83-92%: Good — strong quality with minor areas for improvement
  - 73-82%: Acceptable — meets minimum standards but improvement needed
  - 63-72%: Below standard — significant quality gaps requiring remediation
  - Below 63%: Insufficient — systemic quality failure requiring intervention

## Escalation
- **Accept and archive**: Score of 83% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 16, 21, or 24
- **Improvement plan**: Score of 73-82%, or any FAIL on items 1, 16, 21, or 24 — document specific improvements for next engagement
- **Escalate to lead**: Score of 63-72%, or pattern of recurring quality failures across engagements, or stakeholder dissatisfaction
- **Escalate to management**: Score below 63%, or systemic process failures identified, or quality trends declining across multiple engagements — requires structural intervention in squad processes, training, or resourcing
