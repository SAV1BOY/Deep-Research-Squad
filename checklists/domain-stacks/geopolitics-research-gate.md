# Geopolitics Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that geopolitics research meets domain-specific quality
standards before delivery. Geopolitical research informs strategic decisions
about market entry, supply chain risk, international partnerships, and
regulatory exposure. This domain is uniquely susceptible to bias, narrative
framing, and rapid change. This gate enforces multi-perspective analysis
and source rigor appropriate for geopolitical assessments.

## Gate Question

**Does this geopolitics research meet quality standards?**

Geopolitics research must demonstrate multi-perspective analysis, awareness
of information warfare and propaganda risks, temporal sensitivity, and
scenario-based reasoning grounded in verifiable facts rather than ideology.

## Pass Criteria

1. Multiple national and regional perspectives are represented in the analysis.
2. Sources span diverse geopolitical alignments to prevent single-narrative bias.
3. State-affiliated media and government sources are identified and labeled as such.
4. Propaganda and information operations risks are assessed for the topic area.
5. Historical context is provided to situate current developments accurately.
6. Power dynamics between relevant actors are mapped and analyzed.
7. Economic, military, diplomatic, and cultural dimensions are all considered.
8. Scenario analysis presents multiple plausible future trajectories.
9. Alliance structures and treaty obligations are factored into analysis.
10. Domestic political dynamics that affect international behavior are addressed.
11. Timeline sensitivity is assessed with clear indication of how fast the situation may change.
12. The analysis explicitly states which developments could invalidate conclusions.
13. Non-state actors and transnational forces are included where relevant.

## Fail Actions

- If analysis presents only one national perspective, seek and incorporate alternatives.
- If state-affiliated sources are unlabeled, add provenance and affiliation metadata.
- If propaganda risk is unassessed, evaluate information environment for the topic area.
- If historical context is missing, add relevant precedents and background.
- If scenario analysis is absent, develop at minimum three plausible trajectories.
- If only one dimension (e.g., military) is analyzed, broaden to include other factors.
- If domestic political dynamics are ignored, assess their potential international impact.
- If invalidation conditions are unstated, identify key developments that would change conclusions.
- Do not deliver geopolitics research until domain-specific standards are met.
