# Business Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that business research meets domain-specific quality
standards before delivery. Business research informs strategic decisions
with significant financial and operational consequences. Errors in market
sizing, competitive analysis, or business model evaluation can lead to
costly missteps. This gate enforces the rigor required for business
research to be decision-grade.

## Gate Question

**Does this business research meet quality standards?**

Business research must demonstrate accurate market analysis, validated
competitive intelligence, sound financial reasoning, and practical
strategic insight grounded in verifiable evidence.

## Pass Criteria

1. Market sizing uses bottom-up and top-down approaches with methodology disclosed.
2. Competitive analysis covers direct, indirect, and potential future competitors.
3. Industry data is sourced from recognized providers and cross-referenced.
4. Business model analysis addresses revenue, cost structure, and unit economics.
5. Customer segment analysis is based on data, not assumptions alone.
6. Regulatory and compliance factors relevant to the business context are included.
7. Financial projections include assumptions, sensitivity analysis, and scenario ranges.
8. Competitive moats and vulnerabilities are assessed with supporting evidence.
9. Go-to-market considerations are practical and informed by market evidence.
10. Stakeholder perspectives (customers, partners, regulators) are represented.
11. Historical precedents and comparable situations are referenced where applicable.
12. The research accounts for macroeconomic factors that may affect conclusions.
13. All proprietary or confidential data handling complies with agreed protocols.

## Fail Actions

- If market sizing uses only one approach, add the complementary method.
- If competitive analysis is incomplete, expand the competitor set and reassess.
- If industry data sources are unverified, validate against alternative providers.
- If financial reasoning lacks sensitivity analysis, add scenario modeling.
- If customer segments are assumption-based, seek data validation or flag the limitation.
- If regulatory factors are missing, conduct a regulatory scan for the relevant jurisdictions.
- If macroeconomic context is absent, add relevant economic indicators and trends.
- Document all quality gaps and remediation steps in the research log.
- Do not deliver business research until domain-specific standards are met.
