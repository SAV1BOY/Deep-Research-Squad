# Finance Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that finance research meets domain-specific quality
standards before delivery. Financial research directly informs investment
decisions, risk management, and capital allocation with significant monetary
consequences. Errors in financial analysis can result in material losses,
regulatory violations, or fiduciary breaches. This gate enforces the
precision and rigor required for finance-grade research.

## Gate Question

**Does this finance research meet quality standards?**

Finance research must demonstrate accurate data sourcing, sound analytical
methodology, appropriate risk assessment, regulatory awareness, and
transparent assumptions in all financial models and projections.

## Pass Criteria

1. Financial data is sourced from authoritative providers (exchanges, regulators, audited filings).
2. All financial models disclose their assumptions, inputs, and limitations explicitly.
3. Valuation methodologies are appropriate for the asset class and context.
4. Multiple valuation approaches are used and cross-referenced where applicable.
5. Risk factors are systematically identified and quantified where possible.
6. Sensitivity analysis shows how conclusions change under different assumptions.
7. Regulatory and compliance requirements relevant to the financial context are addressed.
8. Historical performance data includes appropriate time horizons and market contexts.
9. Forward-looking projections are clearly distinguished from historical data.
10. Currency, inflation, and discount rate assumptions are explicit and justified.
11. Counterparty and systemic risks are evaluated where relevant.
12. The research includes appropriate disclaimers about the nature of financial projections.
13. Tax implications and structural considerations are noted where material.

## Fail Actions

- If financial data sources are unverified, cross-check against regulatory filings or exchanges.
- If models lack disclosed assumptions, document all inputs and their justifications.
- If only one valuation method is used, add complementary approaches for triangulation.
- If risk factors are unquantified, provide at minimum qualitative risk assessments.
- If sensitivity analysis is missing, model at least three scenarios (base, upside, downside).
- If regulatory considerations are absent, identify relevant financial regulations.
- If forward-looking and historical data are conflated, separate them clearly.
- If disclaimers are missing, add appropriate financial research disclaimers.
- Do not deliver finance research until domain-specific standards are met.
