# AI Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that AI research meets domain-specific quality standards
before delivery. AI research informs technology strategy, model selection,
deployment decisions, and risk management in a rapidly evolving field.
The AI domain is characterized by hype cycles, benchmark gaming, and fast
obsolescence of findings. This gate enforces critical evaluation of AI
claims and practical grounding of AI research outputs.

## Gate Question

**Does this AI research meet quality standards?**

AI research must demonstrate critical evaluation of capability claims,
understanding of technical limitations, awareness of ethical and safety
considerations, and practical deployment realism grounded in reproducible
evidence rather than marketing narratives.

## Pass Criteria

1. Model capability claims are verified against independent benchmarks, not just vendor claims.
2. Benchmark results are contextualized with known limitations of the benchmarks used.
3. Training data characteristics and potential biases are documented where known.
4. Reproducibility of cited results is assessed and disclosed.
5. Computational requirements and infrastructure costs are included in evaluations.
6. The distinction between research results and production-ready capabilities is maintained.
7. Ethical considerations including bias, fairness, and societal impact are addressed.
8. Safety and alignment considerations are evaluated for the relevant use case.
9. Regulatory landscape for AI in the relevant jurisdiction is included.
10. The research accounts for the rapid pace of change with clear dating of all claims.
11. Open-source versus proprietary trade-offs are analyzed where applicable.
12. Integration complexity and operational requirements for deployment are assessed.
13. Comparison between AI approaches and non-AI alternatives is included where relevant.

## Fail Actions

- If capability claims rely solely on vendor benchmarks, seek independent evaluation.
- If benchmark limitations are unstated, add context about what benchmarks measure and miss.
- If training data biases are unexamined, flag the limitation on downstream conclusions.
- If research results are conflated with production capabilities, separate the two clearly.
- If ethical considerations are absent, conduct an AI ethics review for the use case.
- If safety implications are unaddressed, assess risks appropriate to the deployment context.
- If the regulatory landscape is missing, map relevant AI governance frameworks.
- If dating of claims is absent, add dates to all capability and benchmark references.
- Do not deliver AI research until domain-specific standards are met.
