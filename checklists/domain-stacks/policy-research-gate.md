# Policy Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that policy research meets domain-specific quality
standards before delivery. Policy research informs regulatory strategy,
compliance planning, government affairs, and public advocacy. Policy
analysis requires understanding of legislative processes, stakeholder
dynamics, and implementation realities. This gate enforces the depth
and objectivity needed for policy research to be actionable.

## Gate Question

**Does this policy research meet quality standards?**

Policy research must demonstrate thorough legislative and regulatory
analysis, stakeholder mapping, implementation feasibility assessment,
and balanced representation of policy perspectives grounded in
authoritative sources.

## Pass Criteria

1. The regulatory and legislative landscape is mapped with current status of relevant actions.
2. Jurisdictional scope is clearly defined including which authorities have oversight.
3. Key stakeholders and their positions are identified and analyzed.
4. The policy development timeline and decision points are documented.
5. Implementation mechanisms and enforcement approaches are evaluated.
6. Precedent analysis examines similar policies in other jurisdictions or time periods.
7. Impact analysis covers economic, social, and operational dimensions.
8. Compliance requirements are stated in actionable terms for the intended audience.
9. Political dynamics and coalition structures are assessed for their policy influence.
10. Public comment periods, lobbying disclosures, and advocacy positions are referenced.
11. Unintended consequences and second-order effects are considered.
12. The research distinguishes between enacted policy, proposed policy, and policy advocacy.
13. Regulatory risk scenarios are developed for strategic planning purposes.

## Fail Actions

- If the regulatory landscape is incomplete, map all relevant authorities and actions.
- If jurisdictional scope is unclear, define which levels of government are involved.
- If stakeholder analysis is missing, identify key actors and their influence.
- If precedent analysis is absent, research comparable policy actions for reference.
- If impact analysis covers only one dimension, broaden to economic, social, and operational.
- If compliance requirements are vague, translate into specific actionable obligations.
- If political dynamics are unaddressed, assess how political factors may affect outcomes.
- If enacted and proposed policies are conflated, separate and label them clearly.
- Do not deliver policy research until domain-specific standards are met.
