# Crypto/Blockchain Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that crypto and blockchain research meets domain-specific
quality standards before delivery. This domain is characterized by extreme
volatility, information asymmetry, pervasive conflicts of interest, and
rapidly evolving regulatory landscapes. Research in this space directly
informs investment, technology, and compliance decisions with significant
risk exposure. This gate enforces skeptical rigor and source discipline
appropriate for the unique challenges of crypto and blockchain research.

## Gate Question

**Does this crypto/blockchain research meet quality standards?**

Crypto and blockchain research must demonstrate technical accuracy,
regulatory awareness across jurisdictions, critical evaluation of
tokenomics and project viability, and rigorous source vetting given
the prevalence of promotional content and market manipulation.

## Pass Criteria

1. Technical claims about protocols are verified against source code, whitepapers, or audits.
2. On-chain data analysis uses verified blockchain explorers and analytics platforms.
3. Tokenomics analysis covers supply mechanics, distribution, vesting, and incentive alignment.
4. Smart contract audit status and known vulnerabilities are documented.
5. Team and governance structure transparency is assessed and disclosed.
6. Regulatory status is evaluated across all relevant jurisdictions.
7. Sources are vetted for conflicts of interest including token holdings and advisory roles.
8. Market data is sourced from reputable aggregators with wash trading filters applied.
9. The distinction between decentralized claims and actual governance structure is evaluated.
10. Historical incident analysis covers hacks, exploits, and governance failures.
11. Interoperability and ecosystem dependency risks are assessed.
12. Environmental and energy consumption considerations are addressed where relevant.
13. The research distinguishes between established projects and speculative or early-stage ones.
14. Appropriate risk disclaimers are included given the volatility of the domain.

## Fail Actions

- If technical claims are unverified against source code, flag as unverified or verify directly.
- If tokenomics analysis is incomplete, map the full token economic model before concluding.
- If audit status is unknown, investigate and disclose the gap as a risk factor.
- If source conflicts of interest are unchecked, vet all sources for financial interests.
- If market data may include wash trading, seek filtered data or flag the limitation.
- If regulatory status is only checked in one jurisdiction, expand to all relevant regions.
- If governance decentralization claims are unverified, analyze actual control structures.
- If risk disclaimers are absent, add them prominently given domain volatility.
- Do not deliver crypto/blockchain research until domain-specific standards are met.
