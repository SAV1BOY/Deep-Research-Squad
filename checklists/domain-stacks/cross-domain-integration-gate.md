# Cross-Domain Integration Gate

## Purpose

Ensure that research spanning multiple domains (e.g., technical, financial,
regulatory, geopolitical) is properly integrated rather than siloed. Poor
cross-domain integration produces fragmented analysis that misses
interactions between domains and delivers incomplete insights.

## When Triggered

- When findings from two or more domain-specific research tracks are
  combined.
- Before multi-domain synthesis is finalized for delivery.
- When a domain-specific finding has implications for another domain.

## Prerequisites

- Domain-specific research tracks have individually passed their quality gates.
- Domain experts or leads for each contributing domain are identified.
- A shared vocabulary or glossary exists for cross-domain communication.

## Checklist

- [ ] Findings from each domain are mapped to a common framework or structure.
- [ ] Cross-domain interactions and dependencies are explicitly identified.
- [ ] Domain-specific jargon is translated or defined for cross-domain audiences.
- [ ] Contradictions between domain findings are surfaced and resolved or documented.
- [ ] Assumptions valid in one domain are checked for validity in adjacent domains.
- [ ] Temporal alignment is verified: domain findings refer to the same time periods.
- [ ] Data from different domains uses compatible units, definitions, and scales.
- [ ] Second-order effects across domains are considered (e.g., regulatory impact on market).
- [ ] Each domain's confidence levels are preserved in the integrated output.
- [ ] The integrated analysis adds insight beyond what individual domains provide alone.
- [ ] Domain experts have reviewed how their findings are represented in the integration.
- [ ] Gaps where domains do not overlap are identified and assessed for risk.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

**Pass**: All domain findings are mapped to a common framework, cross-domain
interactions are identified, contradictions are resolved, and the integrated
output provides insights beyond individual domains.

**Fail**: Domain findings remain siloed, cross-domain interactions are missed,
or integration introduces misrepresentation of domain-specific findings.

## Escalation if Failed

- Convene a cross-domain review session with leads from each contributing
  domain.
- If domain contradictions cannot be resolved, present them explicitly with
  per-domain confidence ratings.
- Escalate to the Architect if integration failures reveal gaps in the
  original research design.
- Escalate to the Chief if the integrated output cannot meet delivery
  standards.
