# Marketing Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that marketing research meets domain-specific quality
standards before delivery. Marketing research informs brand positioning,
campaign strategy, audience targeting, and budget allocation decisions.
Flawed marketing research leads to misallocated budgets, missed audiences,
and ineffective campaigns. This gate enforces evidence-based rigor in
marketing research outputs.

## Gate Question

**Does this marketing research meet quality standards?**

Marketing research must demonstrate validated audience insights, data-driven
channel analysis, competitive positioning awareness, and measurable
recommendations grounded in market evidence rather than assumptions.

## Pass Criteria

1. Target audience definitions are based on data-driven segmentation, not assumptions.
2. Market research methodology is disclosed including sample sizes and selection criteria.
3. Consumer behavior insights are sourced from multiple data types (surveys, analytics, studies).
4. Competitive positioning analysis maps the landscape with consistent evaluation criteria.
5. Channel effectiveness assessments include performance data and attribution methodology.
6. Brand perception data is current and sourced from representative populations.
7. Campaign benchmarks use industry-appropriate metrics with context for interpretation.
8. Seasonal, cultural, and regional factors are accounted for in recommendations.
9. Budget recommendations are tied to expected outcomes with defined success metrics.
10. Content and messaging recommendations are grounded in audience research.
11. Digital analytics data is interpreted with awareness of tracking limitations.
12. Trend analysis distinguishes between emerging trends and established patterns.
13. Marketing recommendations include measurable KPIs for evaluation.

## Fail Actions

- If audience segmentation is assumption-based, seek data validation or flag the limitation.
- If methodology is undisclosed, document the research approach and its limitations.
- If competitive analysis is incomplete, expand the competitive set.
- If channel assessments lack performance data, source available benchmarks or flag gaps.
- If brand perception data is outdated, note the limitation and recommend fresh research.
- If budget recommendations lack outcome projections, add expected ROI ranges.
- If KPIs are undefined, establish measurable success criteria for each recommendation.
- If trend analysis conflates emerging and established patterns, distinguish them clearly.
- Do not deliver marketing research until domain-specific standards are met.
