# Research Plan Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Validate that research plans are comprehensive, feasible, and aligned with stakeholder needs before research execution begins. A well-constructed research plan prevents scope creep, ensures efficient resource use, and sets clear expectations for deliverables and timelines.

## When to Use
- Before approving any research plan for execution
- When reviewing research proposals from squad members
- During kickoff meetings to validate plan completeness
- When a research engagement requires formal planning approval

## Prerequisites
- Research request or brief has been received from the stakeholder
- Initial scoping conversation has been conducted
- Available resources (time, team, tools, budget) are understood
- Organizational context and strategic relevance are clear
- Prior research on the topic has been reviewed if available

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Objective and Scope
1. [ ] Research objective is stated as a clear, answerable question or set of questions — PASS: specific questions; FAIL: vague objectives
2. [ ] Scope is bounded with explicit inclusions and exclusions — PASS: boundaries defined; FAIL: open-ended scope
3. [ ] Deliverable type and format are specified — PASS: deliverable defined; FAIL: output undefined
4. [ ] Target audience for the research output is identified — PASS: audience specified; FAIL: audience unclear
5. [ ] Success criteria for the research are defined — PASS: criteria established; FAIL: no definition of done

### Section 2: Methodology
6. [ ] Research methodology is described (qualitative, quantitative, mixed) — PASS: method specified; FAIL: no methodology
7. [ ] Data sources and collection methods are identified — PASS: sources listed; FAIL: vague source plans
8. [ ] Analytical framework or approach is outlined — PASS: analysis method described; FAIL: no analysis approach
9. [ ] Sampling strategy is defined where applicable — PASS: sampling approach stated; FAIL: ad hoc sampling
10. [ ] Quality assurance steps are built into the plan — PASS: QA checkpoints defined; FAIL: no quality gates

### Section 3: Timeline and Milestones
11. [ ] Overall timeline is realistic given scope and resources — PASS: feasible timeline; FAIL: unrealistic deadlines
12. [ ] Key milestones and checkpoints are defined — PASS: milestones with dates; FAIL: no intermediate milestones
13. [ ] Dependencies between tasks are identified — PASS: dependencies mapped; FAIL: tasks treated as independent when they are not
14. [ ] Buffer time is included for unexpected findings or delays — PASS: contingency built in; FAIL: zero-slack plan
15. [ ] Stakeholder review points are scheduled — PASS: review meetings planned; FAIL: no stakeholder touchpoints

### Section 4: Resources and Constraints
16. [ ] Team roles and responsibilities are assigned — PASS: clear ownership; FAIL: unassigned tasks
17. [ ] Required tools, databases, and access are listed — PASS: resource requirements documented; FAIL: access needs not considered
18. [ ] Budget or cost implications are noted if applicable — PASS: costs estimated; FAIL: cost-blind planning
19. [ ] Known constraints are documented (time, access, data availability) — PASS: constraints listed; FAIL: constraints not acknowledged
20. [ ] Ethical considerations and guidelines are addressed — PASS: ethics reviewed; FAIL: ethical dimensions not considered

### Section 5: Risk and Contingency
21. [ ] Risks to plan execution are identified — PASS: risk inventory present; FAIL: no risk assessment
22. [ ] Contingency plans for major risks are outlined — PASS: fallback approaches defined; FAIL: no contingency planning
23. [ ] Criteria for plan revision or scope change are established — PASS: change management process defined; FAIL: no scope change protocol
24. [ ] Communication plan for status updates is defined — PASS: reporting cadence set; FAIL: no communication plan
25. [ ] Exit criteria or conditions for early termination are noted — PASS: stop conditions defined; FAIL: no criteria for stopping unproductive research

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 25) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 92-100%: Excellent — well-structured plan ready for execution
  - 80-91%: Good — minor refinements before execution
  - 68-79%: Acceptable — gaps that should be addressed before starting
  - Below 68%: Insufficient — plan needs significant development

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 80% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 2, 6, or 11
- **Revise**: Score of 68-79%, or any FAIL on items 1, 2, 6, or 11
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 68%, or plan feasibility questioned by team members, or resource requirements exceed availability
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Scope cannot be adequately addressed within given constraints, or research question needs refinement based on preliminary scoping
