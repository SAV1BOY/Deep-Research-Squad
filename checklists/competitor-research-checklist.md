# Competitor Research Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Validate that competitor research deliverables provide comprehensive, accurate, and strategically useful intelligence about competitive dynamics. This checklist ensures that competitor profiles, battlecards, and competitive landscape analyses meet the standards required for strategic and tactical decision-making.

## When to Use
- Before delivering competitor profiles, battlecards, or competitive analyses
- When reviewing competitive intelligence gathered for strategic planning
- During periodic refresh of existing competitor research
- When competitor research feeds into positioning, pricing, or product strategy decisions

## Prerequisites
- Competitor set has been defined and validated with stakeholders
- Competitive dimensions for analysis have been agreed upon
- Ethical guidelines for competitive intelligence gathering have been reviewed
- Access to relevant industry databases, financial filings, and news sources is confirmed
- Previous competitor research (if any) has been reviewed for baseline context

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Competitor Identification and Scope
1. [ ] Direct competitors are identified and categorized — PASS: categorized list with rationale; FAIL: incomplete or unjustified list
2. [ ] Indirect and potential future competitors are considered — PASS: adjacent threats noted; FAIL: only direct competitors analyzed
3. [ ] Competitor selection criteria are documented — PASS: criteria explicit; FAIL: arbitrary selection
4. [ ] Market share estimates are provided where data is available — PASS: share data with sources; FAIL: no market positioning data

### Section 2: Profile Depth and Accuracy
5. [ ] Each competitor profile includes company overview, size, and funding — PASS: basic profile complete; FAIL: key company data missing
6. [ ] Product and service offerings are documented with feature-level detail — PASS: detailed offering map; FAIL: superficial product listing
7. [ ] Pricing model and pricing data are captured where available — PASS: pricing intelligence gathered; FAIL: pricing not addressed
8. [ ] Go-to-market strategy and target customer segments are analyzed — PASS: GTM analysis present; FAIL: no channel or segment analysis
9. [ ] Technology stack and technical capabilities are assessed — PASS: technical evaluation included; FAIL: technology dimension missing
10. [ ] Financial performance indicators are reported where publicly available — PASS: financial data included; FAIL: financials ignored for public companies

### Section 3: Strategic Analysis
11. [ ] SWOT or equivalent framework is applied to each key competitor — PASS: structured analysis per competitor; FAIL: no strategic framework applied
12. [ ] Competitive advantages and differentiators are identified — PASS: differentiators mapped; FAIL: no differentiation analysis
13. [ ] Competitive vulnerabilities and weaknesses are documented — PASS: weaknesses identified; FAIL: only strengths captured
14. [ ] Strategic direction and recent moves are analyzed — PASS: trend and trajectory analysis; FAIL: static snapshot only
15. [ ] Partnerships, alliances, and ecosystem relationships are mapped — PASS: ecosystem view provided; FAIL: competitors analyzed in isolation

### Section 4: Sourcing and Reliability
16. [ ] All competitive claims are sourced and verifiable — PASS: sourced claims; FAIL: unsourced assertions
17. [ ] Source types include at least 3 categories (filings, press, product, interviews, etc.) — PASS: diverse sources; FAIL: single source type
18. [ ] Information recency is noted for each data point — PASS: dates provided; FAIL: undated information
19. [ ] Confidence level is assigned to key intelligence items — PASS: confidence ratings present; FAIL: no reliability assessment
20. [ ] Ethical boundaries for intelligence gathering have been respected — PASS: ethical compliance confirmed; FAIL: methods of questionable ethics used

### Section 5: Actionability and Presentation
21. [ ] Comparative matrix or table enables side-by-side evaluation — PASS: comparison framework provided; FAIL: narrative-only format
22. [ ] Implications for our strategy or positioning are stated — PASS: strategic implications drawn; FAIL: data without interpretation
23. [ ] Recommended responses or counter-strategies are proposed — PASS: actionable recommendations; FAIL: no recommended actions
24. [ ] Update frequency and monitoring plan are defined — PASS: refresh cadence set; FAIL: one-time analysis with no update plan
25. [ ] Executive summary highlights the 3-5 most critical competitive insights — PASS: summary with prioritized insights; FAIL: no prioritization

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 25) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 92-100%: Excellent — comprehensive competitive intelligence
  - 80-91%: Good — solid analysis with minor gaps
  - 68-79%: Acceptable — gaps that should be filled before strategic use
  - Below 68%: Insufficient — significant intelligence gaps

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 80% or above with no FAIL on items 1, 6, 16, or 20
- **Revise**: Score of 68-79%, or any FAIL on items 1, 6, 16, or 20
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 68%, or accuracy of key competitive claims challenged, or ethical concerns about intelligence gathering methods
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Competitive landscape has shifted significantly from initial assumptions, or research reveals urgent competitive threats requiring immediate attention
