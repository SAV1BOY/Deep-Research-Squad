# Statistical Validity Gate

## Purpose

Verify that statistical methods, claims, and data interpretations used in the
research are methodologically sound. Flawed statistics propagate false
confidence and can invalidate entire research conclusions.

## When Triggered

- When quantitative claims or statistical analyses are included in findings.
- Before data-driven conclusions are passed to synthesis.
- When a source's statistical methodology is questioned.

## Prerequisites

- Raw data or summary statistics available for review.
- Statistical methods identified and documented.
- Context for the data (population, sample, time period) established.

## Checklist

- [ ] Sample sizes are reported and adequate for the claims being made.
- [ ] Confidence intervals or margins of error accompany point estimates.
- [ ] Statistical tests used are appropriate for the data type and distribution.
- [ ] P-values, if reported, are interpreted correctly and not treated as effect sizes.
- [ ] Base rates are established before interpreting relative changes or percentages.
- [ ] Survivorship bias, selection bias, and sampling bias are assessed and mitigated.
- [ ] Correlations are not presented as causation without additional causal evidence.
- [ ] Outliers are identified and their impact on results is documented.
- [ ] Data transformations (normalization, aggregation) are disclosed and justified.
- [ ] Comparisons use consistent units, time periods, and population definitions.
- [ ] Sources of uncertainty are quantified and reported alongside results.
- [ ] Results have been checked for replication or consistency with independent data.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

**Pass**: All statistical claims include method disclosure, appropriate
uncertainty measures, and are free from identified biases or fallacies.

**Fail**: Any statistical claim lacks uncertainty measures, uses inappropriate
methods, or contains unaddressed bias.

## Escalation if Failed

- Return to the Data squad for methodological correction.
- If the original source's statistics cannot be validated, flag the source
  with a reduced trust rating.
- Escalate to the Architect if flawed statistics affect the research design.
- Consider engaging a domain statistician for complex methodological disputes.
