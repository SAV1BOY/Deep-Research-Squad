# Deep Dive Report Quality Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure that deep dive reports meet the required depth, rigor, and completeness standards before delivery to stakeholders. This checklist serves as the primary quality gate for the most comprehensive research deliverable type.

## When to Use
- Before finalizing any deep dive report for delivery
- During peer review of deep dive report drafts
- When a report has been returned for revision and is being resubmitted
- As a self-assessment tool during report authoring

## Prerequisites
- Research plan has been approved and followed
- All primary and secondary sources have been collected and cataloged
- Evidence table for the topic has been completed and reviewed
- Source quality assessment has been performed on all cited materials
- Subject matter consultation has occurred where applicable

## Checklist Items

### Section 1: Structure and Completeness
1. [ ] Report contains a clear executive summary of no more than two pages — PASS: summary present and concise; FAIL: missing or exceeds length
2. [ ] Research question or objective is explicitly stated in the introduction — PASS: unambiguous statement present; FAIL: vague or missing
3. [ ] Methodology section describes data collection and analysis approach — PASS: reproducible description; FAIL: missing or insufficient detail
4. [ ] All sections outlined in the research plan are present in the report — PASS: complete coverage; FAIL: any planned section missing
5. [ ] Report includes a conclusion section with actionable findings — PASS: clear conclusions tied to evidence; FAIL: speculative or missing

### Section 2: Evidence and Sourcing
6. [ ] Minimum of 15 distinct sources are cited — PASS: 15+ sources; FAIL: fewer than 15
7. [ ] All factual claims are supported by at least one citation — PASS: no unsupported claims; FAIL: any unsupported factual assertion
8. [ ] Primary sources constitute at least 40% of total citations — PASS: ratio met; FAIL: ratio below threshold
9. [ ] Source recency is appropriate — PASS: 70%+ sources from last 3 years unless historical; FAIL: outdated sources dominate
10. [ ] Contradictory evidence is acknowledged and addressed — PASS: contradictions noted and discussed; FAIL: contradictions ignored

### Section 3: Analysis Quality
11. [ ] Analysis goes beyond summarization to provide original synthesis — PASS: novel insights present; FAIL: mere aggregation of sources
12. [ ] Multiple perspectives or frameworks are applied to the topic — PASS: 2+ analytical lenses used; FAIL: single-perspective analysis
13. [ ] Limitations and caveats are explicitly stated — PASS: limitations section present; FAIL: no acknowledgment of limitations
14. [ ] Assumptions are documented and justified — PASS: assumptions listed; FAIL: unstated assumptions detected
15. [ ] Quantitative data is presented with appropriate context and units — PASS: all data contextualized; FAIL: raw numbers without context

### Section 4: Clarity and Presentation
16. [ ] Writing is free of jargon or jargon is defined on first use — PASS: accessible language; FAIL: undefined technical terms
17. [ ] Visual aids (tables, charts) are used where they improve comprehension — PASS: appropriate visuals included; FAIL: dense text where visuals would help
18. [ ] Report length is between 3,000 and 10,000 words — PASS: within range; FAIL: outside range without justification
19. [ ] Formatting is consistent throughout (headings, fonts, citation style) — PASS: uniform formatting; FAIL: inconsistencies present
20. [ ] Spelling and grammar have been checked — PASS: no errors; FAIL: errors present

### Section 5: Deliverable Readiness
21. [ ] All appendices and supplementary materials are attached — PASS: complete package; FAIL: referenced materials missing
22. [ ] Document metadata (date, author, version) is accurate — PASS: metadata correct; FAIL: metadata missing or incorrect
23. [ ] Confidentiality and sensitivity markings are applied — PASS: appropriate markings; FAIL: unmarked sensitive content
24. [ ] Peer review has been completed and feedback incorporated — PASS: review documented; FAIL: no review performed

## Scoring
- Count the total number of items marked PASS
- Quality Score = (PASS count / 24) x 100
- Grade thresholds:
  - 95-100%: Excellent — ready for delivery
  - 85-94%: Good — minor revisions recommended
  - 70-84%: Acceptable — revisions required before delivery
  - Below 70%: Insufficient — significant rework needed

## Escalation
- **Accept**: Score of 85% or above with no FAIL on items 2, 6, 7, or 10
- **Revise**: Score of 70-84% or any FAIL on items 2, 6, 7, or 10
- **Escalate to lead**: Score below 70%, or three consecutive revision cycles without reaching 85%, or disagreement between author and reviewer on any critical item
- **Escalate to stakeholder**: Timeline at risk due to quality issues, or scope of report found to be misaligned with original request
