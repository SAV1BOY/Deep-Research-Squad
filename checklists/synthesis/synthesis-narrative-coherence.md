# Synthesis Narrative Coherence Check

## Purpose

Ensure the synthesized output tells a coherent, logical story that flows
from evidence to conclusions. Incoherent narratives confuse readers and
undermine the credibility of well-collected evidence.

## Gate Question

**Is the synthesis narrative coherent, logical, and well-structured?**

## Prerequisites

- All layer outputs integrated.
- Draft synthesis document produced.
- Target audience identified (technical, executive, general).
- Output format and structure agreed upon.

## Pass Criteria

1. The narrative follows a logical structure (problem, findings, analysis,
   conclusions, recommendations).
2. Transitions between sections are smooth and logical connections are
   explicit.
3. Each paragraph or section advances the argument (no repetition or
   tangents).
4. Technical depth matches the target audience.
5. Key findings are highlighted and not buried in dense text.
6. The conclusion follows from the evidence presented (no logical leaps).
7. The narrative can be followed by someone who did not participate in
   the research.

## Fail Actions

- If structure is disorganized: restructure according to the standard
  template.
- If logical connections are missing: add transitional reasoning.
- If repetition exists: consolidate and remove duplicates.
- If depth mismatches audience: adjust language and detail level.
- If conclusions do not follow from evidence: strengthen the logical
  chain or qualify the conclusion.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate if the evidence does not support a coherent narrative (the
  findings are genuinely contradictory).
- Escalate if the target audience changes after synthesis is drafted.
- Escalate if narrative coherence requires taking a stronger position
  than the evidence warrants.
