# Narrative Coherence Gate

## Purpose

Verify that the synthesized research narrative is logically coherent,
internally consistent, and tells a clear story from evidence to conclusions.
An incoherent narrative confuses stakeholders, undermines trust, and obscures
the actual findings.

## When Triggered

- After the synthesis squad assembles findings into a narrative.
- Before the narrative is included in any deliverable.
- When reviewers report confusion or contradictions in the draft.

## Prerequisites

- All contributing findings have passed evidence and verification gates.
- The synthesis structure (outline, flow) has been defined.
- Target audience and their context are known.

## Checklist

- [ ] The narrative has a clear throughline from research question to conclusions.
- [ ] Each section logically follows from the preceding section.
- [ ] Claims made in the narrative are traceable to specific evidence.
- [ ] No internal contradictions exist between sections or paragraphs.
- [ ] Terminology is used consistently throughout the narrative.
- [ ] Transitions between topics are explicit and logical.
- [ ] The level of detail is consistent (no sections overly detailed while others are sparse).
- [ ] Uncertainty and confidence levels are woven into the narrative, not relegated to footnotes.
- [ ] The narrative does not overstate conclusions beyond what the evidence supports.
- [ ] Counter-arguments and limitations are integrated rather than omitted.
- [ ] The narrative is accessible to the target audience without sacrificing accuracy.
- [ ] Executive summary and detailed sections tell consistent stories at different depths.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

**Pass**: The narrative flows logically, is internally consistent, accurately
represents the evidence, and is comprehensible to the target audience.

**Fail**: Logical gaps, internal contradictions, unsupported claims, or
audience-inappropriate language are present.

## Escalation if Failed

- Return to the synthesis team for revision with specific incoherence
  points identified.
- If contradictions stem from conflicting evidence, escalate to the
  Verification squad for resolution.
- Escalate to the Chief if narrative incoherence cannot be resolved without
  changing the research conclusions.
- Request peer review from a squad uninvolved in the research for fresh
  perspective on clarity.
