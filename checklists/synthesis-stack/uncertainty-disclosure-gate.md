# Uncertainty Disclosure Gate

## Purpose

This gate ensures that all uncertainties, limitations, and knowledge gaps
in the research are transparently disclosed in the output. Suppressing
uncertainty creates a false sense of precision that leads to overconfident
decisions. This gate enforces honesty about what the research does and does
not establish, protecting both the research team's credibility and the
decision-maker's ability to calibrate risk.

## Gate Question

**Are uncertainties properly disclosed?**

Every source of uncertainty in the research must be identified, categorized,
and communicated in a way that allows decision-makers to understand the
reliability boundaries of each finding and recommendation.

## Pass Criteria

1. Data limitations are explicitly stated including gaps, staleness, and access restrictions.
2. Methodological limitations are documented for each research approach used.
3. The confidence level of each key finding is expressed using a consistent scale.
4. Known unknowns are listed with their potential impact on conclusions.
5. Assumptions that could not be validated are flagged with sensitivity analysis where feasible.
6. Areas where expert opinion diverges are presented as zones of genuine uncertainty.
7. The distinction between statistical uncertainty and interpretive uncertainty is maintained.
8. Uncertainty is disclosed at the point of relevance, not buried in appendices.
9. The overall uncertainty profile of the research is summarized for executive audiences.
10. Scenarios or ranges are used instead of point estimates where uncertainty is high.
11. The impact of each uncertainty on the reliability of recommendations is assessed.
12. Potential future developments that could change conclusions are identified.
13. No false precision is introduced through overly specific numbers or dates.

## Fail Actions

- If data limitations are undisclosed, audit the data sources and add limitation statements.
- If confidence levels are missing, assign them using the evidence strength ratings.
- If known unknowns are unlisted, conduct a gap analysis and document findings.
- If assumptions are unvalidated and unflagged, add explicit assumption disclosure sections.
- If uncertainty is buried in appendices, move key disclosures to relevant sections.
- If point estimates are used where ranges are more appropriate, convert to ranges.
- If false precision is detected, replace with appropriately hedged language.
- If the executive uncertainty summary is missing, create one before final output.
- Do not finalize research output until all material uncertainties are disclosed.
