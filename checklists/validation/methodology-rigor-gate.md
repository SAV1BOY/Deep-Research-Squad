# Methodology Rigor Gate

## Purpose

Validate that the research methodology applied throughout the investigation
is rigorous, appropriate for the research question, and consistently
executed. Methodological weaknesses undermine the credibility and
defensibility of all downstream findings and recommendations.

## When Triggered

- After the research methodology is designed and before execution begins.
- At mid-point review of an ongoing research effort.
- Before final findings are submitted for synthesis.

## Prerequisites

- Research question and scope finalized.
- Methodology documented with rationale for chosen approach.
- Data collection and analysis procedures defined.

## Checklist

- [ ] The methodology is appropriate for the type of research question being asked.
- [ ] The rationale for choosing this methodology over alternatives is documented.
- [ ] Data collection procedures are systematic and reproducible.
- [ ] Inclusion and exclusion criteria are defined and consistently applied.
- [ ] Potential biases in the methodology are identified with mitigation strategies.
- [ ] The methodology accounts for known limitations of available data.
- [ ] Analytical frameworks are applied consistently across all evidence.
- [ ] Quality controls are built into the process (peer review, spot checks, validation).
- [ ] The methodology can withstand scrutiny from domain experts.
- [ ] Changes to methodology during execution are documented with justification.
- [ ] Results are interpreted within the bounds of what the methodology can support.
- [ ] The methodology is documented well enough for an independent team to replicate.
- [ ] Edge cases and boundary conditions are handled explicitly.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

**Pass**: Methodology is appropriate, consistently applied, reproducible,
and includes bias mitigation. All deviations are documented.

**Fail**: Methodology is inappropriate for the question, inconsistently
applied, or lacks bias identification and mitigation.

## Escalation if Failed

- Return to the Architect for methodology redesign if the approach is
  fundamentally inappropriate.
- If inconsistencies are found, audit all findings produced under the
  flawed methodology.
- Escalate to the Chief if methodological weakness affects deliverable
  credibility.
- Document methodological limitations prominently in all outputs if the
  issue cannot be fully resolved.
