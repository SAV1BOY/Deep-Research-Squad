# Literature Seminal Works Check

## Purpose

Ensure that foundational and highly influential works in the field are
identified and included. Missing seminal works indicates an incomplete
understanding of the domain's intellectual foundations.

## Gate Question

**Have the seminal and foundational works in this field been identified
and reviewed?**

## Prerequisites

- Literature search completed.
- Citation analysis performed (most-cited works identified).
- Domain history and intellectual lineage understood at a basic level.
- Key authors and research groups identified.

## Pass Criteria

1. The top 5-10 most-cited works in the field are identified and reviewed.
2. Foundational theories or frameworks are cited and their influence on
   subsequent work is noted.
3. Landmark studies that changed the direction of the field are included.
4. Authors who established key concepts used in the research are credited.
5. The intellectual lineage from seminal works to current research is
   traced.
6. Classic works are included even if old, with a note on their enduring
   relevance.
7. Any seminal work that has been superseded is noted as such.

## Fail Actions

- If citation analysis was not performed: run it on the collected corpus
  to find most-referenced works.
- If foundational works are missing: add them with a priority search.
- If intellectual lineage is not traced: create a brief genealogy of
  key ideas.
- If superseded works are treated as current: update their status.
- If key authors are unidentified: use citation metrics to surface them.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate if the seminal works are in a language the agents cannot read.
- Escalate if foundational works are out of print or otherwise
  inaccessible.
- Escalate if the field is so new that no consensus seminal works exist
  and domain expert input is needed to identify the most influential
  early contributions.
