# Systematic Completeness Gate

## Purpose

Ensure that literature reviews are systematically complete: all relevant
bodies of work have been identified, searched, and evaluated. An incomplete
review risks missing critical findings, established consensus, or known
controversies that should inform the research.

## When Triggered

- After the initial literature search phase concludes.
- Before literature findings are used to inform the research thesis.
- When a reviewer identifies a potentially overlooked body of work.

## Prerequisites

- Research question and scope boundaries defined.
- Search strategy documented with databases, keywords, and date ranges.
- Inclusion and exclusion criteria established.

## Checklist

- [ ] All major databases and repositories relevant to the domain have been searched.
- [ ] Search terms cover synonyms, related concepts, and variant spellings.
- [ ] Both English-language and relevant non-English sources are included.
- [ ] Grey literature (reports, working papers, preprints) has been considered.
- [ ] Seminal works identified through citation analysis are included.
- [ ] Recent publications (last 12-24 months) are represented to capture current state.
- [ ] Inclusion and exclusion criteria are applied consistently across all sources.
- [ ] Backward citation chasing (references of key papers) has been performed.
- [ ] Forward citation chasing (papers citing key works) has been performed.
- [ ] Known experts and research groups in the field are represented in results.
- [ ] Conflicting or minority viewpoints in the literature are captured.
- [ ] The total yield is plausible for the field (not suspiciously low or high).
- [ ] Search strategy is documented well enough to be reproducible.

## Pass / Fail Criteria

**Pass**: All relevant databases searched, citation chasing performed in both
directions, inclusion criteria applied consistently, and the yield is
plausible for the domain.

**Fail**: Major databases unsearched, citation chasing incomplete, or
systematic gaps in coverage identified.

## Escalation if Failed

- Expand search to missed databases or repositories and re-run the gate.
- If language barriers limit coverage, flag the gap and assess its impact
  on conclusions.
- Escalate to the Architect if incomplete coverage may invalidate the
  research design.
- Document any irreducible gaps in the final literature review output.
