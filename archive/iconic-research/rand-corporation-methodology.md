# RAND Corporation Methodology

## Why This Is Iconic

RAND Corporation, founded in 1948, pioneered many of the analytical methods
that underpin modern research: scenario planning, systems analysis, Delphi
method, and game theory applications. Their methodology set the standard
for rigorous policy research.

## Core Methodological Contributions

### 1. Systems Analysis
RAND developed systems analysis as a way to evaluate complex problems holistically.
- Break problems into components
- Model interactions between components
- Evaluate alternatives against multiple criteria
- Account for uncertainty explicitly

### 2. Scenario Planning
Originally developed for military planning, now standard in business strategy.
- Identify key uncertainties (not predictions)
- Construct multiple plausible futures
- Stress-test strategies against each scenario
- Monitor for early indicators

### 3. Delphi Method
Structured expert elicitation to harness collective judgment.
- Anonymous expert surveys in multiple rounds
- Feedback between rounds to refine estimates
- Convergence measured, dissent preserved
- Reduces groupthink while leveraging expertise

### 4. Cost-Benefit Analysis
Systematic comparison of costs and benefits across alternatives.
- Quantify where possible, qualify where not
- Discount future values appropriately
- Account for externalities
- Sensitivity analysis on key assumptions

## RAND Quality Standards

1. **Objectivity**: Analysis must be independent of sponsor preferences
2. **Transparency**: Methods and assumptions must be fully documented
3. **Peer review**: All publications undergo internal and external review
4. **Replicability**: Another analyst should be able to reach similar conclusions
5. **Actionability**: Research must connect to decisions

## Lessons for Deep Research Squad

### What We Adopt
- Systems thinking approach to complex research questions
- Scenario planning for strategic analyses
- Explicit uncertainty handling (never false certainty)
- Transparent methodology documentation
- Multiple rounds of expert review

### What We Adapt
- Delphi method → multi-agent analysis with contrarian challenge
- Systems analysis → structured evidence tables with interaction mapping
- Cost-benefit → options matrices in decision briefs
- Peer review → audit script with standards compliance check

## Key RAND Publications to Study

1. "The Art of Systems Analysis" (1977) — foundational methodology text
2. "Robustness Analysis" — decision-making under deep uncertainty
3. "Truth Decay" (2018) — analysis of declining trust in facts
4. RAND Quality Standards handbook — internal quality protocols
