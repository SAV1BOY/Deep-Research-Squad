# Cochrane Systematic Reviews

## Why This Is Iconic

Cochrane Collaboration established the gold standard for systematic reviews
in healthcare. Their methodology for comprehensively identifying, evaluating,
and synthesizing evidence has been adopted across disciplines as the highest
form of evidence synthesis.

## Core Methodology

### 1. Protocol-First Approach
Every Cochrane review starts with a published protocol:
- Research question defined using PICO framework
- Search strategy specified before execution
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria set before screening
- Analysis plan determined before data extraction
- Prevents post-hoc rationalization and cherry-picking

### 2. Comprehensive Search Strategy
- Minimum 3 databases searched (Cochrane, MEDLINE, EMBASE)
- Hand-searching of key journals
- Grey literature search (conference proceedings, theses)
- Reference list checking of included studies
- Contact with experts for unpublished data
- No language restrictions (ideal)

### 3. PRISMA Flow Diagram
Transparent documentation of study selection:
- Records identified through searching
- Duplicates removed
- Records screened (title/abstract)
- Full-text articles assessed
- Studies included in synthesis
- Reasons for exclusion documented at each stage

### 4. Risk of Bias Assessment
Every included study assessed for:
- Random sequence generation
- Allocation concealment
- Blinding of participants and personnel
- Blinding of outcome assessment
- Incomplete outcome data
- Selective reporting
- Other sources of bias

### 5. Meta-Analysis (When Appropriate)
Statistical synthesis of results across studies:
- Forest plots showing individual and pooled effects
- Heterogeneity assessment (I-squared statistic)
- Subgroup analyses for important moderators
- Sensitivity analyses removing low-quality studies
- Publication bias assessment (funnel plots)

### 6. GRADE Assessment
Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation:
- High: Further research very unlikely to change confidence
- Moderate: Further research likely to have important impact
- Low: Further research very likely to have important impact
- Very Low: Any estimate of effect is very uncertain

## Lessons for Deep Research Squad

### What We Adopt
- Protocol-first approach (research brief before research)
- Transparent search documentation (source maps)
- Systematic inclusion/exclusion criteria
- Quality assessment of all evidence
- GRADE-style confidence assessment

### What We Adapt
- PRISMA flow → source map with inclusion/exclusion tracking
- Risk of bias → evidence grading system (A through F)
- Meta-analysis → evidence table synthesis with confidence scoring
- GRADE → our 0-100 confidence scoring system

### Practical Limitations
- Full Cochrane reviews take 12-24 months — we operate in days
- We adapt the principles while acknowledging speed/rigor trade-off
- Our "rapid reviews" follow the spirit, not the full letter
