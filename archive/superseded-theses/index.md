# Superseded Theses

## Purpose

This directory archives research theses that were initially developed and validated but were later superseded by new evidence, contradicted by outcomes, or revised based on post-delivery learning. Superseded theses are not failures — they represent the natural evolution of understanding.

## Why Archive Superseded Theses

- **Calibration**: Comparing superseded theses against their replacements helps calibrate confidence scoring and identify systematic overconfidence or underconfidence
- **Learning**: Understanding why a thesis was wrong reveals blind spots in methodology, sourcing, or reasoning
- **Humility**: A visible archive of superseded work reinforces the squad's epistemic humility culture
- **Pattern recognition**: Recurring supersession patterns may indicate systemic weaknesses in specific domains or frameworks

## File Format

Each archived thesis includes:

- Original thesis statement and confidence level
- Date of original assessment
- Evidence that supported the original thesis
- What changed (new evidence, outcome data, market shift)
- Revised thesis or conclusion
- Root cause analysis (why the original thesis was wrong or incomplete)
- Lessons learned for future methodology
- Related engagement ID (linking to `data/research/`)

## Naming Convention

`{engagement-id}-superseded-{topic-slug}.md` (e.g., `RO-2026-01-005-superseded-market-entry-timing.md`)
