# Critical Thinking Workshop Kit

## Workshop Overview

**Duration**: 90 minutes
**Audience**: All research squad agents
**Goal**: Strengthen the team's ability to think critically, challenge
assumptions, detect biases, and reason under uncertainty.

## Agenda

### Module 1: Cognitive Bias Awareness (25 min)

**Exercise: Bias Spotting**
Present 8 research scenarios. Each contains a hidden cognitive bias:

1. **Confirmation bias**: Analyst finds 10 supporting sources, stops searching
   (didn't look for disconfirming evidence)
2. **Anchoring**: First market size estimate ($50B) influences all subsequent analysis
3. **Availability**: Recent AI hype leads to overestimation of AI adoption rates
4. **Authority bias**: McKinsey report accepted without checking methodology
5. **Survivorship bias**: Only studying successful companies to find success patterns
6. **Sunk cost**: Continuing research direction because of time already invested
7. **Groupthink**: Team converges on consensus without testing alternatives
8. **Dunning-Kruger**: Overconfidence in unfamiliar domain

Participants identify the bias and propose a debiasing strategy.

### Module 2: Assumption Testing (20 min)

**Exercise: Key Assumptions Check**
Take a recent research conclusion and:
1. List all assumptions underlying the conclusion (aim for 10+)
2. Rate each assumption: How critical is it? How confident are we?
3. For the top 3 critical/uncertain assumptions, find evidence for and against
4. Determine if the conclusion holds if the assumptions fail

**CIA tradecraft application**: This is directly from the Agency's
Structured Analytic Techniques toolkit.

### Module 3: Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (25 min)

**Exercise: ACH in Practice**
Present a complex business scenario with ambiguous evidence.
Participants work in teams to:
1. Generate at least 4 hypotheses explaining the situation
2. List all available evidence
3. Score each piece of evidence against each hypothesis
4. Identify the hypothesis with the least disconfirming evidence
5. Determine what new information would help distinguish between hypotheses

**Debrief**: Compare team results. Discuss why teams reached different
conclusions from the same evidence.

### Module 4: Pre-Mortem Analysis (20 min)

**Exercise: Assume We're Wrong**
Take a high-confidence finding from recent research.
The rule: Assume it is completely wrong.

Each participant independently:
1. Lists 5 reasons why the finding could be wrong
2. Identifies what evidence they'd need to see to change their mind
3. Assesses the probability that they're wrong

**Key insight**: If you can't imagine being wrong, you haven't thought
hard enough. Intellectual humility is a skill, not a personality trait.

## Success Metrics

- Bias detection rate: >75% of scenarios correctly identified
- Assumption generation: >8 assumptions per conclusion
- ACH engagement: All teams generate 4+ hypotheses
- Pre-mortem quality: Each participant finds 3+ plausible failure modes
- Post-workshop: Contrarian challenges in deliverables increase in quality
