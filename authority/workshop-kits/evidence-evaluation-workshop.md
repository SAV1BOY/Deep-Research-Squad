# Evidence Evaluation Workshop Kit

## Workshop Overview

**Duration**: 90 minutes
**Audience**: All research squad agents, especially Source Hunters,
Evidence Verifiers, and Literature Analysts
**Goal**: Sharpen the team's ability to evaluate evidence quality, detect
weak evidence, and calibrate confidence scores.

## Agenda

### Module 1: Evidence Grading Calibration (30 min)

**Exercise: The Grading Challenge**
Present 15 evidence items from real-world research. Each participant
independently grades each item (A through F) and provides a one-line
justification.

Sample items to include:
- A meta-analysis from a top journal (Grade A)
- A company-funded study with positive results (Grade D)
- A large government dataset (Grade A-B)
- An anonymous industry source (Grade F)
- A pre-print from a credible lab (Grade C)
- A consulting firm report without methodology (Grade C-D)

**Debrief**: Compare grades across participants. Where they diverge,
discuss the reasoning. Goal: <1 grade difference on 80% of items.

### Module 2: Detecting Weak Evidence (25 min)

**Exercise: Spot the Problem**
Present 10 claims with supporting evidence. Each has a specific problem:
1. Cherry-picked timeframe showing favorable trend
2. Correlation presented as causation
3. Survivorship bias in case selection
4. Small sample generalized to population
5. Self-reported data treated as objective measurement
6. Outdated evidence for fast-moving topic
7. Single source supporting a major claim
8. Circular citation (A cites B cites A)
9. Conflicted source (funder benefits from conclusion)
10. False precision from imprecise methodology

Participants identify the problem and suggest mitigation.

### Module 3: Confidence Calibration (20 min)

**Exercise: The Calibration Game**
Present 20 factual claims. Participants assign confidence scores (0-100).
Reveal the answers. Calculate calibration:
- Group all answers rated 70-80%
- What percentage were actually correct?
- Perfect calibration: ~75% correct

**Key insight**: Most people are overconfident. Practice drives calibration.

### Module 4: Building Evidence Tables (15 min)

**Exercise: Table Construction**
Given a claim and 8 sources, build a complete evidence table:
- Grade each source
- Assess direction (supports/contradicts/partial)
- Calculate overall confidence
- Write a synthesis paragraph
- Identify gaps

## Facilitator Notes

- Calibration exercises should be repeated quarterly
- Track individual improvement over time
- Celebrate participants who improve most, not those who start highest
- Use real examples from past deliverables (anonymized if needed)

## Success Metrics

- Grading variance across agents: <1 grade on 80% of items
- Problem detection rate: >80% of issues identified
- Calibration score improvement: >10% improvement per workshop
- Evidence table quality: meets standards on first attempt
