# Research Methodology Workshop Kit

## Workshop Overview

**Duration**: 90 minutes
**Audience**: All research squad agents
**Goal**: Calibrate the team on research methodology standards and ensure
consistent application across all research outputs.

## Agenda

### Module 1: Research Design Fundamentals (25 min)

**Opening exercise (10 min)**:
Present a vague research request. Each participant independently:
1. Identifies the core question
2. Decomposes into sub-questions
3. Selects a methodology

Compare approaches. Discuss differences and converge on best practices.

**Key concepts to cover**:
- Hypothesis-driven vs exploratory research
- MECE decomposition of research questions
- Matching methodology to question type
- Time-boxing research effort appropriately

### Module 2: Source Strategy (20 min)

**Exercise (10 min)**:
Given a research question, each participant creates a source map:
- Which source types to search
- Expected quality tier of each
- Minimum source diversity target

**Key concepts**:
- Source hierarchy and tier system
- Source diversity as quality indicator
- Primary vs secondary source importance
- When to stop searching (diminishing returns)

### Module 3: Evidence Evaluation (25 min)

**Exercise (15 min)**:
Provide 10 evidence items for a claim. Participants:
1. Grade each item (A through F)
2. Build an evidence table
3. Calculate a confidence score
4. Determine a verdict

Compare scores across participants. Calibrate disagreements.

**Key concepts**:
- Evidence grading criteria
- Confidence scoring methodology
- Handling contradicting evidence
- Distinguishing correlation from causation

### Module 4: Synthesis and Communication (20 min)

**Exercise (10 min)**:
Take a completed evidence table and write:
1. A BLUF summary (3 sentences)
2. An executive summary (150 words)
3. A recommendation with confidence

Peer review across participants. Apply writing standards.

**Key concepts**:
- Answer-first communication
- Compression techniques
- Decision translation
- Anti-bullshit protocol

## Workshop Materials Needed

- Sample research requests (3 difficulty levels)
- Pre-built evidence sets for grading exercise
- Writing standards reference card
- Confidence scoring reference card
- Evidence grading rubric

## Facilitator Notes

- Focus on calibration: participants should converge on similar quality
  assessments when given the same evidence
- Highlight where reasonable people disagree and establish norms
- Use archive/failures-and-lessons/ for real-world examples
- End with specific action items for each participant

## Success Metrics

- Confidence score variance across agents decreases by 20%+
- Evidence grading consistency improves measurably
- Writing quality meets standards on first draft more frequently
- Post-workshop deliverables show methodology improvements
