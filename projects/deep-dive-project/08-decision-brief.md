# Phase 8: Decision Brief

## Purpose
Translate research findings, models, and implications into a concise,
actionable brief that enables stakeholders to make informed decisions.
This is the primary deliverable for most research engagements.

## Inputs
- Synthesis findings from Phase 6
- Model and implications from Phase 7
- Original research brief and success criteria
- Stakeholder preferences for format and depth

## Process Steps
1. Draft the executive summary (one page maximum).
2. State the research question and how it was answered.
3. Present key findings ranked by importance and confidence.
4. Outline recommended actions with supporting rationale.
5. Document risks and uncertainties for each recommendation.
6. Include a "what we don't know" section for transparency.
7. Add appendices for detailed evidence and methodology.
8. Review against original success criteria for completeness.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Executive summary is self-contained and under one page
- [ ] Findings directly address the original research question
- [ ] Recommendations are specific, actionable, and time-bound
- [ ] Risks and uncertainties are presented alongside findings
- [ ] Evidence chain is traceable from finding to source
- [ ] Format matches stakeholder preferences
- [ ] Success criteria from Phase 0 are all addressed

## Outputs
- Decision brief document (final deliverable)
- Executive summary (standalone extract)
- Recommendation action plan with owners and timelines
- Risk register for identified uncertainties
- Evidence appendix with full citation chain
- Updated decision registry entries
