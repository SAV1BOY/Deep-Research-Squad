# Phase 1: Scope and Questions

## Purpose
Decompose the primary research question into a structured hierarchy of
sub-questions. Define what is in scope and out of scope to prevent
scope creep and ensure focused investigation.

## Inputs
- Finalized research brief from Phase 0
- Domain context and known prior research
- Stakeholder priorities and constraints
- Available time and resource budget

## Process Steps
1. Break the primary question into 3-7 sub-questions using MECE logic.
2. Prioritize sub-questions by impact on the final decision.
3. Identify dependencies between sub-questions.
4. Define explicit exclusions with rationale for each.
5. Map each sub-question to the type of evidence needed.
6. Estimate effort per sub-question and adjust scope if needed.
7. Create a question tree visualization for team alignment.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Sub-questions are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive
- [ ] Each sub-question has a clear evidence type identified
- [ ] Scope boundaries are explicit and documented
- [ ] Priority ranking is agreed upon by stakeholders
- [ ] Total estimated effort fits within timeline
- [ ] Dependencies are mapped and sequencing is feasible

## Outputs
- Question hierarchy document (tree structure)
- Scope boundary statement (in/out of scope)
- Priority-ranked sub-question list
- Evidence type mapping per sub-question
- Effort estimates and sequencing plan
