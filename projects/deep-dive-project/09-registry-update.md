# Phase 9: Registry Update

## Purpose
Archive all research artifacts and update organizational knowledge
registries to preserve institutional memory. Ensure future research
efforts can build on this work without duplication.

## Inputs
- All phase outputs from Phases 0-8
- Decision brief and final deliverables
- Raw evidence and collection metadata
- Team reflections and lessons learned

## Process Steps
1. Update the source registry with all sources used and their ratings.
2. Update the claim registry with validated and invalidated claims.
3. Add new entries to the insight registry from synthesis findings.
4. Record contradictions in the contradiction registry.
5. Update the hypothesis registry with confirmed or rejected hypotheses.
6. Add all citations to the citation registry.
7. Record decisions in the decision registry.
8. Document lessons learned for methodology improvement.
9. Archive raw data with appropriate retention labels.
10. Create a project retrospective summary.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] All registries are updated with complete entries
- [ ] Source ratings reflect post-research assessments
- [ ] No orphan artifacts exist outside the archive
- [ ] Lessons learned are actionable and specific
- [ ] Retrospective captures both successes and failures
- [ ] Cross-references between registries are consistent
- [ ] Archive is searchable and well-organized

## Outputs
- Updated source, claim, insight, and contradiction registries
- Updated hypothesis, citation, and decision registries
- Lessons learned document
- Project retrospective summary
- Archived project folder with retention labels
- Knowledge transfer notes for future researchers

## Next Phase
This is the final phase. The project is complete. Deliverables are
handed off and registries preserve the institutional knowledge.
