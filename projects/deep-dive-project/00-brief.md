# Phase 0: Research Brief

## Purpose
Define the research question, context, and success criteria for a deep-dive
investigation. This document serves as the contract between the requester
and the research squad.

## Inputs
- Raw research question or problem statement from the requester
- Background context and urgency level
- Known constraints (time, budget, access)
- Stakeholder list and their information needs

## Process Steps
1. Capture the verbatim research question as stated by the requester.
2. Clarify ambiguous terms through structured follow-up questions.
3. Identify the decision this research will inform.
4. Define what "done" looks like with measurable success criteria.
5. Establish the timeline and intermediate checkpoints.
6. Assign squad roles and communication cadence.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Research question is specific, measurable, and falsifiable
- [ ] Success criteria are agreed upon by all stakeholders
- [ ] Timeline is realistic given available resources
- [ ] No critical ambiguities remain unresolved
- [ ] Squad roles and escalation paths are documented

## Outputs
- Finalized research question (single sentence)
- Success criteria checklist
- Timeline with milestones
- Stakeholder communication plan
- Initial risk assessment
