# Phase 2: Trend Validation

## Purpose
Validate trend candidates by gathering corroborating evidence, testing
alternative explanations, and assessing whether signals represent a
genuine trend or transient noise.

## Inputs
- Trend candidate clusters from Phase 1
- Signal inventory with strength ratings
- Historical data for trajectory comparison
- Expert opinions and domain knowledge
- Comparable trend patterns from other domains

## Process Steps
1. For each trend candidate, define what validation looks like.
2. Seek corroborating evidence from independent sources.
3. Test alternative explanations for the observed signals.
4. Compare the signal pattern to known trend archetypes.
5. Assess the underlying drivers: structural or temporary?
6. Estimate trend velocity: how fast is it developing?
7. Interview domain experts for calibration and challenge.
8. Assign a validated/unvalidated/watch status to each candidate.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Each trend candidate has a defined validation threshold
- [ ] Corroborating evidence comes from independent sources
- [ ] Alternative explanations are explicitly tested
- [ ] Underlying drivers are identified and assessed
- [ ] Expert calibration includes skeptics, not just advocates
- [ ] Trend velocity estimates include uncertainty ranges
- [ ] Status assignments are justified with evidence summary

## Outputs
- Validation assessment per trend candidate
- Corroborating evidence summaries
- Alternative explanation test results
- Driver analysis per validated trend
- Trend velocity estimates with uncertainty
- Expert calibration notes
- Updated hypothesis and insight registries
