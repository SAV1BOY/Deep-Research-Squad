# Phase 3: Impact Assessment

## Purpose
Assess the strategic impact of each validated trend on the organization.
Determine which trends require immediate action, which need monitoring,
and which can be deprioritized.

## Inputs
- Validated trends with driver analysis from Phase 2
- Organizational strategy and priorities
- Current capabilities and resource constraints
- Industry and competitive context
- Trend velocity and maturity estimates

## Process Steps
1. Map each validated trend to affected strategic priorities.
2. Assess first-order impact: direct effects on operations, market.
3. Assess second-order impact: competitive dynamics, customer shifts.
4. Estimate time-to-impact for each trend effect.
5. Evaluate organizational preparedness for each impact.
6. Identify opportunities created by each trend.
7. Identify threats and vulnerabilities from each trend.
8. Prioritize trends by combined impact and urgency.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Impact assessment covers both opportunities and threats
- [ ] Time-to-impact estimates include fast and slow scenarios
- [ ] Organizational preparedness is honestly assessed
- [ ] Trend interactions and compounding effects are considered
- [ ] Prioritization balances impact magnitude with urgency
- [ ] Assessment is reviewed by stakeholders with domain knowledge
- [ ] No validated trend is left without an impact assessment

## Outputs
- Impact assessment per validated trend
- Opportunity and threat inventory per trend
- Time-to-impact timeline visualization
- Organizational preparedness gap analysis
- Trend priority ranking with action urgency
- Trend interaction map
- Updated insight and decision registries
