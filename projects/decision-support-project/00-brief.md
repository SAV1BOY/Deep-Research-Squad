# Phase 0: Decision Support Brief

## Purpose
Define the decision to be supported, the decision-maker's context,
criteria, and constraints. Establish what evidence and analysis the
decision-maker needs to act with confidence.

## Inputs
- Decision statement from the decision-maker
- Decision timeline and urgency level
- Known options under consideration
- Decision criteria and their relative importance
- Risk tolerance and organizational constraints
- Stakeholders affected by the decision

## Process Steps
1. Capture the specific decision in clear, unambiguous language.
2. Identify the decision-maker and their authority boundaries.
3. Document known options and any options already eliminated.
4. Elicit decision criteria through structured conversation.
5. Understand risk tolerance: conservative, moderate, aggressive.
6. Map stakeholders who will be affected by the outcome.
7. Establish the evidence standard required for confidence.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Decision statement is specific and actionable
- [ ] Decision-maker and authority boundaries are clear
- [ ] Decision criteria are explicit and prioritized
- [ ] Risk tolerance is documented, not assumed
- [ ] Stakeholder impact map is complete
- [ ] Evidence standard is defined (what counts as sufficient)
- [ ] Timeline allows for adequate research

## Outputs
- Decision support charter document
- Decision statement and criteria framework
- Known options inventory
- Risk tolerance profile
- Stakeholder impact map
- Evidence sufficiency standard
- Research timeline with checkpoints
