# Phase 1: Research Plan

## Purpose
Design a targeted research strategy to gather evidence relevant to
each decision criterion and option. Prioritize research effort by
the criteria most likely to differentiate between options.

## Inputs
- Decision support charter from Phase 0
- Decision criteria with priority weights
- Known options inventory
- Evidence sufficiency standards
- Available research resources and tools

## Process Steps
1. Map each decision criterion to the evidence types needed.
2. Identify which criteria most differentiate between options.
3. Prioritize research effort on high-differentiation criteria.
4. Design research questions for each criterion-option pair.
5. Identify sources and methods for each research question.
6. Estimate effort and timeline per research stream.
7. Create the research execution plan with assignments.
8. Define quality standards for evidence collection.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Every decision criterion has mapped evidence requirements
- [ ] High-differentiation criteria receive proportional effort
- [ ] Research questions are specific and answerable
- [ ] Sources are identified and accessible for each question
- [ ] Effort estimates are realistic and within timeline
- [ ] Quality standards match the evidence sufficiency threshold
- [ ] Research plan is approved by decision-maker

## Outputs
- Research plan document with criterion-evidence mapping
- Prioritized research question list
- Source and method plan per research question
- Research execution timeline with assignments
- Quality standards specification
- Decision-maker approval record
- Updated methodology registry
