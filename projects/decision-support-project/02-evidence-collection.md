# Phase 2: Evidence Collection

## Purpose
Execute the research plan to gather evidence for each decision criterion
and option combination. Maintain rigorous documentation for traceability
and quality assurance.

## Inputs
- Research plan from Phase 1
- Source and method specifications
- Quality standards for evidence
- Collection templates and tools
- Access credentials and permissions

## Process Steps
1. Set up evidence collection infrastructure and trackers.
2. Execute research streams in priority order.
3. For each evidence item, record source, date, and quality rating.
4. Cross-reference findings across independent sources.
5. Flag evidence that contradicts initial assumptions.
6. Assess evidence sufficiency per criterion on a rolling basis.
7. Redirect effort if early findings change differentiation analysis.
8. Compile the evidence base organized by criterion and option.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Evidence collection follows the approved research plan
- [ ] Every evidence item has full provenance documentation
- [ ] Cross-referencing identifies corroboration and conflicts
- [ ] Contradictory evidence is flagged, not filtered out
- [ ] Evidence sufficiency is assessed per criterion
- [ ] Collection pace is on track with the decision timeline
- [ ] Quality ratings are assigned to all evidence items

## Outputs
- Evidence base organized by criterion and option
- Evidence provenance documentation
- Cross-reference and corroboration report
- Contradiction and surprise findings log
- Evidence sufficiency assessment per criterion
- Collection progress report
- Updated source and claim registries
