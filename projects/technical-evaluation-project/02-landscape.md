# Phase 2: Technology Landscape

## Purpose
Survey the technology landscape to identify all viable candidate
solutions. Filter the long list to a manageable shortlist based on
must-have requirements and initial feasibility assessment.

## Inputs
- Requirements specification from Phase 1
- Must-have criteria for initial filtering
- Industry analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester, etc.)
- Open source repositories and community health metrics
- Vendor briefings and product documentation
- Peer recommendations and case studies

## Process Steps
1. Cast a wide net to identify all potential solutions.
2. Categorize solutions: commercial, open source, build-in-house.
3. Apply must-have filter to eliminate non-viable candidates.
4. Assess vendor/project viability: funding, community, roadmap.
5. Check for deal-breakers: licensing, geography, compliance.
6. Score remaining candidates on initial impression criteria.
7. Select 3-5 candidates for detailed evaluation.
8. Document rationale for inclusions and exclusions.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Initial scan covers all major solution categories
- [ ] Must-have filter is applied consistently to all candidates
- [ ] Vendor/project viability is assessed, not assumed
- [ ] Deal-breaker checks are thorough and documented
- [ ] Shortlist size is manageable (3-5 candidates)
- [ ] Exclusion rationale is documented for transparency
- [ ] No promising candidate is excluded without justification

## Outputs
- Long list of all identified candidates
- Filtering rationale and elimination log
- Shortlisted candidates with initial profiles
- Vendor/project viability assessments
- Landscape overview visual (quadrant or tier map)
- Updated source registry entries
