# Phase 0: Technical Evaluation Brief

## Purpose
Define the technical evaluation objective, the technology domain under
review, and the criteria for success. Establish the decision context
that this evaluation will inform.

## Inputs
- Technology decision or problem statement
- Business requirements driving the evaluation
- Current technology stack and constraints
- Budget, timeline, and team skill constraints
- Stakeholder list and their technical priorities

## Process Steps
1. Document the specific technology decision to be made.
2. Identify the business drivers and urgency behind the evaluation.
3. Capture current state: existing stack, integrations, tech debt.
4. Define evaluation success criteria with stakeholder input.
5. Set the evaluation timeline and review checkpoints.
6. Assign evaluation team roles and responsibilities.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Technology decision is framed as a clear, answerable question
- [ ] Business drivers are documented with priority ranking
- [ ] Current state is accurately captured including constraints
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable and agreed upon
- [ ] Timeline includes buffer for unexpected complexity
- [ ] Team has sufficient expertise for the evaluation domain

## Outputs
- Technical evaluation brief document
- Business driver priority list
- Current state architecture summary
- Evaluation success criteria checklist
- Timeline with milestones and review points
- Team roster with role assignments
