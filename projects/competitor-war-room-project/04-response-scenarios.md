# Phase 4: Response Scenarios

## Purpose
Model how competitors are likely to respond to your strategic moves
and market changes. War-game potential scenarios to identify optimal
strategies and prepare contingency plans.

## Inputs
- Moat analysis from Phase 3
- Deep competitor profiles and decision DNA models
- Your planned strategic initiatives
- Market trend trajectories
- Historical competitor response patterns

## Process Steps
1. Define 3-5 strategic moves you are considering.
2. For each move, model the most likely competitor responses.
3. War-game each scenario through 2-3 rounds of action-reaction.
4. Identify scenarios where competitors are constrained in response.
5. Identify scenarios where competitor response neutralizes your move.
6. Assess the probability of each response pattern.
7. Model second-order effects on ecosystem and customers.
8. Identify moves that create the best outcome across scenarios.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Response models are grounded in competitor decision DNA
- [ ] Multiple rounds of action-reaction are modeled
- [ ] Probability assignments are justified with evidence
- [ ] Both rational and irrational responses are considered
- [ ] Second-order ecosystem effects are included
- [ ] Historical precedents are referenced where available
- [ ] Scenarios cover optimistic, base, and pessimistic cases

## Outputs
- Scenario matrix (your moves x competitor responses)
- War-game simulation summaries
- Probability-weighted outcome assessments
- Optimal move identification with rationale
- Contingency playbooks per scenario
- Counter-response preparation plans
- Updated hypothesis and decision registries
