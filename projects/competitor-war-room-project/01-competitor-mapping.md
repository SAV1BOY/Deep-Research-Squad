# Phase 1: Competitor Mapping

## Purpose
Create a comprehensive map of the competitive landscape showing all
players, their positions, relationships, and relative strengths.
This map serves as the strategic foundation for deeper profiling.

## Inputs
- War room charter from Phase 0
- Public financial data and market share estimates
- Product and service catalogs of each competitor
- Partnership and ecosystem relationship data
- Recent M&A activity and investment signals

## Process Steps
1. List all competitors: direct, indirect, and potential entrants.
2. Position each on a strategic group map (2-3 dimensions).
3. Map market share distribution and recent trends.
4. Document ecosystem relationships: partners, suppliers, channels.
5. Identify potential new entrants and their entry vectors.
6. Map substitute products and alternative solutions.
7. Assess the overall competitive intensity per segment.
8. Create the master competitive landscape visualization.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] All competitor categories are covered (direct/indirect/potential)
- [ ] Strategic positioning uses consistent, relevant dimensions
- [ ] Market share data is sourced and date-stamped
- [ ] Ecosystem relationships include both formal and informal ties
- [ ] New entrant analysis considers adjacent industry players
- [ ] Substitutes and alternatives are identified
- [ ] Visualization is clear and stakeholder-accessible

## Outputs
- Competitive landscape map (visual)
- Strategic group analysis
- Market share distribution table
- Ecosystem relationship diagram
- New entrant threat assessment
- Substitute analysis summary
- Updated source registry
