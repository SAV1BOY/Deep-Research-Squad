# Phase 0: Competitor War Room Brief

## Purpose
Define the competitive intelligence mission, target competitors, and
the strategic decisions this war room will support. Establish urgency,
scope, and rules of engagement.

## Inputs
- Competitive threat or opportunity triggering the war room
- List of target competitors and priority ranking
- Strategic decisions pending on competitive intelligence
- Available intelligence sources and access levels
- Timeline and resource constraints

## Process Steps
1. Document the competitive trigger event or strategic need.
2. Identify and prioritize target competitors (tier 1, 2, 3).
3. Define specific intelligence questions per competitor.
4. Establish ethical boundaries and legal compliance rules.
5. Inventory available intelligence sources and tools.
6. Set the war room cadence: daily standups, weekly deep-dives.
7. Assign team roles: analyst, collector, synthesizer, presenter.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Competitive trigger is clearly articulated
- [ ] Target competitors are prioritized with rationale
- [ ] Intelligence questions are specific and actionable
- [ ] Ethical and legal boundaries are documented and agreed
- [ ] Source inventory is complete with access status
- [ ] Team roles and cadence are confirmed
- [ ] Escalation protocols are defined

## Outputs
- War room charter document
- Competitor priority matrix
- Intelligence question framework
- Ethical guidelines and compliance checklist
- Source and tool inventory
- Team roster and communication plan
