# Phase 3: Moat Analysis

## Purpose
Analyze the competitive moats (sustainable advantages) and vulnerabilities
of each priority competitor. Identify where moats are strengthening,
eroding, or attackable.

## Inputs
- Deep competitor profiles from Phase 2
- Market dynamics and trend data
- Technology disruption signals
- Customer switching cost data
- Network effect and ecosystem lock-in indicators

## Process Steps
1. Identify each competitor's moat type: network effects, switching
   costs, brand, IP, scale economies, regulatory capture.
2. Assess moat depth: how long would it take to replicate?
3. Evaluate moat width: how many attack vectors does it block?
4. Track moat trajectory: strengthening, stable, or eroding?
5. Identify moat vulnerabilities and potential attack vectors.
6. Assess your own moat relative to each competitor.
7. Map asymmetric advantages that favor specific players.
8. Identify potential moat-building moves available to you.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] All standard moat categories are evaluated per competitor
- [ ] Moat depth assessments include time-to-replicate estimates
- [ ] Erosion factors are identified with timeline projections
- [ ] Vulnerability analysis is specific and actionable
- [ ] Own-moat assessment is honest and evidence-based
- [ ] Asymmetric advantages are clearly articulated
- [ ] Analysis avoids wishful thinking about competitor weakness

## Outputs
- Moat analysis per competitor (depth, width, trajectory)
- Vulnerability map with attack vector identification
- Own-moat comparative assessment
- Moat trajectory forecast (12-36 month outlook)
- Asymmetric advantage inventory
- Moat-building opportunity list
- Updated hypothesis and insight registries
