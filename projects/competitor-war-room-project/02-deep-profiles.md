# Phase 2: Deep Competitor Profiles

## Purpose
Build comprehensive intelligence profiles for each tier-1 competitor.
Go beyond surface data to understand strategy, culture, decision-making
patterns, and operational DNA.

## Inputs
- Competitor map and priority list from Phase 1
- Financial filings, earnings calls, investor presentations
- Patent portfolios and R&D investment patterns
- Job postings, organizational charts, LinkedIn signals
- Product teardowns and reverse engineering data
- Customer reviews, NPS data, and churn signals

## Process Steps
1. Create a structured profile template for consistency.
2. Analyze corporate strategy from public communications.
3. Map organizational structure and key decision-makers.
4. Profile the leadership team: backgrounds, patterns, biases.
5. Analyze product strategy from release patterns and roadmaps.
6. Assess engineering culture from job postings and tech blogs.
7. Track hiring patterns as leading indicators of strategy.
8. Build a "competitor personality" model capturing decision DNA.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Profiles cover strategy, product, org, finance, and culture
- [ ] Leadership profiles include decision pattern analysis
- [ ] Job posting analysis spans at least 6 months of data
- [ ] Product strategy inferences are supported by evidence
- [ ] Cultural assessment uses multiple independent signals
- [ ] Competitor personality model is testable with predictions
- [ ] All intelligence is ethically sourced

## Outputs
- Deep profile document per tier-1 competitor
- Leadership decision pattern analysis
- Hiring trend and signal report
- Product strategy trajectory assessment
- Cultural DNA summary per competitor
- Prediction log with testable forecasts
- Updated claim and insight registries
