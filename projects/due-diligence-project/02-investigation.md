# Phase 2: Investigation

## Purpose
Execute the investigation plan systematically, collecting and analyzing
evidence across all diligence dimensions. Surface red flags early and
escalate deal-breakers immediately.

## Inputs
- Prioritized investigation checklist from Phase 1
- Data room access and document inventory
- Public records databases and search tools
- Interview schedules with target management and references
- Expert network access for domain validation

## Process Steps
1. Begin with deal-breaker investigation areas.
2. Review data room documents against the request list.
3. Cross-reference financial statements with public filings.
4. Verify key claims through independent sources.
5. Conduct management interviews with structured question sets.
6. Engage domain experts for technical validation.
7. Search public records for litigation, liens, regulatory issues.
8. Track findings against the investigation checklist in real-time.
9. Escalate any deal-breaker findings immediately to stakeholders.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Deal-breaker areas are investigated first and reported early
- [ ] Data room document review is systematic and complete
- [ ] Financial cross-referencing covers at least 3 years of data
- [ ] Key claims have independent verification from two sources
- [ ] Management interview findings are corroborated
- [ ] Public records search covers all relevant jurisdictions
- [ ] Investigation checklist shows progress on all dimensions
- [ ] Red flags are escalated within 24 hours of discovery

## Outputs
- Investigation findings organized by dimension
- Red flag report with severity ratings
- Deal-breaker assessment (pass/fail/conditional per criterion)
- Financial analysis workpapers
- Interview notes and corroboration status
- Public records search results
- Updated claim and source registries
