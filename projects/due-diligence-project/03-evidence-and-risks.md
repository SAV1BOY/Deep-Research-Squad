# Phase 3: Evidence and Risks

## Purpose
Consolidate all investigation findings into an evidence-based risk
assessment. Quantify risks where possible, categorize by severity
and probability, and identify mitigation strategies.

## Inputs
- Investigation findings from Phase 2
- Red flag report and deal-breaker assessments
- Financial analysis workpapers
- Interview notes and corroboration data
- Industry risk benchmarks and comparable transactions

## Process Steps
1. Organize all findings into a master evidence table.
2. Rate each finding by confidence level and materiality.
3. Categorize risks: financial, legal, operational, technical,
   reputational, regulatory, and market.
4. Assess probability and impact for each identified risk.
5. Create a risk heat map (probability x impact).
6. Estimate financial exposure for quantifiable risks.
7. Identify risk interdependencies and cascade scenarios.
8. Develop mitigation strategies for each material risk.
9. Determine which risks are deal-adjusting vs deal-breaking.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] All findings are entered in the evidence table with sources
- [ ] Confidence levels are assigned to every finding
- [ ] Risk categories are comprehensive and MECE
- [ ] Probability and impact ratings use a consistent scale
- [ ] Financial exposure estimates include methodology notes
- [ ] Risk interdependencies are mapped
- [ ] Mitigation strategies are practical and costed
- [ ] Deal-adjusting vs deal-breaking distinction is clear

## Outputs
- Master evidence table with confidence ratings
- Risk register with probability, impact, and exposure
- Risk heat map visualization
- Financial exposure summary
- Risk interdependency map
- Mitigation strategy document
- Updated contradiction and claim registries
