# Phase 0: Due Diligence Brief

## Purpose
Define the due diligence scope, target entity, and the investment or
partnership decision this investigation will inform. Establish risk
tolerance and deal-breaker criteria upfront.

## Inputs
- Target entity identification and basic profile
- Transaction type: acquisition, investment, partnership, vendor
- Decision-maker expectations and risk appetite
- Known red flags or areas of concern
- Timeline constraints (deal timeline, exclusivity windows)
- Budget for external verification services

## Process Steps
1. Document the target entity and transaction context.
2. Define the decision this due diligence will support.
3. Identify deal-breakers that would halt the transaction.
4. Establish the scope: financial, legal, technical, operational.
5. Assess available public and private information sources.
6. Set the investigation timeline with go/no-go checkpoints.
7. Assign investigation team members and external advisors.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Target entity is clearly identified with legal name
- [ ] Transaction type and decision context are documented
- [ ] Deal-breakers are explicitly defined and agreed
- [ ] Scope covers all relevant diligence dimensions
- [ ] Information source access is confirmed, not assumed
- [ ] Timeline aligns with deal constraints
- [ ] Team expertise covers all investigation dimensions

## Outputs
- Due diligence charter document
- Deal-breaker criteria list
- Scope definition by diligence dimension
- Information source inventory with access status
- Investigation timeline with checkpoints
- Team assignment and external advisor list
