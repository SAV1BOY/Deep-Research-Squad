# Phase 4: Decision Brief

## Purpose
Deliver a clear, evidence-based recommendation on whether to proceed
with the transaction, along with conditions, pricing adjustments,
and risk mitigation requirements.

## Inputs
- Risk register and evidence table from Phase 3
- Deal-breaker assessment results
- Financial exposure estimates
- Mitigation strategies and their costs
- Original transaction terms and valuation

## Process Steps
1. State the overall recommendation: proceed, conditional, or walk.
2. Summarize the top 5 material findings with evidence chains.
3. Detail any deal-breakers encountered and their status.
4. List conditions required for proceeding (if conditional).
5. Recommend pricing or term adjustments based on discovered risks.
6. Outline required contractual protections (reps, warranties, indemnities).
7. Define post-close monitoring priorities.
8. Prepare the executive presentation with appendix.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Recommendation is clear and defensible with evidence
- [ ] Material findings are ranked by impact on decision
- [ ] Deal-breaker assessment is complete and conclusive
- [ ] Conditions for proceeding are specific and verifiable
- [ ] Pricing adjustments are justified with financial analysis
- [ ] Contractual protection recommendations are legally sound
- [ ] Post-close monitoring priorities reflect residual risks
- [ ] Executive presentation is concise and decision-ready

## Outputs
- Due diligence decision brief (go/conditional-go/no-go)
- Material findings summary with evidence chains
- Conditions and pricing adjustment recommendations
- Contractual protection recommendations
- Post-close monitoring plan
- Executive presentation
- Updated decision registry entries
