# Phase 0: Literature Review Brief

## Purpose
Define the research question for systematic literature review, establish
the review scope, and set quality standards for evidence inclusion.
This brief ensures the review is focused, reproducible, and useful.

## Inputs
- Research question or knowledge gap to investigate
- Domain and disciplinary boundaries
- Known seminal works and starting references
- Stakeholder expectations for review depth and breadth
- Timeline and resource constraints

## Process Steps
1. Formulate the primary review question using PICO or equivalent.
2. Define the domain boundaries and disciplinary scope.
3. Identify known landmark papers and reviews in the area.
4. Set expectations for review type: systematic, scoping, or rapid.
5. Define the output format and intended audience.
6. Establish the review team and assign roles.
7. Register the review protocol for transparency.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Review question is specific and answerable from literature
- [ ] Domain boundaries are explicit and justified
- [ ] Review type matches the decision needs and timeline
- [ ] Team has sufficient domain expertise for critical appraisal
- [ ] Protocol is documented before searches begin
- [ ] Output format matches audience needs
- [ ] Known landmark works are cataloged as starting points

## Outputs
- Literature review protocol document
- Formatted review question (PICO or equivalent)
- Scope boundary statement
- Known works bibliography
- Team roster with role assignments
- Timeline with milestones
