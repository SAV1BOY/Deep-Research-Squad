# Phase 4: Synthesis

## Purpose
Synthesize findings across all included studies to answer the review
question. Use narrative, thematic, or quantitative approaches as
appropriate to the data and review type.

## Inputs
- Master extraction database from Phase 3
- Quality assessment scores per study
- Review question and sub-questions
- Heterogeneity assessment data
- Domain expertise for contextual interpretation

## Process Steps
1. Assess heterogeneity across studies (clinical, methodological,
   statistical) to determine synthesis approach.
2. For quantitative synthesis: conduct meta-analysis if appropriate.
3. For narrative synthesis: organize by themes or sub-questions.
4. Weight study contributions by quality and sample size.
5. Assess consistency of findings across studies.
6. Identify moderators or subgroup differences.
7. Evaluate the overall strength of evidence using GRADE or similar.
8. Document certainty of evidence per outcome.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Synthesis approach matches data characteristics
- [ ] Heterogeneity assessment precedes quantitative synthesis
- [ ] Study weighting reflects quality and relevance
- [ ] Consistency of findings is explicitly assessed
- [ ] Subgroup analyses are pre-specified, not data-dredged
- [ ] Strength of evidence is graded using a standard framework
- [ ] Sensitivity analyses test robustness of conclusions

## Outputs
- Synthesis narrative or meta-analysis results
- Forest plots or effect size tables (if quantitative)
- Thematic analysis summary (if narrative)
- Heterogeneity and consistency assessment
- Strength of evidence grading per outcome
- Sensitivity analysis results
- Updated insight and claim registries
