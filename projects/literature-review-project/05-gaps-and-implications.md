# Phase 5: Gaps and Implications

## Purpose
Identify knowledge gaps revealed by the review and articulate practical
implications for decision-makers. Define a future research agenda and
translate findings into actionable guidance.

## Inputs
- Synthesis results from Phase 4
- Strength of evidence assessments
- Excluded study characteristics (for gap analysis)
- Stakeholder decision context from Phase 0
- Domain expertise for practical interpretation

## Process Steps
1. Map what the review definitively answers vs. what remains unknown.
2. Categorize gaps: insufficient evidence, conflicting evidence,
   unstudied populations, methodological limitations.
3. Prioritize gaps by practical importance and researchability.
4. Formulate a future research agenda addressing priority gaps.
5. Translate findings into practical recommendations by audience.
6. Assess generalizability and applicability caveats.
7. Document limitations of the review itself.
8. Prepare the final review report with all components.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Gaps are systematically identified, not just listed
- [ ] Gap prioritization considers both importance and feasibility
- [ ] Future research recommendations are specific and justified
- [ ] Practical implications distinguish levels of evidence
- [ ] Generalizability caveats are explicit and specific
- [ ] Review limitations are transparently documented
- [ ] Final report follows reporting guidelines (PRISMA, etc.)

## Outputs
- Knowledge gap analysis document
- Prioritized future research agenda
- Practical implications summary by audience
- Generalizability and applicability assessment
- Review limitations statement
- Final literature review report
- Updated insight, unknowns, and lessons-learned registries

## Next Phase
