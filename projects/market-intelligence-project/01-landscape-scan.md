# Phase 1: Landscape Scan

## Purpose
Conduct a broad sweep of the market landscape to identify all relevant
players, segments, dynamics, and forces. Build the foundational map
that subsequent phases will deepen.

## Inputs
- Market intelligence brief from Phase 0
- Industry reports and databases
- News feeds and press releases
- Patent filings and regulatory documents
- Conference proceedings and analyst reports

## Process Steps
1. Map the market value chain from suppliers to end customers.
2. Identify all players in each value chain segment.
3. Categorize players by size, type, and market position.
4. Document market size estimates and growth rates by segment.
5. Identify key regulatory and policy forces shaping the market.
6. Map technology trends influencing market evolution.
7. Note geographic variations and regional dynamics.
8. Create the initial market landscape visual.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Value chain is complete from input to end customer
- [ ] All significant players are identified and categorized
- [ ] Market size data has cited sources and date stamps
- [ ] Regulatory landscape is current and jurisdiction-specific
- [ ] Technology trends are linked to market impact
- [ ] Geographic coverage matches brief scope
- [ ] No major market segments are missing

## Outputs
- Market landscape map (visual)
- Player inventory with categorization
- Market size and growth summary
- Regulatory environment overview
- Technology trend summary
- Source registry updates
