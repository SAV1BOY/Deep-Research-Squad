# Phase 5: Market Intelligence Synthesis

## Purpose
Integrate findings from landscape scanning, competitor analysis, trend
analysis, and benchmarking into a unified strategic intelligence picture
that directly addresses the original market question.

## Inputs
- Landscape scan from Phase 1
- Competitor profiles and analysis from Phase 2
- Trend analysis and interaction maps from Phase 3
- Benchmarking results and gap analysis from Phase 4
- Original market intelligence brief from Phase 0

## Process Steps
1. Map connections between competitor moves and market trends.
2. Identify strategic windows of opportunity with timing estimates.
3. Assess threats from trend-competitor intersections.
4. Build scenario narratives for likely market evolution paths.
5. Assign probabilities to each scenario with supporting rationale.
6. Identify "no regret" moves that work across all scenarios.
7. Highlight key uncertainties that most impact strategy.
8. Draft the integrated market intelligence narrative.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] All phase findings are integrated, not just summarized
- [ ] Scenarios are internally consistent and plausible
- [ ] Probabilities are justified with evidence and reasoning
- [ ] No-regret moves are truly robust across scenarios
- [ ] Key uncertainties are ranked by strategic impact
- [ ] The narrative directly answers the original question
- [ ] Cross-references between analyses are documented

## Outputs
- Integrated market intelligence narrative
- Scenario descriptions with probability assessments
- Opportunity and threat matrix
- No-regret move recommendations
- Key uncertainty watchlist
- Updated insight and hypothesis registries
