# Phase 3: Trend Analysis

## Purpose
Identify, validate, and assess market trends that will shape competitive
dynamics over the intelligence time horizon. Distinguish signal from
noise and quantify trend trajectories.

## Inputs
- Landscape scan data from Phase 1
- Competitor analysis signals from Phase 2
- Industry analyst reports and forecasts
- Patent filing trends and R&D investment data
- Customer behavior data and survey results
- Regulatory pipeline and policy signals

## Process Steps
1. Compile candidate trends from all input sources.
2. Categorize trends: technology, regulatory, customer, economic.
3. Validate each trend with at least three independent signals.
4. Assess trend maturity: emerging, accelerating, mature, declining.
5. Estimate timeline to mainstream impact for each trend.
6. Map trend interactions and reinforcement patterns.
7. Identify wild cards and potential disruption scenarios.
8. Rate each trend by relevance to the strategic question.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Each trend has at least three independent validating signals
- [ ] Trend maturity assessments use a consistent framework
- [ ] Timeline estimates include uncertainty ranges
- [ ] Trend interactions are mapped for compounding effects
- [ ] Wild cards are identified with probability estimates
- [ ] Relevance ratings link back to the strategic question

## Outputs
- Trend inventory with maturity and timeline assessments
- Trend interaction map
- Wild card and disruption scenario list
- Trend relevance ranking for strategic context
- Trend evidence summary per trend
- Updated insight registry entries
