# Phase 6: Recommendations

## Purpose
Translate market intelligence synthesis into specific, actionable
strategic recommendations with clear rationale, implementation
guidance, and risk mitigation strategies.

## Inputs
- Integrated market intelligence from Phase 5
- Scenario analyses and probability assessments
- Opportunity and threat matrix
- Stakeholder decision context and constraints
- Organizational capabilities and resource availability

## Process Steps
1. Formulate 3-5 primary strategic recommendations.
2. Link each recommendation to supporting evidence chain.
3. Assess implementation feasibility and resource requirements.
4. Define success metrics for each recommendation.
5. Identify risks per recommendation and mitigation strategies.
6. Sequence recommendations by urgency and dependency.
7. Create a monitoring dashboard for tracking market changes.
8. Draft the final market intelligence report.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Each recommendation has a clear evidence chain to findings
- [ ] Feasibility is assessed against actual organizational capacity
- [ ] Success metrics are specific, measurable, and time-bound
- [ ] Risks and mitigations are documented per recommendation
- [ ] Sequencing accounts for dependencies and resource constraints
- [ ] Monitoring triggers for strategy review are defined
- [ ] Final report meets stakeholder format requirements

## Outputs
- Strategic recommendation document with evidence chains
- Implementation roadmap with sequencing
- Risk register per recommendation
- Success metrics and KPI dashboard design
- Market monitoring plan with trigger conditions
- Final market intelligence report
- Updated decision and lessons-learned registries

## Next Phase
This is the final phase. Deliver the market intelligence report,
set up monitoring, and update all organizational registries.
