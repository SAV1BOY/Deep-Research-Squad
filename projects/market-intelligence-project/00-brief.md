# Phase 0: Market Intelligence Brief

## Purpose
Define the market intelligence objective, target market segments, and
deliverable expectations. Establish the scope of competitive and market
analysis required by stakeholders.

## Inputs
- Business question or strategic decision requiring market insight
- Target market segments and geographies
- Known competitors and adjacent players
- Time horizon for the intelligence (short/medium/long term)
- Budget and timeline constraints

## Process Steps
1. Capture the strategic question the intelligence will inform.
2. Define target market boundaries (segments, geographies, verticals).
3. Identify key stakeholders and their specific information needs.
4. Establish the time horizon and refresh cadence.
5. Define deliverable format and depth expectations.
6. Agree on confidence thresholds for actionable intelligence.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] Strategic question is clearly articulated
- [ ] Market boundaries are explicitly defined
- [ ] Stakeholder needs are documented and prioritized
- [ ] Timeline and budget are confirmed
- [ ] Deliverable format is agreed upon
- [ ] Confidence thresholds are set

## Outputs
- Market intelligence brief document
- Stakeholder needs matrix
- Scope boundary definition
- Timeline with checkpoints
- Resource allocation plan
