# Phase 2: Competitor Analysis

## Purpose
Conduct detailed analysis of key competitors identified in the landscape
scan. Profile each competitor's strategy, capabilities, strengths,
weaknesses, and likely future moves.

## Inputs
- Player inventory from Phase 1
- Competitor prioritization criteria from brief
- Financial reports and SEC filings
- Product documentation and pricing data
- Job postings and organizational signals
- Patent portfolios and R&D indicators

## Process Steps
1. Select the top 5-10 competitors for deep profiling.
2. Build a profile template covering strategy, products, financials.
3. Analyze each competitor's value proposition and positioning.
4. Assess financial health and investment capacity.
5. Map product portfolios and feature comparisons.
6. Analyze go-to-market strategies and channel presence.
7. Identify leadership team backgrounds and strategic signals.
8. Infer likely next moves based on patterns and signals.

## Quality Gates
- [ ] All priority competitors have complete profiles
- [ ] Financial data is from verified, dated sources
- [ ] Product comparisons use consistent evaluation criteria
- [ ] Strategic inferences are supported by observable signals
- [ ] Likely next moves include confidence ratings
- [ ] Profiles are peer-reviewed for blind spots

## Outputs
- Competitor profile documents (one per competitor)
- Product feature comparison matrix
- Financial health comparison table
- Strategic positioning map
- Predicted moves with confidence levels
- Updated claim and source registries
