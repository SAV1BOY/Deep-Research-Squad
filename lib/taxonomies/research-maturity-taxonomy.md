# Research Maturity Taxonomy

## Purpose

Classifies research efforts by maturity level, enabling appropriate expectations
for confidence, completeness, and decision-readiness at each stage. Prevents
premature decision-making on immature research and over-investment in mature findings.

## Maturity Levels

### Level 0 — Signal Detection
- **Description**: Initial identification of a topic, trend, or question worth investigating
- **Evidence base**: 0-2 sources, often anecdotal or single-signal
- **Confidence range**: 0-25
- **Appropriate use**: Agenda-setting, hypothesis formation, research prioritization
- **Not appropriate for**: Any decision-making
- **Typical output**: Research question, initial hypothesis, scope proposal
- **Example**: "Three customers mentioned a new competitor — worth investigating?"

### Level 1 — Preliminary Scan
- **Description**: Broad survey of available information to scope the landscape
- **Evidence base**: 3-8 sources, mixed quality, surface-level review
- **Confidence range**: 20-45
- **Appropriate use**: Deciding whether to invest in deeper research, initial framing
- **Not appropriate for**: Strategic decisions, external-facing claims
- **Typical output**: Scan summary, initial findings with heavy caveats, scope recommendation
- **Example**: "Initial scan suggests market is $2-5B, 4 major players identified, regulatory landscape unclear"

### Level 2 — Structured Analysis
- **Description**: Systematic investigation with deliberate source discovery and evidence evaluation
- **Evidence base**: 8-20 sources, quality-assessed, cross-referenced
- **Confidence range**: 45-70
- **Appropriate use**: Internal planning, resource allocation, further research scoping
- **Not appropriate for**: High-stakes strategic decisions without caveats
- **Typical output**: Analysis report, evidence tables, confidence-scored findings
- **Example**: "Market sized at $3.2B (confidence: 62), with 6 competitors mapped and 3 key trends identified"

### Level 3 — Deep Research
- **Description**: Comprehensive investigation with triangulated evidence, contradiction resolution, and expert validation
- **Evidence base**: 15-40+ sources, high-quality, independently corroborated
- **Confidence range**: 65-85
- **Appropriate use**: Strategic decisions, investment cases, external publications
- **Not appropriate for**: Only where 90%+ confidence is legally/contractually required
- **Typical output**: Deep-dive report, scenario analysis, validated recommendations
- **Example**: "Market is $3.4B growing 12% CAGR (confidence: 78), with detailed competitive positioning and 3 strategic scenarios"

### Level 4 — Authoritative Assessment
- **Description**: Exhaustive research with expert review, primary data collection, and peer validation
- **Evidence base**: 30-100+ sources, primary research included, expert-reviewed
- **Confidence range**: 80-95
- **Appropriate use**: Board-level decisions, regulatory submissions, public commitments
- **Not appropriate for**: Routine decisions (over-investment)
- **Typical output**: Comprehensive report with methodology disclosure, peer review notes, full evidence chain
- **Example**: "Definitive market assessment with primary survey (n=500), 3 expert interviews, and full competitive teardown"

## Level Selection Guide

| Decision Stakes | Time Available | Recommended Level |
|----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Low (informational) | Hours | Level 0-1 |
| Medium (internal planning) | Days | Level 2 |
| High (strategic) | 1-2 weeks | Level 3 |
| Critical (board/regulatory) | 2-4 weeks | Level 4 |

## Maturity Progression Rules

- Research can only advance one level at a time
- Each level requires meeting the evidence base threshold
- Skipping levels is an anti-pattern — flag if requested
- Not all research needs to reach Level 4 — match to decision stakes
