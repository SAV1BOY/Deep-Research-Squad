# Research Type Taxonomy

## Purpose

Classifies research efforts by type and goal to guide methodology, resource
allocation, deliverable format, and quality criteria.

## Categories

### 1. Exploratory Research
Broad investigation to map an unfamiliar space.
- **Landscape Survey** - Map the solution space, identify major options
- **Feasibility Study** - Determine if a proposed approach is viable
- **Technology Scan** - Identify emerging relevant technologies

**Example:** "What message queue options exist for our use case?"

### 2. Evaluative Research
Systematic assessment of specific options against criteria.
- **Comparative Evaluation** - Side-by-side comparison on defined criteria
- **Benchmark Study** - Performance-focused quantitative evaluation
- **Risk Assessment** - Focused on identifying and quantifying risks
- **Maturity Assessment** - Evaluation of production readiness

**Example:** "Compare Kafka vs. Pulsar for event streaming."

### 3. Investigative Research
Deep dive into a specific question or problem.
- **Root Cause Analysis** - Investigate why something is happening
- **Architecture Analysis** - Deep study of how a system works
- **Failure Mode Analysis** - Identify how a system can fail

**Example:** "What are the failure modes of CockroachDB under partition?"

### 4. Predictive Research
Forward-looking analysis for strategic decisions.
- **Trend Analysis** - Identify and project trends
- **Scenario Planning** - Evaluate outcomes under different futures
- **Capacity Planning** - Project future resource needs

**Example:** "How will our storage costs grow over 3 years?"

### 5. Synthesis Research
Combining existing knowledge into new understanding.
- **Literature Review** - Comprehensive survey of existing work
- **Best Practice Compilation** - Gather proven approaches
- **Decision Framework** - Build reusable framework for recurring decisions

**Example:** "What are best practices for database migration?"

### 6. Validation Research
Confirming or disproving specific hypotheses.
- **Hypothesis Testing** - Test a specific claim or assumption
- **Proof of Concept** - Validate feasibility through implementation
- **Assumption Audit** - Review assumptions underlying a decision

**Example:** "Validate that Redis can handle our session volume."

## Usage

Classify each research engagement at the outset to select methodology, set
deliverable expectations, allocate resources, and choose quality criteria.
