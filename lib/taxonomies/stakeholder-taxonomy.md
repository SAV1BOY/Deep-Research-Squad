# Stakeholder Taxonomy

## Purpose

Classifies stakeholders who consume research outputs, enabling appropriate calibration
of format, depth, language, and delivery method. Ensures research is packaged for
maximum impact with each audience type.

## Stakeholder Categories

### 1. Decision Makers

**1a. Executive Decision Makers**
- **Role**: C-suite, board members, partners
- **Needs**: Bottom-line answers, risk assessment, clear recommendations
- **Format preference**: 1-page briefs, BLUF format, visual summaries
- **Detail tolerance**: Low — conclusions and implications only
- **Jargon tolerance**: Low — plain language required
- **Time budget**: 2-5 minutes per deliverable
- **Key question**: "What should we do and why?"

**1b. Operational Decision Makers**
- **Role**: VPs, directors, senior managers
- **Needs**: Findings with supporting evidence, options with tradeoffs
- **Format preference**: 3-5 page reports, structured sections
- **Detail tolerance**: Medium — key evidence, not exhaustive
- **Jargon tolerance**: Medium — domain terms acceptable
- **Time budget**: 10-20 minutes per deliverable
- **Key question**: "What are our options and what does the evidence say?"

### 2. Technical Consumers

**2a. Subject Matter Experts**
- **Role**: Engineers, analysts, domain specialists
- **Needs**: Full evidence chains, methodology, raw data access
- **Format preference**: Detailed reports, evidence tables, appendices
- **Detail tolerance**: High — want to verify independently
- **Jargon tolerance**: High — expect precise terminology
- **Time budget**: 30-60 minutes, will deep-read relevant sections
- **Key question**: "Is the methodology sound and the evidence sufficient?"

**2b. Research Peers**
- **Role**: Other researchers, analysts continuing the work
- **Needs**: Full research package including dead ends and working hypotheses
- **Format preference**: Research logs, source indices, raw notes
- **Detail tolerance**: Exhaustive — need everything to continue
- **Jargon tolerance**: High
- **Time budget**: Hours — will consume entire package
- **Key question**: "What was tried, what worked, what is left to explore?"

### 3. Compliance and Governance

**3a. Legal/Regulatory Stakeholders**
- **Role**: General counsel, compliance officers, regulatory affairs
- **Needs**: Precise citations, verifiable claims, risk quantification
- **Format preference**: Evidence tables, structured compliance matrices
- **Detail tolerance**: High for citations, low for speculation
- **Jargon tolerance**: Legal/regulatory terms expected, tech jargon explained
- **Time budget**: Variable — will focus on risk-relevant sections
- **Key question**: "What are the compliance implications and what is the evidence?"

**3b. Audit/Oversight**
- **Role**: Internal audit, external reviewers, board committees
- **Needs**: Methodology transparency, source provenance, decision trail
- **Format preference**: Structured reports with clear methodology disclosure
- **Detail tolerance**: Medium-High for process, low for domain detail
- **Jargon tolerance**: Low — process clarity valued over domain depth
- **Key question**: "Was the process rigorous and well-documented?"

### 4. External Stakeholders

**4a. Clients/Customers**
- **Needs**: Polished deliverables, actionable insights, professional presentation
- **Format preference**: Branded reports, presentations
- **Detail tolerance**: Varies by engagement — confirm at kickoff

**4b. Public/Media**
- **Needs**: Clear narratives, verifiable claims, quotable findings
- **Format preference**: Press-ready summaries, data visualizations
- **Detail tolerance**: Low — headline findings with backup available on request

## Stakeholder-to-Format Mapping

| Stakeholder | Primary Format | Fallback Format |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Executive DM | Executive brief | Decision card |
| Operational DM | Analysis report | Research brief |
| SME | Deep-dive report | Evidence tables |
| Research peer | Research package | Full report + logs |
| Legal/regulatory | Evidence table | Compliance matrix |
| Audit | Methodology disclosure | Process report |
| Client | Custom (per engagement) | Branded report |
