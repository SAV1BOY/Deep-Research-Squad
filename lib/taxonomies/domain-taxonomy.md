# Domain Taxonomy

## Purpose

Classifies research domains to guide source selection, methodology choice, and
expertise requirements. Helps researchers calibrate their approach based on domain.

## Categories

### 1. Technology and Engineering
Technical systems, tools, and infrastructure.

**Subcategories:**
- **Databases and Storage** - Relational, NoSQL, object storage, file systems
- **Distributed Systems** - Consensus, replication, partitioning, fault tolerance
- **Cloud Infrastructure** - Cloud providers, IaC, containers, orchestration
- **Programming Languages** - Language features, ecosystems, runtime performance
- **DevOps and SRE** - CI/CD, monitoring, incident response, reliability
- **Security** - Authentication, encryption, vulnerability assessment, compliance
- **AI and Machine Learning** - Models, training, inference, MLOps

**Examples:** Evaluating Kubernetes vs. Nomad, comparing encryption libraries

### 2. Product and Design
User-facing product decisions and design choices.

**Subcategories:**
- **User Experience** - Usability, accessibility, interaction design
- **Product Strategy** - Market fit, feature prioritization, roadmap planning
- **Analytics and Metrics** - KPI definition, measurement, A/B testing

**Examples:** Researching onboarding flow best practices, evaluating analytics platforms

### 3. Business and Strategy
Organizational and market-level research.

**Subcategories:**
- **Market Analysis** - Competitive landscape, market sizing, trends
- **Vendor Evaluation** - Pricing, SLA comparison, vendor stability
- **Regulatory and Compliance** - GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2, industry regulations
- **Cost Analysis** - TCO, ROI, build vs. buy

**Examples:** Comparing SaaS vendor pricing models, assessing GDPR compliance requirements

### 4. Science and Research
Academic or scientific investigation.

**Subcategories:**
- **Computer Science** - Algorithms, data structures, complexity theory
- **Data Science** - Statistical methods, data modeling, visualization
- **Domain-Specific Science** - Biology, physics, chemistry, as relevant to the project

**Examples:** Evaluating consensus algorithm correctness proofs, statistical test selection

### 5. Process and Methodology
How teams work, organize, and deliver.

**Subcategories:**
- **Software Development** - Agile, testing strategies, code review practices
- **Architecture** - System design, patterns, trade-off analysis
- **Team and Organization** - Team structure, hiring, knowledge management

**Examples:** Researching trunk-based development practices, evaluating testing frameworks

## Usage

Tag each research question with its domain from this taxonomy. Use this to:
- Identify subject matter experts needed for the research
- Select appropriate source types (academic papers for science, vendor docs for tech)
- Calibrate evidence standards (engineering may accept benchmarks; science needs rigor)
- Determine currency requirements (technology domains need recent sources)
- Guide cross-domain considerations (tech decisions often have business implications)
