# Deliverable Taxonomy

## Purpose

Classifies research output types into a structured hierarchy. Ensures consistent
naming, appropriate scope, and correct format selection for each deliverable.
Maps deliverable types to stakeholder needs and research maturity levels.

## Deliverable Categories

### 1. Briefs
Short-form deliverables optimized for speed-to-decision.

| Deliverable | Length | Audience | Maturity Level | Description |
|-------------|--------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| Executive Brief | 1 page | C-suite | Level 2-4 | BLUF + key findings + recommendation |
| Decision Brief | 1-2 pages | Operational DM | Level 2-3 | Options with tradeoffs, tied to evidence |
| Crisis Brief | 1-2 pages | Executive + Ops | Level 1-2 | Rapid-response: facts, impact, actions |
| Research Brief | 2-3 pages | Mixed | Level 2-3 | Structured findings with evidence summary |

### 2. Reports
Medium-to-long-form deliverables with full evidence presentation.

| Deliverable | Length | Audience | Maturity Level | Description |
|-------------|--------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| Deep Dive Report | 10-30 pages | Mixed | Level 3-4 | Comprehensive analysis with full evidence chain |
| Market Map | 5-15 pages | Strategic | Level 2-3 | Landscape visualization with company/segment mapping |
| Competitive Analysis | 5-20 pages | Strategic | Level 2-4 | Competitor positioning, strengths, vulnerabilities |
| Technical Evaluation | 5-15 pages | Technical | Level 2-4 | Technology assessment with criteria-based scoring |
| Due Diligence Pack | 15-40 pages | Investment/M&A | Level 3-4 | Comprehensive target assessment across dimensions |

### 3. Structured Artifacts
Data-oriented deliverables with tabular or structured format.

| Deliverable | Length | Audience | Maturity Level | Description |
|-------------|--------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| Evidence Table | 2-10 pages | SME, Legal | Level 2-4 | Structured evidence with citations and confidence |
| Timeline Map | 3-8 pages | Mixed | Level 2-3 | Chronological event/trend mapping |
| Scenario Memo | 5-12 pages | Strategic | Level 3-4 | Scenario matrix with probabilities and implications |
| Claim Verification | 1-3 pages | Any | Level 1-3 | Single claim assessed with evidence for/against |

### 4. Internal/Process Artifacts
Deliverables for research process management.

| Deliverable | Length | Audience | Maturity Level | Description |
|-------------|--------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| Research Log | Variable | Research peer | Level 0-4 | Running record of research process |
| Source Index | Variable | Research peer | Level 1-4 | Cataloged sources with metadata |
| Methodology Disclosure | 1-2 pages | Audit, peer | Level 2-4 | How research was conducted |
| Knowledge Transfer Pack | Variable | Research peer | Level 2-4 | Full handoff package for continuation |

## Selection Guide

```
What is the primary need?
├── Quick decision support → Briefs
│   ├── Board/C-suite audience → Executive Brief
│   ├── Choose between options → Decision Brief
│   ├── Urgent/time-critical → Crisis Brief
│   └── General research output → Research Brief
├── Deep understanding → Reports
│   ├── Single topic, comprehensive → Deep Dive Report
│   ├── Market landscape → Market Map
│   ├── Competitor intelligence → Competitive Analysis
│   ├── Technology assessment → Technical Evaluation
│   └── Acquisition/investment → Due Diligence Pack
├── Structured data presentation → Structured Artifacts
│   ├── Evidence compilation → Evidence Table
│   ├── Chronological mapping → Timeline Map
│   ├── Future scenarios → Scenario Memo
│   └── Single claim check → Claim Verification
└── Process documentation → Internal Artifacts
```

## Deliverable Quality Requirements

| Category | Min Sources | Confidence Required | Peer Review | Methodology Disclosure |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------|----------------------|
| Briefs | 3 | Score stated | Optional | Summary |
| Reports | 8 | Score + justification | Recommended | Required |
| Structured Artifacts | 5 | Per-item scoring | Optional | Required |
| Internal Artifacts | N/A | N/A | N/A | Implicit |
