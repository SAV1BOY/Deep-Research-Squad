# Operator Tone Profile

## Purpose

This profile defines the writing voice for operational and decision-support deliverables.
Every output should enable action. If the reader cannot do something with it, rewrite it.

## Core Principles

1. **Orient toward decisions.** Every section should map to a choice or action.
2. **Provide options, not just analysis.** Present alternatives with trade-offs.
3. **Quantify when possible.** Time, cost, probability, impact.
4. **Include ownership and timelines.** Who does what by when.
5. **Anticipate execution risks.** Flag what could go wrong and how to mitigate.

## Voice Characteristics

| Attribute         | Target                                |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Sentence length   | 10-18 words average                   |
| Paragraph length  | 2-4 sentences                         |
| Jargon            | Operational terms, defined if cross-team |
| Passive voice     | Avoid; assign clear ownership         |
| Hedging           | Minimal; state confidence and move on |

## Structure Pattern

- **Situation:** What is happening and why it matters now.
- **Options:** Two to four courses of action with pros, cons, and costs.
- **Recommendation:** The preferred option with rationale.
- **Execution Plan:** Steps, owners, timelines, dependencies.
- **Risk Register:** Top risks with likelihood, impact, and mitigation.
- **Decision Required:** Explicit statement of what needs to be decided.

## Decision Framework Language

- "Option A achieves X at the cost of Y. Option B achieves Z with risk W."
- "We recommend Option A because..."
- "The decision point is whether to prioritize speed or coverage."
- "If [condition], then pivot to [alternative]."

## Language Dos

- "The recommended next step is..."
- "Owner: [Name/Role]. Deadline: [Date]."
- "This requires a decision by [date] because..."
- "The primary execution risk is... Mitigation: ..."

## Language Don'ts

- Analysis without action implications
- Vague timelines ("soon," "in the near future")
- Unassigned tasks
- Recommendations without trade-off discussion

## When to Use

- Decision memos
- Operational playbooks
- Sprint planning inputs
- Risk assessments
- Go/no-go documents

## Quality Check

Before delivering in operator tone, verify:
- Can the reader identify the decision to be made?
- Are options presented with clear trade-offs?
- Does every action item have an owner and a deadline?
- Are risks identified with mitigations?
- Is the next step unambiguous?
