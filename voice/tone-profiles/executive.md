# Executive Tone Profile

## Purpose

This profile defines the writing voice for executive-facing deliverables.
Every sentence must earn its place. If it does not advance the argument, cut it.

## Core Principles

1. **Lead with the conclusion.** The first paragraph answers the question.
2. **Support with evidence, not opinion.** Every claim links to a source or data point.
3. **Be decisive.** State what the evidence supports. Do not hedge without reason.
4. **Eliminate noise.** Remove filler words, caveats that add no information, and redundant context.
5. **Respect the reader's time.** Assume the reader has 90 seconds.

## Voice Characteristics

| Attribute     | Target                        |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| Sentence length | 12-20 words average          |
| Paragraph length | 2-4 sentences               |
| Jargon        | Minimal; define if necessary  |
| Passive voice | Avoid unless attribution is unclear |
| Hedging       | Only when confidence is genuinely low |

## Structure Pattern

- **Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF):** One to two sentences with the core finding.
- **Supporting Evidence:** Two to four bullets with the strongest data points.
- **Implications:** What this means for decision-making.
- **Recommendation:** Clear, actionable next step.
- **Confidence & Caveats:** Stated at the end, not woven into every sentence.

## Language Dos

- "The evidence indicates X."
- "We recommend Y based on Z."
- "Three factors drive this conclusion."
- "The primary risk is..."

## Language Don'ts

- "It could potentially be the case that..."
- "There are many factors to consider..."
- "It is interesting to note..."
- "As previously mentioned..."

## When to Use

- Executive briefs and memos
- Board-level summaries
- Decision documents
- Escalation reports

## Quality Check

Before delivering in executive tone, verify:
- Can the reader act on the first paragraph alone?
- Is every sentence necessary?
- Are confidence levels stated once, clearly?
- Does the document respect the 90-second rule?
