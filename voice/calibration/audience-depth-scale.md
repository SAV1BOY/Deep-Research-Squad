# Audience Depth Scale

## Purpose

This guide defines how to calibrate the depth and detail of deliverables
based on the seniority and role of the audience. Different audiences need
different levels of detail, context, and abstraction.

## The Five Audience Levels

### Level 1: Analyst

**Profile:** Domain expert who will use the research as input to their own work.
**What they need:** Full evidence, methodology, data, and nuance.
**What they skip:** Background context they already have.

| Attribute        | Guideline                           |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Length           | Full report (no compression)        |
| Evidence detail  | Complete with source annotations    |
| Methodology      | Fully described and reproducible    |
| Caveats          | Inline with findings                |
| Jargon           | Domain-specific terms expected      |
| Visuals          | Data-dense charts, tables           |

---

### Level 2: Manager

**Profile:** Oversees analysts and translates research into team actions.
**What they need:** Key findings, confidence levels, and team implications.
**What they skip:** Granular methodology and raw data.

| Attribute        | Guideline                           |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Length           | 3-5 pages or structured brief       |
| Evidence detail  | Summarized with source counts       |
| Methodology      | One paragraph overview              |
| Caveats          | Grouped at the end                  |
| Jargon           | Moderate; define cross-domain terms |
| Visuals          | Summary charts, trend lines         |

---

### Level 3: VP / Director

**Profile:** Makes resource allocation and prioritization decisions.
**What they need:** Findings, implications, options, and recommendations.
**What they skip:** How the research was conducted.

| Attribute        | Guideline                           |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Length           | 1-2 pages                           |
| Evidence detail  | Top 3 supporting points             |
| Methodology      | Not included; available on request  |
| Caveats          | One confidence statement             |
| Jargon           | Minimal; business language           |
| Visuals          | One chart or table maximum           |

---

### Level 4: C-Level

**Profile:** Sets strategic direction. Reads dozens of inputs daily.
**What they need:** Conclusion, risk, and recommended action.
**What they skip:** Everything else.

| Attribute        | Guideline                           |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Length           | Half page or 1-3-1 format           |
| Evidence detail  | Implied by confidence statement     |
| Methodology      | Not included                        |
| Caveats          | One sentence                         |
| Jargon           | None                                 |
| Visuals          | One number or one visual             |

---

### Level 5: Board

**Profile:** Governance and fiduciary oversight. Quarterly cadence.
**What they need:** Strategic narrative with risk framing.
**What they skip:** Operational detail.

| Attribute        | Guideline                           |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Length           | 3-5 bullet points or one slide      |
| Evidence detail  | "Based on comprehensive analysis"   |
| Methodology      | Not included                        |
| Caveats          | Integrated into risk language        |
| Jargon           | None                                 |
| Visuals          | One high-level visual                |

## Calibration Rules

1. **Ask who is reading.** Never write without knowing the audience level.
2. **Layer the document.** BLUF for Level 4-5, detail below for Level 1-2.
3. **Offer drill-down.** "See full analysis" links for those who want depth.
4. **Match visual complexity to audience.** Data-dense for analysts, minimal for C-Level.
5. **When audience is mixed, default to the most senior level** and append detail.
