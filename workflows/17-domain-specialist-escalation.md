# Workflow 17: Domain Specialist Escalation

## Purpose
Define when and how to activate a domain specialist agent. Establish clear escalation criteria so the squad does not under-escalate (missing important domain nuance) or over-escalate (wasting specialist capacity on routine questions). Provide a structured protocol for specialist engagement.

## Trigger
- Intake identifies domain flags requiring specialist knowledge (Workflow 00).
- Collection team encounters domain-specific barriers (Workflow 04).
- Evidence grading requires domain expertise to assess quality (Workflow 05).
- Contradiction resolution needs domain knowledge (Workflow 06).
- Model building requires domain validation (Workflow 11).

## Agents Involved
- **Research Lead**: Approves the escalation decision and assigns the specialist.
- **Escalating Agent**: The agent who identifies the need and initiates escalation.
- **Domain Specialist**: The specialist agent activated to provide expertise.
- **Sprint Coordinator**: Adjusts timelines and resource allocation for specialist involvement.

## Inputs
- Escalation request with specific question or problem description.
- Relevant evidence and context gathered so far.
- Research plan and question tree for scope context.
- Domain specialist availability roster.

## Steps

1. **Apply escalation criteria checklist**: Before escalating, the requesting agent must confirm at least one criterion is met:
   - **Domain vocabulary barrier**: Key terms or concepts are ambiguous without domain expertise.
   - **Methodology uncertainty**: Unsure whether the analytical approach is valid for this domain.
   - **Source evaluation gap**: Cannot assess source credibility without domain knowledge.
   - **Regulatory or legal complexity**: Findings may have legal implications that require expert interpretation.
   - **Technical depth requirement**: The subquestion requires technical knowledge beyond the generalist level.
   - **Contradiction requiring domain judgment**: Two sources disagree and resolution requires domain expertise.
   - **High stakes**: The research directly informs a high-consequence decision and domain accuracy is critical.
   - Document which criterion or criteria are met.

2. **Prepare the escalation brief**: Before contacting the specialist, prepare:
   - **Specific question**: Exactly what domain knowledge is needed (not "help me with this topic").
   - **Context summary**: What the research is about, what has been found so far, and where the gap is.
   - **Evidence package**: Relevant evidence collected, with grades and any contradictions.
   - **Desired output**: What form should the specialist's response take (validation, analysis, recommendation)?
   - **Time constraint**: When is the specialist input needed by?

3. **Route to the right specialist**: Match the domain need to the specialist roster:
   - Check specialist availability and current commitments.
   - If the exact domain specialist is unavailable, identify the closest available alternative.
   - If no suitable specialist exists, flag this as a capability gap for future squad development.
   - Assign the specialist and notify them.

4. **Specialist onboarding**: Provide the specialist with the escalation brief and:
   - Walk through the research context if needed (brief call or document review).
   - Clarify the scope of their involvement (specific question only, not the entire research).
   - Agree on deliverable format and deadline.
   - Establish a communication channel for follow-up questions.

5. **Specialist performs domain analysis**: The specialist works on the assigned question:
   - Reviews the evidence package with domain expertise.
   - Identifies domain-specific nuances the generalist team may have missed.
   - Assesses source credibility from a domain perspective.
   - Provides domain-grounded analysis or validation.

6. **Specialist delivers structured response**: The specialist provides:
   - Direct answer to the escalation question.
   - Confidence level in their assessment.
   - Key domain context the research team should know.
   - Caveats or limitations of their analysis.
   - Recommendations for how to proceed.
   - Any additional sources or references the team should consult.

7. **Integrate specialist input**: The research team receives the specialist response and:
   - Incorporates domain insights into the evidence registry.
   - Updates evidence grades where specialist input changes the assessment.
   - Resolves contradictions using specialist judgment (with documentation).
   - Adjusts models or synthesis as needed.

8. **Document the escalation**: Record the full escalation in the research log:
   - What triggered the escalation (which criterion).
   - What question was asked.
   - What the specialist provided.
   - How it changed the research output.
   - Time elapsed for the escalation cycle.

9. **Evaluate escalation effectiveness**: After integration, assess:
   - Did the specialist input materially improve the research quality?
   - Was the escalation necessary, or could it have been avoided with better generalist preparation?
   - Was the escalation timely, or did it cause delays?
   - Feed this evaluation into the methodology registry.

10. **Release specialist**: Once input is integrated and any follow-up questions are resolved:
    - Thank the specialist and confirm no further questions.
    - Update the specialist availability roster.
    - Close the escalation formally in the research log.

## Quality Gates
- At least one escalation criterion must be documented and confirmed before escalation proceeds.
- Escalation brief must be complete with specific question, context, evidence, desired output, and deadline.
- Specialist response must include confidence level and caveats.
- Integration of specialist input must be traceable in the evidence registry.
- Escalation effectiveness must be evaluated and recorded.
- Specialist must be formally released when engagement ends.

## Outputs
- Specialist analysis and response (structured document).
- Updated evidence registry with specialist-informed grades and assessments.
- Escalation log entry (trigger, question, response, impact, effectiveness).
- Updated contradiction resolutions (if applicable).
- Specialist availability roster update.
