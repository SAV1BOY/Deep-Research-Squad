# Domain Activation Rules

## Purpose

This document defines when and how to activate Domain Specialist agents.
Not every research question requires domain expertise. These rules ensure
specialists are engaged when they add value and not used as a default.

## When to Activate a Domain Specialist

### Mandatory Activation

Activate a Domain Specialist when:

1. **The question requires domain-specific terminology or frameworks** that
   a general analyst would not reliably handle.
2. **Regulatory or compliance dimensions exist** that require jurisdiction-specific
   knowledge (activate Regulatory Scanner).
3. **The analysis involves quantitative modeling** beyond descriptive statistics
   (activate Quantitative Analyst).
4. **Competitive intelligence is a primary input** and requires structured
   monitoring methodology (activate Competitive Intelligence).
5. **The question involves predicting outcomes** under uncertainty with multiple
   scenarios (activate Scenario Planner).

### Recommended Activation

Consider activating when:

- The research topic is in a rapidly evolving field where state-of-the-art
  awareness matters.
- Stakeholder feedback on prior deliverables indicated insufficient domain depth.
- The Methodology Advisor flags that the analytical approach needs domain input.
- Cross-domain synthesis requires a bridge between two specialist areas.

### When NOT to Activate

Do not activate a Domain Specialist when:

- The question is answerable with general research skills and public sources.
- The domain expertise needed is shallow and can be covered by the Deep Analyst.
- Time constraints do not permit the additional coordination overhead.
- The specialist would duplicate work already completed by another agent.

## Activation Protocol

### Step 1: Request from Research Director

The Research Director decides on activation based on the scope document.
Other agents may recommend activation, but the Director approves.

### Step 2: Briefing the Specialist

Provide the specialist with:
- The specific sub-question they are addressing.
- The overall research context (one paragraph).
- What has already been found by other agents.
- The expected output format and deadline.
- Relevant source inventory from the Source Hunter.

### Step 3: Integration

The specialist's output feeds into the Synthesizer's work:
- Findings are tagged with confidence levels like any other agent.
- The specialist notes where their expertise adds context beyond the sources.
- Domain-specific terminology is defined for non-specialist readers.

### Step 4: Deactivation

The specialist is deactivated when:
- Their sub-question is answered.
- Their findings are integrated into the synthesis.
- Follow-up questions, if any, are documented for future activation.

## Domain Specialist Roster

| Specialist Area        | Activation Trigger                          |
|------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Technology / AI        | Technical architecture, model evaluation     |
| Financial Markets      | Valuation, market dynamics, instruments     |
| Healthcare / Biotech   | Clinical data, regulatory pathways          |
| Legal / Regulatory     | Jurisdiction-specific compliance            |
| Energy / Climate       | Technical feasibility, policy landscape     |
| Geopolitics            | Country risk, political dynamics            |
| Consumer / Retail      | Demand patterns, brand dynamics             |

## Quality Check

- Was the activation decision justified and documented?
- Was the specialist adequately briefed?
- Were findings integrated with consistent confidence tagging?
- Was deactivation clean (no loose threads)?
