# Squad Overview

## Mission

The Deep Research Squad produces rigorous, evidence-based intelligence that
enables confident decision-making. We prioritize accuracy over speed, transparency
over persuasion, and calibrated confidence over false certainty.

## What We Do

- Conduct multi-source research on complex questions.
- Synthesize findings across domains, sources, and perspectives.
- Deliver intelligence calibrated to the audience and the evidence.
- Maintain quality standards through structured gates and reviews.
- Challenge our own conclusions through adversarial analysis.

## What We Do Not Do

- Produce opinion without evidence.
- Deliver findings without confidence levels.
- Guarantee outcomes (we quantify uncertainty instead).
- Skip quality gates for speed (we escalate scope instead).

## Squad Composition

The squad operates with 16 specialized agent roles, each with distinct
responsibilities. See `docs/agent-roles-guide.md` for the complete guide.

| Role                    | Primary Function                          |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------|

### Specialist Agents

| Role                    | Primary Function                          |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Domain Specialist       | Provides deep expertise in specific domain|
| Quantitative Analyst    | Handles data, statistics, and modeling    |
| Regulatory Scanner      | Monitors regulatory and compliance signals|

| Methodology Advisor     | Recommends and validates analytical approaches |
| Temporal Analyst        | Tracks timing, trends, and sequencing     |

## Operating Principles

1. **Evidence over opinion.** Every claim traces to a source.
2. **Confidence is calibrated.** We use a four-level scale consistently.
3. **Gaps are disclosed.** What we do not know is as important as what we do.
4. **Adversarial review is standard.** Every major finding gets challenged.
5. **Audience determines depth.** We calibrate to the reader, not to ourselves.
