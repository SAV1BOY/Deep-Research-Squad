# Agent Roles Guide

## Purpose

This guide describes the 16 agent roles in the Deep Research Squad.
Each role has defined responsibilities, inputs, outputs, and quality criteria.

## Role Directory

### 1. Research Director

- **Function:** Scopes research questions, assigns agents, reviews final output.
- **Inputs:** Stakeholder request, context, constraints.
- **Outputs:** Research scope document, agent assignments, final sign-off.
- **Quality criteria:** Scope is clear, feasible, and tied to a decision.

### 2. Source Hunter

- **Function:** Identifies, locates, and pre-validates sources.
- **Inputs:** Research scope and question.
- **Outputs:** Source inventory with tier classification and access notes.
- **Quality criteria:** Source diversity, tier balance, access confirmation.

### 3. Deep Analyst

- **Function:** Conducts primary analysis on assigned sub-questions.
- **Inputs:** Scoped question, source inventory.
- **Outputs:** Findings document with evidence tags and citations.
- **Quality criteria:** Every finding is tagged, cited, and limitation-noted.

### 4. Synthesizer

- **Function:** Integrates findings from multiple agents into unified intelligence.
- **Inputs:** Individual agent findings.
- **Outputs:** Synthesis document with five layers (see `synthesis-writing-style.md`).
- **Quality criteria:** Creates new understanding; maintains source traceability.

### 5. Red Team / Contrarian

- **Function:** Stress-tests conclusions through adversarial analysis.
- **Inputs:** Draft findings or synthesis.
- **Outputs:** Challenge document with alternative theses and falsification criteria.
- **Quality criteria:** Steel-mans before challenging; evidence-based objections.

### 6. Quality Gate Keeper

- **Function:** Enforces quality standards at each gate.
- **Inputs:** Deliverable at gate checkpoint.
- **Outputs:** Pass/fail decision with specific feedback.
- **Quality criteria:** Consistent application of gate criteria.

### 7. Evidence Auditor

- **Function:** Verifies evidence tiers, citation accuracy, and source claims.
- **Inputs:** Draft deliverable with citations.
- **Outputs:** Audit report noting discrepancies and tier adjustments.
- **Quality criteria:** Every citation checked, tiers verified.

### 8. Executive Translator

- **Function:** Compresses research into executive-ready formats.
- **Inputs:** Completed and reviewed research.
- **Outputs:** Executive brief, presentation, or Slack summary.
- **Quality criteria:** Preserves confidence levels; enables decisions.

### 9. Delivery Coordinator

- **Function:** Manages output format, timing, and channel selection.
- **Inputs:** Completed deliverable, audience profile.
- **Outputs:** Formatted and delivered output.
- **Quality criteria:** Right format, right channel, right time.

### 10. Domain Specialist

- **Function:** Provides deep domain expertise on activated topics.
- **Inputs:** Questions requiring domain knowledge.
- **Outputs:** Domain-specific analysis and context.
- **Quality criteria:** State-of-the-art awareness, precise terminology.

### 11. Quantitative Analyst

- **Function:** Handles statistical analysis, modeling, and data interpretation.
- **Inputs:** Data sets, quantitative questions.
- **Outputs:** Statistical findings, models, data visualizations.
- **Quality criteria:** Methodology disclosed, limitations stated, reproducible.

### 12. Regulatory Scanner

- **Function:** Monitors regulatory and compliance developments.
- **Inputs:** Jurisdiction and topic scope.
- **Outputs:** Regulatory landscape summary with timeline and risk flags.
- **Quality criteria:** Current, jurisdiction-specific, actionable.

### 13. Competitive Intelligence

- **Function:** Tracks competitor activities, positioning, and signals.
- **Inputs:** Competitor list and monitoring scope.
- **Outputs:** Competitive landscape analysis with signal classification.
- **Quality criteria:** Source-verified, signal-vs-noise distinguished.

### 14. Scenario Planner

- **Function:** Develops and evaluates alternative futures.
- **Inputs:** Key uncertainties and driving forces.
- **Outputs:** Scenario set with probabilities and implications.
- **Quality criteria:** Scenarios are distinct, plausible, and decision-relevant.

### 15. Methodology Advisor

- **Function:** Recommends and validates analytical approaches.
- **Inputs:** Research question and available data.
- **Outputs:** Methodology recommendation with rationale.
- **Quality criteria:** Appropriate to the question, feasible, reproducible.

### 16. Temporal Analyst

- **Function:** Analyzes timing, trends, and event sequencing.
- **Inputs:** Time-series data, event logs, chronologies.
- **Outputs:** Trend analysis, timeline, temporal pattern identification.
- **Quality criteria:** Date-stamped, trend direction and rate quantified.
