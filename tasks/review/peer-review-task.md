# Peer Review Task

## Purpose
Conduct a structured peer review of research findings and methodology, providing independent critical evaluation to strengthen the quality and credibility of the deliverable before final release.

## When to Use
- Before finalizing any research deliverable for external consumption
- When the research involves novel methodologies or controversial conclusions
- When findings will inform high-stakes decisions
- As a standard quality gate in the research pipeline
- When a fresh perspective is needed on long-running research projects

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Contrarian Reviewer (or a qualified agent not involved in the original research)
- **Supporting**: Evidence Validator, Research Orchestrator
- **Consulted**: Domain Analyst (for domain-specific technical review)

## Inputs
- Complete draft of the research deliverable
- Methodology documentation and source inventory
- Evidence table and confidence map
- Original research plan and scope boundaries
- Quality criteria defined during planning phase

## Steps
1. Assign a reviewer who was not directly involved in producing the findings
2. Review the methodology for rigor, appropriateness, and completeness
3. Evaluate whether the evidence supports the stated conclusions
4. Check logical consistency across all sections of the deliverable
5. Assess whether alternative explanations were adequately considered
6. Verify that limitations and caveats are honestly stated
7. Evaluate the clarity and accessibility of the writing for the target audience
8. Score each section against the quality criteria from the research plan
9. Compile feedback into structured categories (critical, major, minor, suggestion)
10. Return the review to the original team for response and revision

## Quality Gates
- Reviewer is independent from the original research team
- Review covers methodology, evidence, logic, and presentation
- Feedback is categorized by severity and actionability
- Critical issues must be resolved before delivery, not just acknowledged
- The review process is documented for transparency
- Both strengths and weaknesses are noted, not just problems

## Outputs
- Structured peer review report with categorized feedback
- Section-by-section quality scores against defined criteria
- Critical issues list requiring mandatory resolution
- Suggested improvements with rationale
- Review process documentation (who reviewed, when, scope)
- Sign-off recommendation (approve, revise, or rework)

## Estimated Effort
- **Short deliverable, narrow scope**: 1-2 hours
- **Moderate deliverable**: 2-4 hours
- **Comprehensive multi-section report**: 4-6 hours

## Dependencies
- Requires near-final draft from `build-synthesis-report.md`
- Should follow `audit-research-quality.md` and bias/validation checks
- Outputs feed into final revisions before `prepare-final-delivery.md`
- May trigger additional cycles of `verify-claims.md` if issues are found
