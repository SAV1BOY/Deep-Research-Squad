# Run Pre-Mortem

## Purpose
Conduct a pre-mortem analysis on the research findings by imagining scenarios where the conclusions prove wrong, then working backward to identify the most likely failure modes and blind spots.

## When to Use
- Before finalizing and delivering high-stakes research conclusions
- When the research will inform significant decisions with major consequences
- When the research team has high confidence and groupthink risk is elevated
- As an advanced quality step beyond standard contrarian review
- When the cost of being wrong significantly exceeds the cost of additional analysis

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Contrarian Reviewer
- **Executing**: Domain Analyst, Evidence Validator
- **Supporting**: Research Orchestrator
- **Consulted**: All research agents (for diverse failure mode identification)

## Inputs
- Final or near-final research conclusions and recommendations
- Confidence map and evidence table
- Contrarian pass results
- Scenario analysis (if available)
- Stakeholder decision context and stakes

## Steps
1. State each major conclusion as if it has proven wrong six months from now
2. For each failed conclusion, brainstorm plausible reasons for the failure
3. Categorize failure modes (evidence gap, analytical error, bias, black swan, timing)
4. Assess the likelihood of each failure mode based on current evidence
5. Identify which failure modes were already addressed by existing validation steps
6. Identify which failure modes represent genuine remaining risks
7. For each unaddressed failure mode, assess whether mitigation is possible now
8. Determine what information, if available, would most reduce failure risk
9. Recommend specific actions to reduce the highest-likelihood failure modes
10. Identify early warning signals that would indicate a conclusion is failing
11. Suggest contingency plans for the most impactful failure scenarios
12. Compile the pre-mortem report with failure modes, mitigations, and monitoring plan

## Quality Gates
- Every major conclusion receives pre-mortem analysis
- Failure modes span multiple categories (not just one type of failure)
- Assessment includes failure modes the team wants to be true but might not be
- Likelihood assessments are honest and evidence-based
- Mitigations and early warning signals are specific and actionable
- The pre-mortem is conducted with genuine intellectual humility

## Outputs
- Pre-mortem analysis report
- Failure mode inventory with likelihood and impact ratings
- Unaddressed risk catalog
- Early warning signal dashboard
- Mitigation recommendations
- Contingency plan outlines for highest-impact failures

## Estimated Effort
- **Focused conclusions (2-3 major conclusions)**: 1-2 hours
- **Standard research (4-6 conclusions)**: 2-4 hours
- **Comprehensive research (7+ conclusions)**: 4-6 hours
