# Audit Research Quality

## Purpose
Conduct a comprehensive quality audit of the entire research effort, evaluating methodology, evidence quality, source diversity, analytical rigor, and output quality against defined standards.

## When to Use
- As a final quality gate before research delivery
- For periodic quality reviews of ongoing research programs
- When research quality concerns are raised by stakeholders
- As part of continuous improvement for the research squad
- When establishing quality baselines for new research domains

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: DeepResearch Chief
- **Executing**: Contrarian Analyst, Evidence Verifier
- **Supporting**: Source Hunter, Domain Specialist
- **Consulted**: All agents who contributed to the research

## Inputs
- Complete research deliverables (synthesis report, evidence table, all artifacts)
- Original research plan with quality criteria
- Source audit results
- Confidence map
- Contrarian pass results
- Quality standards and benchmarks from prior research

## Steps
1. Review the research plan for completeness and adherence during execution
2. Assess methodology quality (was the planned approach followed and appropriate?)
3. Evaluate evidence quality using the evidence table and source audit
4. Review analytical rigor (logic, reasoning, bias avoidance)
5. Assess completeness (were all questions in the tree addressed?)
6. Evaluate source quality and diversity against minimum standards
7. Review confidence calibration (are confidence levels justified by evidence?)
8. Check for internal consistency across all research outputs
9. Assess the quality of documentation and traceability
10. Compare output quality against benchmarks from prior research
11. Identify specific quality improvements for future research
12. Produce the quality audit report with scores, findings, and recommendations

## Quality Gates
- The audit covers all dimensions of research quality
- Scoring criteria are explicit and consistently applied
- Both strengths and weaknesses are documented
- Recommendations are specific and actionable
- The audit is conducted independently from the research execution team
- Comparison to benchmarks provides objective context for scores

## Outputs
- Quality audit report with dimension scores
- Methodology assessment
- Evidence quality summary
- Analytical rigor evaluation
- Completeness checklist results
- Improvement recommendations for future research
- Quality trend data (if prior audits exist)

## Estimated Effort
- **Focused research audit**: 1-2 hours
- **Standard research audit**: 2-4 hours
- **Comprehensive research audit**: 4-6 hours
