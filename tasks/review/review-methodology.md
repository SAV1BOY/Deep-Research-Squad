# Review Methodology

## Purpose
Evaluate the research methodology used in the investigation to assess whether the approaches, tools, and techniques were appropriate, well-executed, and sufficient to produce reliable findings.

## When to Use
- As part of the overall quality audit process
- When research findings are challenged on methodological grounds
- For retrospective learning after a research project completes
- When adapting methodology for a new domain or research type
- When establishing methodological standards for the squad

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Contrarian Analyst
- **Executing**: DeepResearch Chief, Evidence Verifier
- **Supporting**: Domain Specialist
- **Consulted**: Agents who executed the research methodology

## Inputs
- Research plan with planned methodology
- Actual methodology as executed (documented in research artifacts)
- Research findings and conclusions
- Evidence table and source registry
- Domain-specific methodological standards and best practices

## Steps
1. Document the methodology as actually executed (may differ from planned)
2. Compare planned versus actual methodology and document deviations
3. Assess whether the methodology was appropriate for the research questions
4. Evaluate search strategy effectiveness (were the right sources found?)
5. Assess data collection rigor (consistency, completeness, accuracy)
6. Review analytical methods for appropriateness and correct application
7. Evaluate validation and verification procedures
8. Assess whether methodology introduced systematic biases
9. Review triangulation effectiveness (were findings cross-validated?)
10. Compare methodology against domain best practices and standards
11. Identify methodological limitations and their impact on findings
12. Document lessons learned and recommendations for methodology improvement

## Quality Gates
- Methodology is documented as actually executed, not just as planned
- Deviations from the plan are explained with rationale
- Assessment criteria reference domain best practices
- Bias assessment is thorough and honest
- Limitations are connected to their potential impact on findings
- Recommendations are specific enough to improve future research

## Outputs
- Methodology review report
- Planned versus actual methodology comparison
- Methodological strengths and weaknesses assessment
- Bias risk assessment
- Limitation and impact analysis
- Improvement recommendations for future methodology
- Lessons learned documentation

## Estimated Effort
- **Simple methodology review**: 1-2 hours
- **Standard methodology review**: 2-3 hours
- **Deep methodology review**: 3-5 hours
