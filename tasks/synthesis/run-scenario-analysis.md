# Run Scenario Analysis

## Purpose
Develop and analyze multiple plausible future scenarios based on research findings, helping stakeholders understand the range of possible outcomes and prepare for uncertainty.

## When to Use
- When research conclusions have significant future-oriented implications
- For strategic planning that needs to account for multiple possible futures
- When key variables have high uncertainty and could evolve in different directions
- To stress-test strategies, plans, or decisions against different outcomes
- When trend analysis reveals multiple possible trajectories

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Synthesis Specialist
- **Executing**: Domain Specialist, Timeline Analyst
- **Supporting**: Contrarian Analyst, Data Researcher
- **Consulted**: DeepResearch Chief

## Inputs
- Validated research findings and trend analysis
- Causal map showing key drivers and relationships
- Confidence map highlighting areas of uncertainty
- Key uncertainties and variables that could evolve differently
- Stakeholder decision context and planning horizon

## Steps
1. Identify the key uncertainties that most influence outcomes
2. Select two to three high-impact, high-uncertainty variables as scenario axes
3. Define the plausible range for each variable (optimistic, baseline, pessimistic)
4. Construct three to five distinct scenarios by combining variable states
5. Develop a narrative for each scenario describing how it unfolds
6. Assess the plausibility of each scenario based on current evidence
7. Analyze the implications of each scenario for the stakeholder context
8. Identify indicators that would signal which scenario is materializing
9. Test current strategies or plans against each scenario
10. Identify robust strategies that perform acceptably across multiple scenarios
11. Map scenario-specific risks and opportunities
12. Compile scenario analysis with narratives, implications, and monitoring indicators

## Quality Gates
- Scenarios cover a meaningfully different range of outcomes
- Each scenario is internally consistent and plausible
- Scenarios are based on validated research findings, not speculation
- Key uncertainties are correctly identified from the evidence base
- Implications are specific and actionable for the stakeholder context
- Leading indicators are identified for real-time scenario monitoring

## Outputs
- Scenario descriptions with narratives and plausibility assessments
- Scenario comparison matrix
- Implication analysis per scenario
- Leading indicator dashboard for scenario monitoring
- Robust strategy recommendations
- Risk and opportunity maps per scenario

## Estimated Effort
- **Two-scenario analysis**: 2-4 hours
- **Three to four scenario analysis**: 4-6 hours
- **Comprehensive five-plus scenario analysis**: 6-10 hours
