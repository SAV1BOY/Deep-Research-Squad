# Build Synthesis Report

## Purpose
Integrate findings from all research layers, branches, and validation steps into a unified synthesis report that presents coherent conclusions, insights, and narratives grounded in validated evidence.

## When to Use
- After all collection and validation phases are complete
- When research findings from multiple branches need to be unified
- As the primary deliverable assembly step before final packaging
- When updating a previous synthesis with new evidence

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Research Orchestrator
- **Executing**: Domain Analyst, Synthesis Specialist
- **Supporting**: Evidence Validator
- **Reviewed by**: Contrarian Reviewer

## Inputs
- All validated findings organized by research branch
- Evidence table with confidence levels
- Contradiction map with resolution statuses
- Confidence map
- Source audit results
- Contrarian pass results
- Original research plan and question tree

## Steps
1. Review the original research question and all sub-questions from the question tree
2. Organize validated findings by theme and research branch
3. Identify cross-branch connections, patterns, and reinforcing findings
4. Construct the central narrative that answers the root research question
5. Integrate supporting evidence chains for each major conclusion
6. Address contradictions and explain how they were resolved or accommodated
7. Present confidence levels alongside each finding and conclusion
8. Highlight unexpected findings, surprises, and emergent insights
9. Document remaining uncertainties, gaps, and areas for further research
10. Structure the report with executive summary, detailed findings, and appendices
11. Ensure every conclusion is traceable to the evidence table
12. Review the synthesis for internal consistency and logical coherence

## Quality Gates
- The synthesis addresses every sub-question in the question tree
- All major conclusions are traceable to validated evidence
- Confidence levels are presented alongside findings, not hidden
- Contradictions are addressed transparently, not omitted
- Cross-branch insights are identified and highlighted
- The narrative is internally consistent and logically coherent

## Outputs
- Complete synthesis report with executive summary
- Findings organized by theme and branch
- Cross-branch insight analysis
- Uncertainty and gap documentation
- Evidence traceability appendix
- Recommendations for further research

## Estimated Effort
- **Focused research (3-4 branches)**: 2-4 hours
- **Moderate research (5-7 branches)**: 4-8 hours
- **Comprehensive research (8+ branches)**: 8-14 hours
