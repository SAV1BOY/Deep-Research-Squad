# Build Decision Brief

## Purpose
Distill research findings into a focused decision brief that presents the key information a decision-maker needs: options, trade-offs, risks, and a recommendation with supporting rationale.

## When to Use
- When research is intended to support a specific decision
- When stakeholders need actionable output, not just information
- For presenting options analysis with clear trade-offs
- When time-pressed decision-makers need the essential findings quickly
- As a companion to detailed research reports for executive audiences

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Research Orchestrator
- **Executing**: Synthesis Specialist, Domain Analyst
- **Supporting**: Evidence Validator
- **Reviewed by**: Contrarian Reviewer

## Inputs
- Synthesis report with validated findings
- Confidence map with levels for all findings
- Decision context (who is deciding, what are the options, what are the constraints)
- Stakeholder priorities and risk tolerance
- Scenario analysis results (if available)

## Steps
1. Clarify the specific decision to be made and the decision-maker's context
2. Define the decision criteria based on stakeholder priorities
3. Identify and describe all viable options or courses of action
4. For each option, summarize supporting evidence and confidence level
5. Analyze trade-offs between options across all decision criteria
6. Assess risks associated with each option using research findings
7. Identify irreversible consequences and option value considerations
8. Map each option against scenario analysis outcomes (if available)
9. Formulate a recommendation with clear rationale tied to evidence
10. Document dissenting views or contrarian considerations
11. Specify what additional information would change the recommendation
12. Format the brief for rapid consumption by the decision-maker

## Quality Gates
- The decision question is precisely stated
- All viable options are represented, not just the preferred one
- Trade-offs are presented objectively with evidence support
- Risks include both likelihood and impact dimensions
- The recommendation has explicit rationale tied to the evidence
- Dissenting views and conditions for changing the recommendation are documented

## Outputs
- Decision brief document (concise, typically 2-5 pages equivalent)
- Options comparison matrix
- Risk summary per option
- Recommendation with rationale and confidence level
- Dissenting views section
- Conditions that would change the recommendation

## Estimated Effort
- **Binary decision (two options)**: 1-2 hours
- **Multi-option decision (three to five options)**: 2-4 hours
- **Complex decision with scenarios**: 4-6 hours
