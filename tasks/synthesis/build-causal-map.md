# Build Causal Map

## Purpose
Construct a causal map that identifies and documents cause-and-effect relationships between factors discovered in the research, enabling stakeholders to understand the mechanisms driving observed outcomes.

## When to Use
- When research findings reveal complex interdependencies between factors
- For understanding why outcomes occur, not just what outcomes exist
- When stakeholders need to identify intervention points or leverage factors
- To support scenario analysis by mapping causal chains
- When policy or strategy recommendations require causal understanding

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Domain Analyst
- **Executing**: Synthesis Specialist, Deep Researcher
- **Supporting**: Evidence Validator
- **Reviewed by**: Contrarian Reviewer

## Inputs
- Validated research findings with identified factors and outcomes
- Evidence table with causal claims and their support levels
- Domain knowledge about known causal mechanisms
- Contradiction map (for conflicting causal claims)
- Confidence map for strength of causal evidence

## Steps
1. Extract all factors, variables, and outcomes from the research findings
2. Identify claimed or inferred causal relationships between factors
3. Classify each relationship by type (direct cause, contributing factor, necessary condition, catalyst)
4. Assess the evidence strength for each causal claim
5. Distinguish correlation from causation for each relationship
6. Map causal chains (A causes B causes C) and feedback loops
7. Identify root causes versus intermediate causes
8. Map reinforcing and balancing feedback loops
9. Identify key leverage points where intervention would have outsized impact
10. Validate causal claims against the evidence base and domain knowledge
11. Document uncertainty in causal relationships with confidence levels
12. Produce the visual causal map with annotations and evidence references

## Quality Gates
- Causal claims are distinguished from correlational observations
- Each causal relationship has documented evidence support
- Feedback loops are identified and correctly classified
- Confidence levels reflect actual evidence strength for causation
- Alternative causal explanations are considered and documented
- The map is internally consistent (no contradictory causal paths)

## Outputs
- Visual causal map with annotated relationships
- Causal relationship inventory with evidence and confidence levels
- Feedback loop analysis
- Root cause identification
- Leverage point analysis
- Causal uncertainty documentation

## Estimated Effort
- **Simple causal map (5-10 factors)**: 1-3 hours
- **Moderate causal map (10-20 factors)**: 3-5 hours
- **Complex causal map (20+ factors)**: 5-10 hours
