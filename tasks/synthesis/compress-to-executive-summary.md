# Compress to Executive Summary

## Purpose
Distill the full research output into a concise executive summary that communicates the most important findings, conclusions, and recommendations in a format suitable for senior stakeholders with limited time.

## When to Use
- As a standard deliverable for any substantial research effort
- When senior stakeholders need research highlights without full detail
- For briefing documents that precede deeper discussions
- When research findings need to be communicated across organizational levels
- As the lead section of a comprehensive research delivery package

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Research Orchestrator
- **Executing**: Synthesis Specialist
- **Supporting**: Domain Analyst
- **Reviewed by**: Contrarian Reviewer

## Inputs
- Complete synthesis report
- Decision brief (if applicable)
- Confidence map
- Key stakeholder priorities and information needs
- Target length and format requirements

## Steps
1. Identify the three to five most important findings from the research
2. Distill the core answer to the original research question in one to two sentences
3. Select the findings with the highest confidence and greatest relevance
4. Draft concise summaries of each key finding with evidence strength indicators
5. Include the most critical caveats, uncertainties, and limitations
6. Add the primary recommendation or conclusion with confidence level
7. Note any high-impact contradictions or surprises that stakeholders must know
8. Include two to three key metrics or data points that anchor the findings
9. Add a brief "what this means" interpretation for the stakeholder context
10. Review for length compliance and trim to meet target constraints
11. Ensure every statement in the summary is supported in the full report
12. Format for maximum readability (clear headers, bullet points, bold key terms)

## Quality Gates
- The summary accurately represents the full research findings
- No conclusion in the summary contradicts the detailed report
- Confidence levels and caveats are included, not omitted for brevity
- The summary stands alone (readable without the full report)
- Length meets the target constraint
- Key stakeholder questions are addressed

## Outputs
- Executive summary document (typically one to two pages equivalent)
- Key findings highlight list
- Critical caveats and limitations callout
- Link references to detailed sections in the full report
- Confidence level indicators for each key finding

## Estimated Effort
- **Short summary from focused research**: 30-60 minutes
- **Standard summary from moderate research**: 1-2 hours
- **Summary from comprehensive research**: 2-3 hours
