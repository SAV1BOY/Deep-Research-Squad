# Build Implications Map

## Purpose
Map the downstream implications of research findings across multiple dimensions (strategic, operational, financial, risk) to help stakeholders understand the full impact of what was discovered.

## When to Use
- When research findings have multi-dimensional consequences
- For strategic planning that needs to translate findings into action areas
- When stakeholders need to understand ripple effects of key findings
- To connect research insights to specific business or operational domains
- As input to decision briefs and recommendation development

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Synthesis Specialist
- **Executing**: Domain Specialist
- **Supporting**: Data Researcher, DeepResearch Chief
- **Reviewed by**: Contrarian Analyst

## Inputs
- Validated research findings with confidence levels
- Synthesis report or draft conclusions
- Causal map (if available)
- Stakeholder context (organization, strategy, operations)
- Domain knowledge about downstream impact channels

## Steps
1. List all significant research findings that have potential downstream impact
2. Define implication dimensions relevant to the stakeholder (strategic, operational, financial, risk, regulatory, competitive)
3. For each finding, trace implications across each dimension
4. Classify implications by timeframe (immediate, short-term, medium-term, long-term)
5. Assess the magnitude of each implication (transformative, significant, moderate, minor)
6. Identify implication chains where one implication triggers further consequences
7. Map interactions between implications across different dimensions
8. Identify critical implications that require immediate attention or action
9. Assess confidence in each implication based on underlying finding confidence
10. Highlight implications that create opportunities versus those that create threats
11. Identify implications that are contingent on specific conditions materializing
12. Compile the implications map with prioritization and confidence annotations

## Quality Gates
- Implications are traceable to specific validated findings
- Multiple dimensions are covered, not just the most obvious one
- Timeframe and magnitude assessments use consistent scales
- Implication chains are logically sound and evidence-based
- Both opportunities and threats are identified
- Confidence levels reflect the underlying finding confidence

## Outputs
- Implications map organized by dimension and timeframe
- Priority implications list with magnitude and confidence
- Implication chain analysis
- Opportunity and threat classification
- Critical implications requiring immediate attention
- Contingent implications with trigger conditions

## Estimated Effort
- **Focused findings (3-5 key findings)**: 1-3 hours
- **Moderate findings (6-10 key findings)**: 3-5 hours
- **Broad findings (10+ key findings)**: 5-8 hours
