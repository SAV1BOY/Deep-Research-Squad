# Executive Summary Task

## Purpose
Distill a comprehensive research report into a concise executive summary that communicates key findings, implications, and recommended actions for time-constrained decision-makers.

## When to Use
- After the full synthesis report is complete and validated
- When the audience includes senior leaders who need a condensed view
- When research findings need to be communicated across organizational levels
- When a quick-reference document is needed alongside the full report

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Synthesis Writer
- **Supporting**: DeepResearch Chief, Domain Specialist
- **Reviewed by**: Contrarian Analyst

## Inputs
- Completed synthesis report with validated findings
- Confidence map and evidence table
- Stakeholder alignment document (audience context and priorities)
- Implications map and decision brief (if available)
- Quality audit results and known limitations

## Steps
1. Review the full synthesis report and identify the three to five most consequential findings
2. Distill each key finding into a single clear statement with its confidence level
3. Summarize the supporting evidence for each finding in one to two sentences
4. Extract the most important implications and map them to stakeholder priorities
5. Draft actionable recommendations ranked by urgency and impact
6. State key uncertainties and limitations that affect the conclusions
7. Structure the summary with a clear narrative arc (situation, findings, implications, actions)
8. Ensure the summary stands alone without requiring the full report for comprehension
9. Review for jargon and adjust language to match the target audience
10. Validate that the summary accurately represents the full report without distortion

## Quality Gates
- Summary captures all critical findings, not just the most interesting ones
- Confidence levels are preserved from the full report, not inflated
- Recommendations are specific and actionable, not vague
- Limitations and uncertainties are stated, not omitted for brevity
- Language matches the target audience's expertise level
- Summary can be read independently and still conveys the core message

## Outputs
- Executive summary document (typically 1-3 pages)
- Key findings list with confidence ratings
- Prioritized recommendations with urgency indicators
- Critical uncertainties and limitations statement
- Reference pointers to full report sections for deeper reading

## Estimated Effort
- **Short report, narrow topic**: 30-60 minutes
- **Moderate report**: 1-2 hours
- **Comprehensive multi-domain report**: 2-4 hours
