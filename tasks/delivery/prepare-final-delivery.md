# Prepare Final Delivery

## Purpose
Assemble, quality-check, and package all research outputs into a complete, polished delivery package ready for the stakeholder, ensuring consistency, completeness, and professional presentation.

## When to Use
- After all research, validation, synthesis, and review phases are complete
- When preparing research outputs for stakeholder handoff
- When assembling multiple research artifacts into a unified package
- Before any formal presentation or distribution of research findings

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: DeepResearch Chief
- **Executing**: Synthesis Specialist, Domain Specialist
- **Supporting**: Source Hunter, Evidence Verifier
- **Reviewed by**: Contrarian Analyst (final pass)

## Inputs
- Synthesis report and executive summary
- Decision brief (if applicable)
- Evidence table, confidence map, and all validation artifacts
- Source map and bibliography
- Quality audit results
- Stakeholder delivery preferences (format, structure, level of detail)

## Steps
1. Assemble the complete inventory of research deliverables
2. Verify all required artifacts are complete and up to date
3. Check cross-references between documents for consistency
4. Ensure all conclusions in the summary match the detailed report
5. Verify that confidence levels are consistently presented across all outputs
6. Format all documents according to delivery standards and stakeholder preferences
7. Build the table of contents and navigation aids for the package
8. Add metadata (date, version, authors, scope, classification)
9. Perform a final proofreading pass for clarity and professionalism
10. Assemble supporting materials (raw data, methodology docs, source registry)
11. Create a delivery cover note summarizing the package contents
12. Perform final quality check and sign off on the complete package

## Quality Gates
- All planned deliverables are included in the package
- Cross-document consistency is verified (no contradictions between artifacts)
- Formatting is professional and consistent across all documents
- Metadata is complete and accurate
- The package is navigable and self-explanatory
- A final independent review confirms quality standards are met

## Outputs
- Complete delivery package with all research artifacts
- Delivery cover note with package summary
- Table of contents and navigation guide
- Version-controlled document set
- Supporting materials appendix
- Delivery confirmation record

## Estimated Effort
- **Small delivery (2-3 artifacts)**: 1-2 hours
- **Standard delivery (4-7 artifacts)**: 2-4 hours
- **Comprehensive delivery (8+ artifacts)**: 4-6 hours
