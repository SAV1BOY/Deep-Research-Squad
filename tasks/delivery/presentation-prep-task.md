# Presentation Prep Task

## Purpose
Transform validated research findings into a structured presentation format optimized for live or asynchronous delivery, ensuring the narrative is compelling, evidence-backed, and tailored to the audience.

## When to Use
- When research findings need to be presented to stakeholders in a meeting
- When an asynchronous presentation deck is requested alongside the report
- When findings need to be communicated to a non-technical audience
- When the delivery format requires a different structure than the written report

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Synthesis Writer
- **Supporting**: DeepResearch Chief, Domain Specialist
- **Consulted**: Contrarian Analyst (for anticipating tough questions)

## Inputs
- Finalized research report and executive summary
- Visual synthesis outputs (charts, diagrams, maps)
- Audience profile (role, expertise level, decision context)
- Time allocation for the presentation
- Stakeholder alignment document with priorities and acceptance criteria

## Steps
1. Define the presentation objective and the single most important takeaway
2. Analyze the audience to calibrate depth, language, and emphasis
3. Design the narrative arc: context, key findings, implications, recommendations, next steps
4. Select the three to five findings that best serve the audience's decision needs
5. Map supporting evidence to each finding at the appropriate level of detail
6. Integrate or adapt visual synthesis outputs for presentation format
7. Prepare an appendix of backup slides for anticipated deep-dive questions
8. Draft speaker notes or talking points for each section
9. Anticipate likely challenges and prepare evidence-backed responses
10. Rehearse the flow to confirm it fits within the allocated time

## Quality Gates
- Presentation tells a coherent story, not just a sequence of findings
- Key findings are supported by evidence within the slides, not just in notes
- Visual elements enhance comprehension rather than adding complexity
- Language and depth are calibrated to the audience's expertise level
- Backup materials cover the most likely follow-up questions
- Presentation fits within the allocated time with room for discussion

## Outputs
- Structured presentation deck or outline
- Speaker notes or talking points for each section
- Backup appendix slides for deep-dive questions
- Anticipated questions with prepared responses
- Handout version of key findings for distribution

## Estimated Effort
- **Short presentation (10-15 minutes)**: 1-2 hours
- **Standard presentation (30-45 minutes)**: 2-4 hours
- **Extended presentation with Q&A prep**: 4-6 hours

## Dependencies
- Requires completed `build-synthesis-report.md` and `executive-summary-task.md`
- Benefits from `visual-synthesis-task.md` outputs
- Should follow `peer-review-task.md` to incorporate final feedback
- Feeds into `present-research-findings.md` for actual delivery
