# Cross-Squad Research Handoff

## Purpose
Execute a structured handoff of research findings, context, and supporting materials to another squad or team, ensuring the receiving team can understand, use, and build upon the research effectively.

## When to Use
- When research outputs need to be consumed by another squad (e.g., strategy, product, engineering)
- When transferring ongoing research responsibility to another team
- For multi-squad projects where research feeds into downstream work
- When research findings need to be operationalized by a different team

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Research Orchestrator
- **Executing**: Synthesis Specialist, Domain Analyst
- **Supporting**: Source Curator
- **Receiving**: Designated contact from the receiving squad

## Inputs
- Complete research delivery package
- Receiving squad's context, needs, and preferred formats
- Handoff protocol requirements
- Outstanding questions or ongoing research threads
- Access requirements for source materials and data

## Steps
1. Identify the receiving squad and their designated contact
2. Understand the receiving squad's context and how they will use the research
3. Prepare a handoff briefing document tailored to the receiving squad's needs
4. Translate research findings into the receiving squad's domain language
5. Highlight the most actionable findings for the receiving squad's context
6. Document any caveats, limitations, or uncertainties critical for their use case
7. Package source materials and data access for the receiving team
8. Prepare a FAQ anticipating common questions about the research
9. Schedule and conduct a handoff briefing session
10. Transfer all research artifacts with clear organization and navigation
11. Establish a follow-up channel for questions that arise after handoff
12. Document the handoff completion and any agreed follow-up commitments

## Quality Gates
- The receiving squad confirms they understand the research findings
- Research is translated into the receiving squad's domain context
- Critical caveats and limitations are communicated prominently
- Source materials are accessible to the receiving team
- A follow-up channel is established for post-handoff questions
- The handoff is documented for institutional knowledge

## Outputs
- Handoff briefing document tailored to receiving squad
- Translated findings in receiving squad's domain language
- FAQ document for common questions
- Packaged research artifacts with access provisions
- Handoff completion record
- Follow-up channel and commitment log

## Estimated Effort
- **Simple handoff (single artifact)**: 30-60 minutes
- **Standard handoff (full package)**: 1-3 hours
- **Complex handoff (multi-squad, ongoing research)**: 3-5 hours
