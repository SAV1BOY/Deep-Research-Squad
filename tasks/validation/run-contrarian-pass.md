# Run Contrarian Pass

## Purpose
Challenge the research conclusions by systematically arguing against them, identifying weaknesses in reasoning, untested assumptions, and alternative explanations that the research may have overlooked.

## When to Use
- After initial synthesis or conclusions have been drafted
- As a mandatory quality step before final delivery of high-stakes research
- When the research team suspects confirmation bias may have influenced findings
- When conclusions seem too clean or unanimous (potential groupthink indicator)

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Contrarian Reviewer
- **Supporting**: Evidence Validator, Domain Analyst
- **Consulted**: Research Orchestrator
- **Observed by**: All agents involved in the research (for learning)

## Inputs
- Draft conclusions and synthesis from the research
- Evidence table and contradiction map
- Thesis test results (if available)
- List of assumptions underlying the conclusions
- Known limitations of the research methodology

## Steps
1. Review all conclusions and identify the strongest claims
2. For each conclusion, construct the strongest possible counter-argument
3. Identify assumptions that, if wrong, would invalidate each conclusion
4. Search for evidence that was available but not included in the analysis
5. Evaluate whether conclusions follow logically from the evidence presented
6. Test for common cognitive biases (confirmation, anchoring, availability, survivorship)
7. Identify alternative explanations for the same evidence
8. Assess whether the research adequately addressed known unknowns
9. Challenge the source selection for potential systematic bias
10. Evaluate whether conclusions are appropriately scoped (no over-generalization)
11. Rate each conclusion on a vulnerability scale (robust, moderate, fragile)
12. Compile contrarian report with challenges, vulnerabilities, and recommendations

## Quality Gates
- Every major conclusion receives a dedicated counter-argument attempt
- Counter-arguments are genuine and substantive, not strawman arguments
- Cognitive bias testing covers at least five common biases
- Alternative explanations are explored with the same rigor as primary conclusions
- The contrarian pass is conducted independently from the synthesis team
- Recommendations for strengthening are actionable and specific

## Outputs
- Contrarian analysis report with challenges to each conclusion
- Vulnerability ratings for each conclusion
- Alternative explanation catalog
- Assumption risk register
- Cognitive bias assessment results
- Recommendations for strengthening conclusions

## Estimated Effort
- **Focused research (3-5 conclusions)**: 1-3 hours
- **Moderate research (6-10 conclusions)**: 3-5 hours
- **Comprehensive research (10+ conclusions)**: 5-8 hours
