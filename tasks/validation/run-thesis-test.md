# Run Thesis Test

## Purpose
Rigorously test a thesis or hypothesis against the collected evidence, applying structured reasoning to determine whether the evidence supports, contradicts, or is inconclusive regarding the thesis.

## When to Use
- When research has produced a central thesis that needs validation
- When evaluating competing hypotheses about a phenomenon
- Before committing to a conclusion in the synthesis phase
- When stakeholders have a specific theory they want tested

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Contrarian Analyst
- **Executing**: Evidence Verifier, Domain Specialist
- **Supporting**: Data Researcher
- **Consulted**: DeepResearch Chief (for alignment with research goals)

## Inputs
- Clearly stated thesis or hypothesis to test
- Complete evidence table with confidence levels
- Contradiction map
- Alternative hypotheses to test against (if available)
- Criteria for thesis support, contradiction, and inconclusiveness

## Steps
1. State the thesis precisely in testable terms
2. Define what evidence would support the thesis (confirmation criteria)
3. Define what evidence would contradict the thesis (falsification criteria)
4. Define what would constitute inconclusive evidence
5. Catalog all evidence relevant to the thesis from the evidence table
6. Classify each piece of evidence as supporting, contradicting, or neutral
7. Weight evidence by quality, independence, and relevance
8. Calculate the overall evidence balance for and against the thesis
9. Test alternative hypotheses using the same evidence and methodology
10. Assess whether the thesis is the best explanation given all evidence
11. Identify conditions under which the thesis would fail (boundary conditions)
12. Produce a verdict with confidence level and supporting rationale

## Quality Gates
- The thesis is stated precisely enough to be testable
- Both confirmation and falsification criteria are defined before testing
- Evidence is evaluated against the thesis, not selected to support it
- Alternative hypotheses receive the same rigor of testing
- The verdict reflects the evidence balance, not prior beliefs
- Boundary conditions and limitations of the verdict are documented

## Outputs
- Thesis test report with verdict and confidence level
- Evidence classification table (supporting, contradicting, neutral)
- Alternative hypothesis comparison
- Boundary condition analysis
- Falsification risk assessment
- Recommendations for strengthening or revising the thesis

## Estimated Effort
- **Single thesis, clear evidence**: 1-2 hours
- **Single thesis, mixed evidence**: 2-4 hours
- **Multiple competing hypotheses**: 4-8 hours
