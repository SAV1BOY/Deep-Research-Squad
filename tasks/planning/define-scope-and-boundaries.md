# Define Scope and Boundaries

## Purpose
Establish clear boundaries for a research effort, specifying what is in scope, what is explicitly out of scope, and the rationale for each boundary decision to prevent scope creep and ensure focused execution.

## When to Use
- At the start of any new research plan before execution begins
- When a research effort is expanding beyond its original intent
- When stakeholders disagree on what the research should cover
- During plan revision when constraints change

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: DeepResearch Chief
- **Supporting**: Domain Specialist
- **Consulted**: Contrarian Analyst (to challenge boundary decisions)

## Inputs
- Core research question and sub-questions
- User constraints (time, budget, depth, breadth)
- Domain context and prior research
- Known unknowns and areas of uncertainty

## Steps
1. Restate the core research question in precise, unambiguous language
2. List all dimensions of the question (temporal, geographic, industry, technical, etc.)
3. For each dimension, define the included range (e.g., "2020-2025", "North America only")
4. For each dimension, explicitly state what is excluded and why
5. Identify boundary cases and decide inclusion or exclusion for each
6. Define depth limits for each branch of the question tree
7. Set a maximum number of sources per branch to prevent over-collection
8. Identify adjacent topics that might cause drift and mark them out of scope
9. Document assumptions that underpin boundary decisions
10. Review boundaries against the original request to ensure alignment
11. Get stakeholder confirmation on scope decisions
12. Record scope decisions in the research plan for reference during execution

## Quality Gates
- Every dimension of the question has explicit in/out scope markers
- Boundary decisions have documented rationale
- No critical aspect of the original question is inadvertently excluded
- Scope is achievable within the stated timeline and resources
- Adjacent topics are acknowledged even if excluded

## Outputs
- Scope definition document with in-scope and out-of-scope lists
- Boundary rationale log
- Assumptions register
- Dimension coverage matrix

## Estimated Effort
- **Narrow question**: 15-20 minutes
- **Moderate scope**: 30-60 minutes
- **Broad or multi-domain**: 1-2 hours
