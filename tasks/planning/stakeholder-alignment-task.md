# Stakeholder Alignment Task

## Purpose
Align research objectives, scope, and deliverables with stakeholder needs, ensuring that the research output addresses the right questions at the right depth for the intended audience.

## When to Use
- At project kickoff when multiple stakeholders have input on research direction
- When research scope is ambiguous or stakeholder expectations are unclear
- When conflicting priorities among stakeholders need resolution
- Before committing significant resources to a research track

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Research Orchestrator
- **Supporting**: Domain Analyst, Synthesis Architect
- **Consulted**: All agents assigned to the research plan

## Inputs
- Initial research request or brief from the requester
- List of stakeholders and their roles
- Any existing strategic documents, OKRs, or priority frameworks
- Prior research or context relevant to the topic
- Timeline and resource constraints

## Steps
1. Identify all stakeholders who will consume or act on the research output
2. Catalog each stakeholder's key questions, decision context, and success criteria
3. Map stakeholder priorities to detect overlaps, gaps, and conflicts
4. Reconcile conflicting priorities by clarifying trade-offs with the primary requester
5. Define the primary research objective and rank secondary objectives
6. Agree on output format, depth, and delivery timeline with stakeholders
7. Document acceptance criteria that stakeholders will use to evaluate the deliverable
8. Create a stakeholder communication plan with check-in milestones
9. Circulate the alignment summary for sign-off before research begins
10. Archive the alignment document for reference during synthesis and review phases

## Quality Gates
- Every stakeholder's core question is addressed in the research plan
- Conflicting priorities are explicitly resolved, not silently dropped
- Output format and depth match the decision context of the audience
- Acceptance criteria are measurable and agreed upon
- Communication cadence is documented and realistic

## Outputs
- Stakeholder alignment summary document
- Prioritized objective list with rationale
- Acceptance criteria checklist
- Communication and check-in schedule
- Conflict resolution log (if applicable)

## Estimated Effort
- **Single stakeholder**: 30-60 minutes
- **Multiple aligned stakeholders**: 1-2 hours
- **Conflicting stakeholder priorities**: 2-4 hours

## Dependencies
- Must be completed before `build-research-plan.md`
- Feeds into `define-scope-and-boundaries.md`
