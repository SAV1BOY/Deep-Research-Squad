# Risk Assessment Task

## Purpose
Identify and assess risks that could compromise research quality, timeline, or relevance, and define mitigation strategies to keep the research effort on track.

## When to Use
- During research planning for complex or high-stakes projects
- When the topic involves rapidly changing information or contested claims
- When resource constraints create risk of incomplete coverage
- When the research domain is unfamiliar to the assigned agents

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Research Orchestrator
- **Supporting**: Contrarian Reviewer, Evidence Validator
- **Consulted**: Domain Analyst, Source Curator

## Inputs
- Draft research plan with scope and timeline
- Question decomposition tree
- Source strategy document
- Agent assignment matrix
- Known constraints (time, access, expertise gaps)

## Steps
1. Review the research plan and identify areas with high uncertainty or complexity
2. Catalog risks across categories: source availability, data quality, timeline, expertise gaps, and scope creep
3. Assess each risk for likelihood (low/medium/high) and impact (low/medium/critical)
4. Identify leading indicators that would signal a risk is materializing
5. Define mitigation actions for each medium and high-priority risk
6. Assign risk owners responsible for monitoring each indicator
7. Establish fallback strategies for critical risks (alternative sources, reduced scope, extended timeline)
8. Document acceptable quality thresholds if mitigation fails
9. Schedule risk review checkpoints aligned with research milestones
10. Compile the risk register and integrate it into the research plan

## Quality Gates
- All major risk categories are evaluated, not just obvious ones
- Each risk has both likelihood and impact assessed
- Mitigation strategies are actionable, not generic
- Leading indicators are specific and observable
- Fallback plans exist for every critical-impact risk
- Risk owners are clearly assigned

## Outputs
- Risk register with likelihood, impact, and mitigation for each entry
- Risk monitoring checklist with leading indicators
- Fallback strategy document for critical risks
- Risk review schedule
- Updated research plan incorporating risk mitigations

## Estimated Effort
- **Low-complexity project**: 30-60 minutes
- **Moderate complexity**: 1-2 hours
- **High-stakes or multi-domain**: 2-3 hours

## Dependencies
- Requires completed draft of `build-research-plan.md`
- Informs `allocate-research-agents.md` if expertise gaps are identified
- Should be revisited after `run-pre-mortem.md`
