# Run Trend Analysis

## Purpose
Execute a structured trend analysis to identify, validate, and project patterns of change in a domain, market, or technology area, distinguishing signal from noise and assessing trend durability.

## When to Use
- When strategic planning requires understanding of directional changes
- For forecasting and scenario planning exercises
- When evaluating whether an observed pattern represents a durable trend
- To identify emerging trends before they reach mainstream awareness
- For periodic trend monitoring and update cycles

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Trend Analyst
- **Executing**: Deep Researcher, Market Researcher, Domain Analyst
- **Supporting**: Source Curator, Data Collector
- **Validating**: Contrarian Reviewer, Evidence Validator

## Inputs
- Domain or market to analyze for trends
- Time horizon for trend analysis (short, medium, long term)
- Known or suspected trends to validate
- Baseline data for comparison
- Stakeholder interests and decision context

## Steps
1. Define the trend analysis scope (domain, geography, timeframe)
2. Collect historical data and establish baseline patterns
3. Scan for signals of change across multiple source categories
4. Catalog observed signals and classify by strength and consistency
5. Cluster related signals into candidate trend hypotheses
6. Validate each candidate trend against multiple independent data points
7. Assess trend maturity (emerging, accelerating, peaking, declining)
8. Analyze driving forces behind each validated trend
9. Evaluate trend durability and potential for reversal
10. Project trend trajectories under different assumptions
11. Identify counter-trends and potential disruptions to current patterns
12. Compile trend analysis report with projections and confidence levels

## Quality Gates
- Trends are supported by multiple independent data points, not single anecdotes
- Historical baselines provide context for claimed changes
- Driving forces are identified and evidence-based
- Trend maturity assessments use consistent framework
- Counter-trends and reversal risks are documented
- Projections include explicit assumptions and confidence ranges

## Outputs
- Trend analysis report with validated trends
- Trend maturity assessments and lifecycle positioning
- Driving forces analysis per trend
- Trend projection scenarios with confidence ranges
- Counter-trend and disruption risk assessment
- Signal monitoring recommendations for ongoing tracking

## Estimated Effort
- **Single trend validation**: 1-3 hours
- **Domain trend scan (3-5 trends)**: 3-6 hours
- **Comprehensive trend landscape**: 6-12 hours
