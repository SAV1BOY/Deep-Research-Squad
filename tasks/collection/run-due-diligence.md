# Run Due Diligence

## Purpose
Execute a structured due diligence investigation to verify claims, assess risks, and build a comprehensive profile of a target entity (company, product, partnership, investment) for informed decision-making.

## When to Use
- Before making investment, acquisition, or partnership decisions
- When evaluating a vendor, supplier, or service provider
- For risk assessment of a business relationship or transaction
- When verifying representations made by a counterparty
- As part of regulatory or compliance requirements

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Due Diligence Analyst
- **Executing**: Data Researcher, OSINT Investigator, Domain Specialist
- **Supporting**: Source Hunter, Data Researcher
- **Validating**: Evidence Verifier, Contrarian Analyst

## Inputs
- Target entity and transaction context
- Due diligence scope (financial, legal, operational, technical, reputational)
- Claims or representations to verify
- Risk tolerance and deal-breaker criteria
- Timeline and reporting requirements

## Steps
1. Define the due diligence framework and checklist based on scope
2. Collect and verify basic entity information (registration, ownership, history)
3. Research financial health and performance from available public data
4. Investigate legal history (litigation, regulatory actions, compliance issues)
5. Assess operational capabilities and track record
6. Verify key claims and representations against independent sources
7. Investigate key personnel backgrounds and reputations
8. Analyze market position and competitive standing
9. Identify red flags, risks, and areas of concern
10. Assess reputational factors from media, reviews, and public sentiment
11. Compile risk assessment with severity and likelihood ratings
12. Prepare due diligence report with findings, risk matrix, and recommendations

## Quality Gates
- All checklist items are addressed or explicitly marked as not applicable
- Verified claims are supported by independent evidence
- Red flags are documented with supporting evidence and context
- Risk assessments include both severity and likelihood dimensions
- Sources include both entity-provided and independent third-party data
- Findings distinguish between confirmed facts, likely inferences, and uncertainties

## Outputs
- Due diligence report with executive summary
- Verified claims register (confirmed, unconfirmed, contradicted)
- Risk matrix with severity and likelihood ratings
- Red flag summary with supporting evidence
- Key personnel profiles
- Recommendation with confidence level

## Estimated Effort
- **Light due diligence (basic verification)**: 2-4 hours
- **Standard due diligence**: 4-10 hours
- **Comprehensive due diligence**: 10-24 hours
