# Run Technical Research

## Purpose
Execute a structured technical or technology research process to evaluate technologies, architectures, implementations, or engineering approaches with depth and rigor.

## When to Use
- When evaluating technology choices or architectural decisions
- For technology landscape mapping and comparison
- When investigating technical feasibility of an approach
- For deep technical due diligence on a product or system
- When researching emerging technologies or technical trends

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Technical Researcher
- **Executing**: Deep Researcher, Domain Analyst
- **Supporting**: Source Curator, Data Collector
- **Validating**: Evidence Validator, Contrarian Reviewer

## Inputs
- Technical question or technology to evaluate
- Evaluation criteria (performance, scalability, maturity, ecosystem, etc.)
- Context of use (environment, constraints, integration requirements)
- Comparison candidates if evaluating alternatives
- Required depth (survey, evaluation, deep analysis)

## Steps
1. Define the technical scope and evaluation framework
2. Map the technology landscape relevant to the question
3. Collect technical documentation, specifications, and benchmarks
4. Analyze architecture, design principles, and implementation approaches
5. Evaluate performance characteristics using available benchmarks and data
6. Assess maturity, stability, and production readiness
7. Analyze ecosystem health (community, tooling, support, adoption)
8. Identify technical risks, limitations, and known issues
9. Compare alternatives using the evaluation framework if applicable
10. Assess future trajectory and roadmap alignment
11. Validate findings through independent technical sources and expert opinions
12. Compile technical assessment with recommendations and trade-off analysis

## Quality Gates
- Technical claims are supported by documentation, benchmarks, or verifiable data
- Evaluation criteria are applied consistently across all candidates
- Known limitations and risks are documented alongside strengths
- Analysis distinguishes between theoretical capability and proven production use
- Sources include official documentation, independent benchmarks, and practitioner reports
- Trade-offs are clearly articulated with context-dependent guidance

## Outputs
- Technology evaluation report with scored criteria
- Architecture and design analysis
- Performance and benchmark summary
- Risk and limitation assessment
- Comparison matrix (if evaluating alternatives)
- Recommendations with trade-off analysis
- Technical landscape map

## Estimated Effort
- **Single technology evaluation**: 2-4 hours
- **Technology comparison (2-4 candidates)**: 4-8 hours
- **Comprehensive landscape analysis**: 8-16 hours
