# Run OSINT Investigation

## Purpose
Execute an open-source intelligence investigation to gather, analyze, and correlate publicly available information about entities, events, or activities using structured OSINT methodologies.

## When to Use
- When investigating a company, organization, or individual using public sources
- For due diligence that requires background verification from open sources
- When mapping networks, relationships, or organizational structures
- For verifying claims using publicly accessible evidence
- When investigating events, incidents, or activities from open data

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: OSINT Investigator
- **Executing**: Deep Researcher, Data Collector
- **Supporting**: Source Curator, Domain Analyst
- **Validating**: Evidence Validator

## Inputs
- Investigation target (entity, person, organization, event)
- Investigation scope and boundaries (what to look for, what to avoid)
- Known information and starting points
- Legal and ethical constraints for the investigation
- Required evidence standards and documentation format

## Steps
1. Define the investigation scope, objectives, and ethical boundaries
2. Catalog all known information about the target as starting points
3. Map potential open source categories (web, social media, public records, filings, media)
4. Execute systematic searches across each source category
5. Collect and preserve findings with full provenance (URL, timestamp, archive)
6. Cross-reference findings across independent sources for corroboration
7. Map relationships, connections, and networks discovered in the data
8. Build a timeline of events and activities from collected evidence
9. Identify information gaps and plan targeted follow-up searches
10. Assess the reliability and credibility of each piece of evidence
11. Synthesize findings into a coherent intelligence picture
12. Document the complete investigation trail for reproducibility and audit

## Quality Gates
- All evidence has documented provenance (source, date, access method)
- Findings are corroborated by at least two independent sources where possible
- The investigation stays within defined legal and ethical boundaries
- Assumptions and inferences are clearly distinguished from verified facts
- Information gaps are explicitly documented
- The investigation trail is reproducible by another investigator

## Outputs
- OSINT investigation report with findings and analysis
- Evidence catalog with provenance documentation
- Relationship and network maps
- Timeline of events and activities
- Source reliability assessments
- Investigation methodology documentation

## Estimated Effort
- **Focused investigation (single entity)**: 2-4 hours
- **Moderate investigation (multiple entities)**: 4-8 hours
- **Comprehensive investigation (network mapping)**: 8-20 hours
