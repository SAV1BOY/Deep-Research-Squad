# Run Market Research

## Purpose
Execute a structured market research process to analyze market size, dynamics, segments, trends, and growth drivers, providing a comprehensive understanding of a target market.

## When to Use
- When evaluating market opportunities or sizing a market
- For strategic planning that requires market context
- When entering a new market or expanding into adjacent segments
- To validate market assumptions in a business plan or investment thesis

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Market Researcher
- **Executing**: Deep Researcher, Domain Analyst
- **Supporting**: Source Curator, Data Collector
- **Validating**: Evidence Validator

## Inputs
- Market definition (industry, segment, geography, timeframe)
- Specific market questions to answer
- Known market data or estimates to validate
- Required granularity (top-level vs. segment-level analysis)
- Output format preferences (report, dashboard, brief)

## Steps
1. Define the market boundaries precisely (product category, geography, customer segment)
2. Identify and collect market size data from multiple independent sources
3. Triangulate market size estimates and document variance between sources
4. Map market segments and sub-segments with relative sizing
5. Identify and analyze key market drivers and restraints
6. Research market growth rates (historical and projected) with supporting evidence
7. Analyze market structure (concentration, fragmentation, value chain)
8. Identify major market participants and their estimated shares
9. Research customer segments, needs, and buying patterns
10. Analyze regulatory, technological, and macroeconomic factors affecting the market
11. Identify emerging trends and potential disruptions
12. Compile findings into a structured market research report

## Quality Gates
- Market size estimates are triangulated from at least three independent sources
- Growth projections include underlying assumptions and methodology
- Market segments are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive
- Driver and restraint analysis is supported by evidence, not speculation
- Data recency meets the defined threshold for the market
- Confidence levels are assigned to all quantitative estimates

## Outputs
- Market sizing analysis with confidence ranges
- Market segmentation framework
- Growth driver and restraint analysis
- Market structure and value chain map
- Trend and disruption analysis
- Customer segment profiles
- Comprehensive market research report

## Estimated Effort
- **Single segment, one geography**: 2-4 hours
- **Multi-segment or multi-geography**: 4-8 hours
- **Comprehensive market study**: 8-16 hours
