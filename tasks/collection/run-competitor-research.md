# Run Competitor Research

## Purpose
Execute a structured competitive intelligence research process to map the competitive landscape, analyze competitor strategies, capabilities, and positioning, and identify competitive advantages and threats.

## When to Use
- When a user needs to understand the competitive landscape in a market or domain
- As part of market entry analysis or strategic planning research
- When evaluating a product, service, or technology against alternatives
- For ongoing competitive monitoring and intelligence updates

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Domain Specialist
- **Executing**: Data Researcher, Insight Modeler
- **Supporting**: Source Hunter, Contrarian Analyst
- **Validating**: Evidence Verifier

## Inputs
- Target entity or product to benchmark against competitors
- Market or domain definition and boundaries
- Known competitors list (if available; discovery is part of the task if not)
- Evaluation dimensions (pricing, features, market share, strategy, etc.)
- Timeline and depth requirements

## Steps
1. Define the competitive landscape boundaries (market segment, geography, tier)
2. Identify and enumerate all relevant competitors through systematic search
3. Categorize competitors by tier (direct, indirect, potential, substitute)
4. Design the competitor evaluation framework with weighted dimensions
5. Collect data on each competitor across all evaluation dimensions
6. Analyze competitor positioning, messaging, and target audience
7. Map competitor strengths and weaknesses relative to the target entity
8. Identify competitive patterns, clusters, and white spaces in the market
9. Assess competitive dynamics (trends, movements, emerging threats)
10. Build competitor comparison matrices and visual maps
11. Validate findings through cross-referencing multiple sources per competitor
12. Compile the competitive intelligence report with actionable insights

## Quality Gates
- Competitor list is comprehensive (no significant player missed)
- Data is collected consistently across all dimensions for fair comparison
- Sources include both competitor-published and independent third-party data
- Analysis separates facts from inferences and labels confidence levels
- Findings are current (within the defined recency threshold)
- Competitive dynamics and trends are supported by evidence

## Outputs
- Competitor landscape map
- Competitor comparison matrix with scored dimensions
- Individual competitor profiles
- Competitive dynamics and trends analysis
- White space and opportunity identification
- Actionable competitive intelligence summary

## Estimated Effort
- **Small market (3-5 competitors)**: 2-4 hours
- **Medium market (6-15 competitors)**: 4-8 hours
- **Large or fragmented market (15+ competitors)**: 8-16 hours
