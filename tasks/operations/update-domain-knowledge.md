# Update Domain Knowledge

## Purpose
Capture and organize domain knowledge gained during research projects into the squad's knowledge base, building institutional expertise that accelerates and improves future research.

## When to Use
- After completing research in a domain where significant new knowledge was gained
- When domain landscape changes require knowledge base updates
- During periodic knowledge base maintenance cycles
- When onboarding agents who need domain context
- When preparing for research in a domain where prior knowledge exists

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Domain Analyst
- **Executing**: Deep Researcher, Source Curator
- **Supporting**: Research Orchestrator
- **Consulted**: All agents who gained domain knowledge during research

## Inputs
- Research findings and synthesis reports from recent projects
- Domain-specific insights and context learned during research
- Updated industry or field developments
- Expert knowledge captured from primary sources
- Existing domain knowledge base entries for comparison

## Steps
1. Extract domain-specific knowledge from recent research outputs
2. Categorize new knowledge by domain, sub-domain, and topic
3. Compare new knowledge against existing knowledge base entries
4. Update existing entries where new information supersedes or supplements
5. Create new entries for previously undocumented domain areas
6. Document domain dynamics (key actors, trends, controversies, terminology)
7. Record domain-specific methodological insights (what works, what to avoid)
8. Update domain source recommendations based on research experience
9. Capture domain mental models and frameworks useful for future research
10. Validate knowledge base updates against research evidence
11. Cross-link related knowledge entries for discoverability
12. Version and timestamp all updates for tracking knowledge evolution

## Quality Gates
- New knowledge is validated against research evidence before entry
- Updates clearly distinguish between established facts and emerging understanding
- Knowledge entries include confidence levels and evidence references
- Domain terminology is defined and used consistently
- Cross-links between related entries are comprehensive
- All updates are timestamped and versioned

## Outputs
- Updated domain knowledge base entries
- New domain area documentation
- Domain dynamics summary (actors, trends, terminology)
- Methodological insights for the domain
- Domain source recommendations
- Knowledge base update changelog

## Estimated Effort
- **Minor updates (single domain area)**: 30-60 minutes
- **Moderate updates (multiple areas)**: 1-3 hours
- **Major domain knowledge build**: 3-6 hours
