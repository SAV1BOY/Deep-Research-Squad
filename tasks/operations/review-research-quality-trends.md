# Review Research Quality Trends

## Purpose
Analyze quality metrics and patterns across multiple research projects over time to identify systemic strengths, recurring weaknesses, and improvement opportunities for the research squad.

## When to Use
- On a regular schedule (monthly or quarterly) as part of operations review
- When quality issues are recurring across multiple projects
- During squad retrospectives and planning sessions
- When establishing or revising quality standards
- When evaluating the impact of process improvements

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: DeepResearch Chief
- **Executing**: Evidence Verifier, Contrarian Analyst
- **Supporting**: Source Hunter, Domain Specialist
- **Consulted**: All squad agents (for feedback and observations)

## Inputs
- Quality audit reports from recent research projects
- Source diversity review results across projects
- Confidence level review results across projects
- Methodology review findings
- Stakeholder feedback on research quality
- Historical quality metrics for trend comparison

## Steps
1. Compile quality metrics from all recent research projects
2. Normalize metrics for comparability across different project types and scales
3. Calculate trend lines for key quality dimensions over the review period
4. Identify quality dimensions that are improving, stable, or declining
5. Analyze declining dimensions to identify root causes
6. Identify recurring quality issues that appear across multiple projects
7. Assess the impact of previously implemented improvements
8. Benchmark squad quality against external standards or comparable teams
9. Solicit qualitative feedback from all squad agents on quality challenges
10. Prioritize improvement opportunities by impact and feasibility
11. Develop specific improvement recommendations with success metrics
12. Produce the quality trend report and present to the squad

## Quality Gates
- Trend analysis covers at least three research projects for statistical meaning
- Metrics are normalized for fair comparison across project types
- Root cause analysis goes beyond symptoms to underlying factors
- Improvement recommendations are specific, measurable, and time-bound
- Agent feedback is incorporated alongside quantitative metrics
- Prior improvement initiatives are evaluated for effectiveness

## Outputs
- Quality trend report with visualized metrics
- Dimension-by-dimension trend analysis
- Recurring issue identification
- Improvement impact assessment (for prior initiatives)
- Prioritized improvement recommendations
- Quality benchmarking results
- Squad-wide quality discussion summary

## Estimated Effort
- **Quarterly review (3-5 projects)**: 2-4 hours
- **Semi-annual review (6-12 projects)**: 4-6 hours
- **Annual comprehensive review**: 6-10 hours
