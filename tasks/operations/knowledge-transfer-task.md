# Knowledge Transfer Task

## Purpose
Transfer research knowledge, methodology insights, and domain expertise gained during a project to the broader team, ensuring institutional learning is captured and reusable for future research efforts.

## When to Use
- After completing a significant research project
- When a research domain will be revisited by different agents in the future
- When novel methodologies or source strategies proved effective
- During agent onboarding to share accumulated domain knowledge
- When transitioning ongoing research between team members

## Agents Involved
- **Lead**: Research Orchestrator
- **Supporting**: Domain Analyst, Source Curator
- **Receiving**: All agents who may work on related topics in the future

## Inputs
- Completed research deliverables and supporting documentation
- Methodology documentation and lessons learned
- Source library additions and evaluation notes
- Agent performance observations and specialization insights
- Stakeholder feedback on the delivered research

## Steps
1. Compile a project retrospective covering what worked well and what did not
2. Extract reusable methodology patterns and document them as templates
3. Identify domain knowledge that should be added to the team knowledge base
4. Catalog high-value sources discovered during the project with quality ratings
5. Document dead-end approaches and why they failed to prevent repetition
6. Summarize key domain concepts and terminology for future reference
7. Record stakeholder feedback and map it to process improvement opportunities
8. Update agent specialization profiles based on demonstrated capabilities
9. Create a briefing document for agents who may continue or revisit the research
10. Schedule a knowledge-sharing session and archive all transfer materials

## Quality Gates
- Retrospective covers both successes and failures, not just highlights
- Reusable templates are generalized enough for different topics
- Source evaluations include quality ratings and access notes
- Dead-end documentation explains why the approach failed, not just that it did
- Knowledge base updates are structured and searchable
- Transfer materials are accessible to all relevant team members

## Outputs
- Project retrospective document
- Reusable methodology templates
- Domain knowledge briefing document
- Curated source additions with evaluation metadata
- Dead-end and anti-pattern log
- Updated agent specialization profiles
- Process improvement recommendations

## Estimated Effort
- **Small project, narrow domain**: 1-2 hours
- **Medium project**: 2-4 hours
- **Large multi-domain project**: 4-6 hours

## Dependencies
- Requires completed delivery via `prepare-final-delivery.md`
- Feeds into `curate-source-library.md` and `update-domain-knowledge.md`
- Outputs support `onboard-new-researcher.md` for future team members
- Stakeholder feedback informs `review-research-quality-trends.md`
