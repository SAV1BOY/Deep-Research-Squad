# Methodology Disclosure Prompts - Language Patterns

## Purpose

These language patterns help agents disclose research methodology clearly.
Methodology transparency enables the reader to assess the reliability of
findings and, if needed, reproduce the analysis.

## Describing the Approach

### Overall Methodology

- "This analysis employed a [qualitative / quantitative / mixed-methods] approach."
- "The research methodology consisted of [N] phases: [list]."
- "We used [framework/method] to analyze [subject], selected because [rationale]."
- "The approach combines [method A] for [purpose] with [method B] for [purpose]."

### Source Selection

- "Sources were selected based on [criteria: relevance, recency, credibility, tier]."
- "We consulted [N] sources across [M] source types: [breakdown]."
- "Source selection prioritized [primary / peer-reviewed / government] sources."
- "We excluded sources that [exclusion criteria: age, bias, unreliability]."

### Analytical Framework

- "Findings were analyzed using [framework name], which structures analysis by [dimensions]."
- "We applied [framework] because it is suited to [type of question]."
- "The analytical lens was [perspective], chosen because [reason]."
- "Multiple frameworks were considered. We selected [choice] because [rationale]."

## Describing Data Collection

- "Data was collected between [start date] and [end date]."
- "Primary data sources include [list]. Secondary sources include [list]."
- "We accessed [N] databases, [M] reports, and [K] expert sources."
- "Data collection was bounded by [constraints: access, time, geography]."

## Describing Analytical Steps

- "Step 1: [action]. Step 2: [action]. Step 3: [action]."
- "Each source was evaluated against our [N]-tier evidence framework."
- "Cross-referencing was performed by comparing [source types] on [dimensions]."
- "Synthesis followed the five-layer model: convergence, divergence, integration, implications, gaps."

## Describing Limitations

- "This methodology does not account for [specific limitation]."
- "The approach is limited by [constraint: data availability, time, access]."
- "Results may be affected by [bias type: selection, survivorship, confirmation]."
- "An alternative methodology ([alternative]) could address [limitation] but was not used due to [reason]."

## Reproducibility Language

- "A researcher with access to the same sources could reproduce this analysis."
- "The full source list and analytical steps are documented in [appendix / link]."
- "Key parameters: timeframe [X], geography [Y], source count [N], framework [Z]."
- "Reproducibility note: [specific aspect] depends on [access/tool] that may not be universally available."

## Methodology Comparison Phrases

- "We chose [method A] over [method B] because [trade-off reason]."
- "An alternative approach would be [method]. This would offer [benefit] but sacrifice [cost]."
- "The methodology is consistent with [standard / prior research / industry practice]."
- "This represents a [standard / novel / adapted] approach to this type of question."

## Disclosure Depth by Document Type

| Document Type    | Methodology Depth                              |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Deep dive        | Full section (1-2 pages)                       |
| Executive brief  | One sentence + link to full methodology         |
| Synthesis        | Brief per-section methodology notes             |
| Slack update     | "Based on analysis of [N] sources"              |

## Quality Check

- Can a qualified reader reproduce the analysis from this description?
- Are source selection criteria stated?
- Are analytical frameworks named and justified?
- Are limitations honestly disclosed?
- Is the methodology appropriate for the research question?
