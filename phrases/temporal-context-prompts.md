# Temporal Context Prompts - Language Patterns

## Purpose

These language patterns help agents establish and communicate temporal context
in research. Every finding exists in time. Without temporal framing, the reader
cannot judge whether a finding is current, historical, or a prediction.

## Date-Stamping Findings

### Current State

- "As of [date], the data shows [finding]."
- "Current state ([date]): [description]."
- "The most recent data, from [date], indicates [finding]."
- "Based on information available through [date]: [finding]."

### Historical Reference

- "At the time of [event/date], the situation was [description]."
- "Historically, from [start] to [end], the pattern was [pattern]."
- "Prior to [event], the prevailing condition was [condition]."
- "For context, in [year], the comparable figure was [figure]."

### Forward-Looking

- "Looking ahead to [timeframe], the expected trajectory is [trajectory]."
- "By [date/quarter], we expect [outcome], assuming [conditions hold]."
- "The forecast horizon extends to [date]. Beyond that, uncertainty increases."
- "Near-term ([0-6 months]): [expectation]. Medium-term ([6-18 months]): [expectation]."

## Change Over Time Phrases

### Describing Trends

- "Over the past [N] months/years, [metric] has [increased/decreased/stabilized] by [amount]."
- "The trend from [start] to [end] shows [direction] at a rate of [rate]."
- "This represents a [acceleration / deceleration / reversal] compared to the prior period."
- "The [N]-year trend is [direction], though the most recent [period] shows [deviation]."

### Describing Shifts

- "A notable shift occurred in [date/period] when [event/change]."
- "Before [event], the pattern was [A]. After [event], it shifted to [B]."
- "The inflection point was [date/event], after which [new trend] emerged."
- "We observe a structural change beginning around [date], driven by [cause]."

### Describing Stability

- "The metric has remained stable at [value] over the past [period]."
- "No significant change has been observed since [date]."
- "Despite [external event], the [metric/condition] has held steady."

## Temporal Caveats

- "This finding is time-sensitive and should be reviewed by [date]."
- "Conditions may have changed since data collection ended on [date]."
- "The [event/development] on [date] occurred after our analysis cutoff."
- "This assessment assumes no major disruptions between now and [forecast date]."

## Temporal Comparison Phrases

- "Compared to [same period last year / previous quarter / pre-event baseline]: [comparison]."
- "Year-over-year, [metric] moved from [A] to [B], a change of [percentage]."
- "The current figure of [X] compares to a [N]-year average of [Y]."
- "Relative to the [pre-pandemic / pre-regulation / pre-launch] baseline: [comparison]."

## Sequencing Events

- "The sequence of events was: (1) [event], (2) [event], (3) [event]."
- "[Event A] preceded [Event B] by [duration]."
- "These events occurred concurrently, suggesting [possible connection]."
- "The lag between [cause] and [effect] was approximately [duration]."

## Temporal Formatting Standards

| Element             | Format                    | Example              |
|---------------------|---------------------------|----------------------|
| Specific date       | YYYY-MM-DD                | 2026-03-13           |
| Month               | Month YYYY                | March 2026           |
| Quarter             | QN YYYY                   | Q1 2026              |
| Fiscal year         | FY[YY]                    | FY26                 |
| Relative time       | [N] [units] ago           | 6 months ago         |
| Date range          | [Start] to [End]          | Jan 2025 to Mar 2026 |

## Quality Check

- Is every finding date-stamped?
- Are trends described with start/end dates and rates of change?
- Are temporal caveats included for time-sensitive findings?
- Is the forecast horizon clearly bounded?
- Are comparisons anchored to specific baseline periods?
