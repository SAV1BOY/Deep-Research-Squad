# Uncertainty Language - Phrase Library

## Purpose

This phrase library provides ready-to-use language for communicating
uncertainty at different levels. Use these phrases consistently across
all deliverables to maintain calibrated communication.

## Certainty Spectrum Phrases

### High Certainty (We Know)

- "The evidence confirms that..."
- "This is well-established: ..."
- "We are confident that..."
- "Multiple independent sources verify that..."
- "The data unambiguously shows..."

### Strong Probability

- "The evidence strongly indicates..."
- "It is highly likely that..."
- "With high confidence, we assess that..."
- "The weight of evidence points to..."
- "All indicators suggest..."

### Moderate Probability

- "The balance of evidence suggests..."
- "It is more likely than not that..."
- "Our best assessment is that..."
- "The evidence leans toward..."
- "A reasonable reading of the data is..."

### Low Probability / Possible

- "There are preliminary indications that..."
- "One possibility is..."
- "It cannot be ruled out that..."
- "Limited evidence suggests..."
- "If early signals are confirmed, then..."

### Speculative

- "Extrapolating beyond the available data..."
- "A speculative but plausible scenario is..."
- "If we assume [assumption], then [consequence]."
- "This is conjecture informed by [limited basis]."
- "We have no direct evidence, but the logic suggests..."

### Unknown

- "We do not have evidence on this point."
- "This remains an open question."
- "The available data does not address this."
- "We cannot assess this with current information."
- "Further investigation is required before any assessment."

## Uncertainty Source Phrases

### Data Limitations

- "Uncertainty arises from the limited sample size of [N]."
- "The most recent data is from [date], and conditions may have shifted."
- "No primary data is available; our assessment relies on secondary sources."

### Methodological Limitations

- "Our methodology does not capture [specific dimension]."
- "The analytical framework assumes [assumption], which may not hold."
- "Different analytical approaches could yield different conclusions."

### Structural Uncertainty

- "The outcome depends on [external variable] that is inherently unpredictable."
- "Multiple plausible scenarios exist, and the determining factor is [factor]."
- "This is a novel situation with limited historical precedent."

## Quantifying Uncertainty

- "We estimate [range] with [confidence level]."
- "The likely range is [low] to [high], with a central estimate of [mid]."
- "In [N] of [M] comparable situations, the outcome was [outcome]."
- "The margin of error on this estimate is approximately [percentage]."

## Combining Uncertainty with Authority

- "While uncertainty exists on timing, the direction is clear: [direction]."
- "We are uncertain about magnitude but confident about the trend."
- "The specific outcome is unpredictable, but the range of outcomes is bounded."
- "We state this with appropriate caution, not because the evidence is weak, but because the situation is genuinely complex."

## Quality Check

- Is the uncertainty level proportionate to the evidence?
- Is the source of uncertainty identified?
- Does the language maintain authority while being honest?
- Is the phrase calibrated to the correct level on the spectrum?
